## 4.1 总纲：主持人是唯一不可缺的角色

> **原文（p83）**：
> 「The role of the test moderator or test administrator is **the most critical of all the test team members** ... In fact, the moderator is the one team member that **you absolutely must have** in order to conduct the test.」

> **原文（p83）**：
> 「If the usability test were an athletic contest, the moderator would be the captain of the team. As such, he or she has the potential to make or break the test. **An ineffective moderator can seriously negate test results and even waste much of the preliminary preparation work.**」

> **★★★ 判据：主持人失败 = 全部前期投入归零**
> 这句话是资源配置依据：预算紧张时，**优先保证主持人的质量**，而不是加被试数量或升级设备。

## 4.2 谁来做：五类候选人，以及"自测"问题

### 4.2.1 自测的客观性困境

> **原文（p83）**：
> 「One of the basic tenets of usability testing — and of this book — is that **it is almost impossible to remain objective when conducting a usability test of your own product**.」

> **原文（p83–p84，跨页）**：
> 「There is simply too strong a tendency to lead participants in a direction that you want the results to go, rather than acting as a **neutral enabler**」
> 「of the process.」
> （原文此句跨 p83/p84 页界，中间夹页眉，故分行照录。）

> **原文（p84）**：
> 「In fact, **asking someone to test his or her own product is like asking parents to objectively evaluate the abilities of their child**. It is an impossible endeavor.」

> **原文（p84）**：
> 「Having said that, **if there is only you available to test your product, do so**. In almost every case, it is still better to test than not to test, even if you must do the testing yourself. However, for the long term, you would want to be out of the self-testing business as soon as possible.」

> **★★★★ 判据：自测是"可接受的次优解"，不是"可接受的常态"**
> 两条一起读才完整：**不能因为无法客观就不测**（更好的做法是自己测），**也不能因为自己测了就认为够**（要尽快脱离）。
> 落地：自己主持时必须**额外加强**——按脚本逐字念（见 ch06 §6.3）、事后看录像复盘、请他人旁观复核。

### 4.2.2 五类候选人

| # | 来源 | 原文理由（摘录） | 客观性 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Human Factors Specialist** | 「typically has an advanced degree in psychology, industrial engineering, or similar discipline, and is familiar with experimental methodology and test rigor」（p84） | 高（若未参与设计） |
| 2 | **Marketing Specialist** | 「typically customer-oriented, user-oriented, or both, has good interpersonal and communication skills」（p84） | 中高 |
| 3 | **Technical Communicator** | 「often make wonderful test moderators. Many technical communicators already serve as user advocates」（p85） | 中高 |
| 4 | **Rotating Team Members** | 「draw upon colleagues of similar disciplines, **who are not working on the same product**」（p85） | 高（不同产品） |
| 5 | **External Consultant** | 「Using an external consulting company **guarantees the objectivity** that testing requires.」（p85） | 最高 |

> **原文（p84）**：
> 「This grounding is crucial in **differentiating the important from the superficial** usability factors in a product and ultimately in designing and conducting the test.」

> **★★ 判据：主持人的专业背景不是为了"操作设备"，而是为了"当场判断什么值得追问"**
> 这是人因/心理学背景被排在第一位的真正原因：**即时的相关性判断**（见 §4.3.6「Big Picture Thinker」）。

### 4.2.3 外部顾问的一条硬约束

> **原文（p86）**：
> 「Just make sure that if you conduct the test off-site, **its location is physically close enough to allow development team members to attend the test sessions**. Do not simply farm out the test to a remote location.」

> **原文（p86）**：
> 「**Viewing tests in person is much more effective than watching or listening to a recording**, especially for those who are skeptical about the value of testing.」

> **★★★ 判据：外包测试时，"离团队多远"是设计约束，不是后勤细节**
> 团队到不了现场 → 报告说服力下降 → 建议不被采纳 → 测试的商业价值归零。
> 落地：选外包供应商时，**距离与到场便利性**应列为评分项。

## 4.3 好主持人的十项特质

原文以「Characteristics of a Good Test Moderator」为题的十项（p86–p92），逐项给原文关键词与实操含义。

### 4.3.1 Grounding in the Basics of User-Centered Design（p86）

> **原文（p86）**：
> 「it enables the test moderator to **sense, even before the test begins, which interactions, operations, messages, or instructions are liable to cause problems**.」

> **★★ 判据：好的主持人"开测前就知道哪里会坏"**
> 这直接决定观察的注意力分配——不是平均地看，而是**有预判地盯**。

### 4.3.2 Quick Learner（p86–p87）

> **原文（p87）**：
> 「An effective test moderator **need not be expert in the intricacies of the specific product being tested**. ... However, he or she must be able to **absorb new concepts quickly**」

### 4.3.3 Instant Rapport（p87）

> **原文（p87）**：
> 「**If you are able to test only five participants, one uneasy participant represents a potential loss of 20 percent of your test data.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：小样本下，单个被试的状态就是 20% 的数据质量**
> 这条把小样本的脆弱性量化了：5 人样本里，一个紧张到行为失常的被试，**损失不是 1/5 的人是"废的"，而是整体结论的可信度**。
> 落地：开场破冰、茶点、闲谈不是软性环节，是**数据保真措施**。

### 4.3.4 Excellent Memory（p87）

> **原文（p87）**：
> 「Because a test session can be rather long, the test moderator needs to **remember behaviors or comments that took place earlier in the session in order to cross-check and probe those behaviors later** in the session.」

> **★★ 判据：主持人要在同场次内做"纵向对照"**
> 例：同一任务被试换了 2–3 种做法 → 必须在会话结束前回去追问"为什么每次都不同"。
> **这解释了为什么记录员不能替代主持人：记录员记"发生了什么"，主持人记"与前面哪一次矛盾"。**

### 4.3.5 Good Listener（p87–p88）

> **原文（p88）**：
> 「Listening skills involve the test moderator's ability to **hear with "new ears"** during each session and to **lay aside personal biases and strong opinions**」

> **原文（p88）**：
> 「The test moderator must pick up on **the subtle nuances of speech and emphasis**, as a participant's comments are often **indirect and less than forthcoming**.」

### 4.3.6 Comfortable with Ambiguity（p88）

> **原文（p88）**：
> 「Usability is **not a precise science** consisting of formulas and black and white answers. Even if a usability test is conducted under the most rigorous conditions, which is atypical, you are still **not assured that all of the results are valid and generalizable** to your entire user population.」

> **原文（p88）**：
> 「**Without tolerance for ambiguity and the patience to persevere, the test moderator tends to rationalize and to blame the participants** for making unplanned choices during the test.」

> **★★★ 判据：结果含糊时，归咎被试是主持人失格的第一征兆**
> 这是可自检的红旗：如果你发现自己说"这个被试不典型/不配合"，先怀疑测试设计或自己的主持。

### 4.3.7 Flexibility（p88–p89）

> **原文（p88）**：
> 「Jeff conducted a test when **the entire high-level design of an interface became questionable after testing only two participants**. He could see immediately that the premise for the design was flawed. At that time, he **recommended that the company halt testing and go back to the drawing board**.」

> **★★★★ 判据：允许"测到一半就停"**
> 极端但明确：**当发现前提错误时，继续测细节是浪费所有人的时间**。
> 落地：测试计划里应有"中止/改道判据"，而不是只有"完成判据"（对照 ch07 §7.6「When to Deviate」）。

### 4.3.8 Long Attention Span（p89）

> **原文（p89）**：
> 「Experienced test moderators share a secret: **Usability testing can be tedious and boring.** ... The moderator cannot possess the type of personality that needs new stimulation every 5 to 10 minutes.」

> **原文（p89）**：
> 「because the moderator may view up to **10, 15, or 20 sessions**, all of which involve observing the same or similar tasks」

### 4.3.9 Empathic "People Person"（p89）

> **原文（p89）**：
> 「**Participants will tend to hold back if they feel that the test moderator cannot relate to their particular situation**, this being especially true if the session was unusually frustrating or difficult.」

### 4.3.10 "Big Picture" Thinker（p89）

> **原文（p89）**：
> 「The test moderator must be able to **weed out the significant from the insignificant**, and this ability takes two concrete forms.」
> - 「The ability to draw together all of the various inputs, comments, and data from a single test to form **a cohesive picture of a participant's performance**.」
> - 「The ability to draw together the varied inputs from different test sessions and focus on **the most important and critical findings**.」

> **★★★ 判据：十项特质里，只有这一项是"跨场次"能力**
> 其余九项都在单场次内生效；第 10 项决定报告质量——**能不能把 8 个被试的碎片拼成 3 个核心结论**。

## 4.4 测中形态：Sit-By 与"在隔壁看"

这是本章与"实验室 vs 远程"分类相关的部分。

> **原文（p91–p92，跨页）**：
> 「When the practice of usability testing started, most sessions were conducted in a **highly clinical style, with the moderator seated in a control room** to observe and collect data. Over the years, a less formal, "**sit-by**" style of conducting」
> 「sessions has developed」
> （此句跨 p91/p92 页界，故分行照录。）

### 4.4.1 何时必须 sit-by

> **原文（p92）**：
> 「being with the participant in the testing room is advantageous for **exploratory situations** in which one wants to be able to gather the first impressions of participants about the design or functionality of a product.」

> **原文（p92）**：
> 「if there is very quick interaction that must be observed closely or if you are conducting **paper prototyping** sessions, the moderator **must** be with the participant to mimic the computer actions.」

### 4.4.2 何时必须离开房间

> **原文（p92）**：
> 「if there is a chance in an assessment or validation test that **the moderator's presence may bias the data**, or if the emphasis is on **gathering quantitative rather than qualitative data**, set the test up to let the participant work by him- or herself while you manage the sessions from another room.」

> **★★★★ 判据：形态由"偏定性还是偏定量"决定，不由"有没有实验室"决定**
> - 定性（探索、出声思维、追问）→ **sit-by**
> - 定量（计时、成功率、基准比对）→ **主试离场**
> **理由**：主试在场会系统性拉长任务时长、提高努力程度（Barnum p246 有实测：主试在场时点击次数与耗时都是无主试条件的 2 倍）。

### 4.4.3 四种房间布局（原书 ch06 的形态谱系）

原文在 ch06（p139–p148）给了从最简到最全的四种办公室布局，属于"实验室 vs 非实验室"维度的具体落法：

| 布局 | 原文名称 | 主试位置 | 观察者容量 | 原文对它的态度 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 简式单间 | Simple Single-Room | 与主试「at about a 45-degree angle」、几英尺外 | 很少 | 「the most basic type」 |
| 改良单间 | Modified Single-Room | 被试视线外的工作台 | 受房间限制 | 「This is not our favorite setup」 |
| 大单间 | Large Single-Room | 被试旁，双方戴麦，投影 | 「5 to 30」 | 适合大团队围观 |
| 电子观察室 | Electronic Observation Room | 隔壁房间看监视器 | 若干 | 可自由进出、可互相讨论 |
| 经典实验室 | Classic Testing Laboratory | 单向镜后的控制室 | 「typically 4 to 12」 | 「requires a large capital outlay」 |

> **原文（p147–p148，跨页）**：
> 「It allows unparalleled **unobtrusive data collection**. Because the test moderator is not in the room, this eliminates almost entirely any」
> 「possible biasing effects due to inadvertent nonverbal communication or mannerisms.」
> （此句跨 p147/p148 页界，故分行照录。）

> **原文（p148）**：
> 「(This effect can be exacerbated by the type of intercom system used, some of which make the test moderator's instructions sound like the "**voice of God**.")」

> **★★ 判据：经典实验室的最大风险是"豚鼠综合征"，最大收益是"无干扰观察"**
> 原文把风险命名为「the "guinea pig" syndrome」（p148）。**取舍点**：被试的技术自信度。原文建议对「less sophisticated」的被试，主试应留在房间里。

> **原文（p148–p149）**：
> 「The setup in the section "Minimalist Portable Lab Setup" is the setup **we recommend for organizations just beginning to test** and for those with limited resources.」

> **★★★ 判据：新手从"便携极简实验室"起步，不是从实验室起步**
> 原文给的推荐序：极简便携 → （程序化后）→ 专用实验室。

## 4.5 典型主持失误（六种，原文 p95–p97）

| # | 失误（原文标题） | 原文机理（摘录） | 判据 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Leading Rather Than Enabling** | 「being too close to the product and unintentionally providing cues ... **tone of voice, a nod of the head, cutting short tasks**」（p95） | 只采集，不评价；环境保持中性 |
| 2 | **Too Involved with the Act of Data Collection** | 「the act of collecting that information should not interfere with direct observation」（p95） | 记录让位于观察；用记录员/编码 |
| 3 | **Acting Too Knowledgeable** | 「Participants will tend to defer to the test moderator and ask many questions」（p95） | 装傻（「play dumb」），扮演「a research technician」 |
| 4 | **Too Rigid with the Test Plan** | 「the moderator to sense when the test design is not achieving its objectives」（p96） | 允许偏离，但要留痕 |
| 5 | **Not Relating Well to Each Participant** | 「the participant is always right」（p96） | 调整风格适配被试，不评判 |
| 6 | **Jumping to Conclusions** | 「Inexperienced test moderators tend to overreact to early results」（p96） | 早期模式可记，不可据此改产品 |

> **原文（p95）**：
> 「One simple way to counteract the problem of acting too knowledgeable is for the test moderator to "**play dumb**." That is, the test moderator downplays any knowledge of the product and takes on the role of **a research technician who is simply collecting data**.」

> **★★★ 判据："装傻"是技术，不是态度**
> 它的机制是**改变被试的求助预期**：一旦被试确信问不出答案，就会转向自己摸索——而这正是你要观察的行为。
> 与 ch05 §5.5「不许说的话」互为表里：装傻的失败表现就是说了不该说的话。

## 4.6 如何变强（原文 p97–p99 的六条）

| # | 途径（原文） | 原文要点 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 「Learn the Basic Principles of Human Factors/Ergonomics」 | 「human information processing, experimental psychology, cognitive psychology, statistics, interface design, and usability engineering」（p97） |
| 2 | 「Learn from Watching Others」 | 「ask the test moderator why he or she uses a particular technique」（p97） |
| 3 | 「Watch Yourself on Tape」 | 「Take notes on what you do well and on behaviors that need improvement」（p97） |
| 4 | 「Work with a Mentor」 | 「Have your mentor watch you and critique your performance」（p98） |
| 5 | 「Practice Moderating Sessions」 | 「**Do not be a perfectionist.** You are going to make mistakes, bias participants, reveal information you should not」（p98） |
| 6 | 「Learn to Meditate」／「Practice "Bare Attention"」 | 「observe the participant's behavior **free from the tyranny of his or her own expectations and biases**」（p98）；「bare attention」每次 15–30 分钟（p99） |

> **原文（p98）**：
> 「Usability testing has a twofold saving grace — **testing multiple participants and iterative design**. Testing multiple participants means that if you invalidate something in one session, there is always another opportunity to do it right.」

> **★★★ 判据：主持失误有"两次容错"——多人 + 多轮**
> 这是新手可以开始的底气来源。**但两次容错都有前提：多个被试（不是 1 个）、多轮测试（不是 1 轮）。** 只有 1 人 1 轮时，失误不可挽回。
