## 2.0 本章解决什么：材料信息为什么要"分维度"

> **原文（p70）**：
> 「**Figure 4.1 illustrates the steps involved in moving a material from the laboratory into a successful product. Tests yield raw data. These are distilled, via appropriate statistical analyses, into data for "material properties": values for properties on which design can safely be based (typically, 3 standard deviations below the mean).** A material may have attractive "material properties" but to make it into a product requires that it can be shaped, joined, and finished.」

**图 4.1 Confidence in New Materials**
The steps in moving a material from the laboratory to a successful product.

★★★★ **判据：材性数据是"从原始测试数据经统计蒸馏"得来的，且是均值下 3 个标准差。**
> 这条直接决定了 CMF 的**数据纪律**：
> **① 你在手册上看到的每一个数值都是"保守值"（3σ 下界），不是典型值；**
> **② 手册给不了 CMF 要的东西——因为手册的蒸馏目标是"安全承载"，不是"好看/好摸"。**

> **原文（p70）**：
> 「Once made, the product will be used – the choice of material is influenced by the nature of the user: children, perhaps, or travelers, or the elderly. Does it comply with legislative requirements that the product must meet (FDA approval, perhaps, or requirements limiting flammability, noise, vibration, or bio-compatibility)? Would it survive the use, and misuse it will encounter in service? Is it toxic? **Information for this – the dimension of use – is as important in guiding selection.**」

> **原文（p70–p71）**：
> 「There is more. Manufacturers and their customers are searching for solutions that are sustainable and green. … **This, the environmental dimension, requires yet another layer of materials information, one relating to product manufacture, use, and disposal.** And even that is not the end.」

> **原文（p71）**：
> 「**The industrial design of a product is as much a part of its creation as any other. The characteristics of a material that contributes to industrial design, though harder to document, are as important as the more technical properties.** This – creating emotional connection and doing good design – requires a **fourth dimension** of materials information: that relating to **aesthetics and personality**.」

> **原文（p71）**：
> 「**Given these five attributes, rational and emotional design with materials becomes possible.** … Here we explore **the five dimensions of materials information: engineering, product use, the environment, aesthetics, and emotion.**」

★★★★★ **全书级判据：材料信息有五个维度，且"第五个"才是情感。**
> 原文的措辞极精确：美学是**第四个**维度，情感是**第五个**（"fourth dimension … that relating to aesthetics and personality"，随后总结为 five: engineering, product use, environment, aesthetics, **emotion**）。
> **判据：`美学属性` 与 `情感属性` 不是一回事** —— 美学是"感官接收到什么"，情感是"它让你感觉如何"（第 5 章 p45 给出了四分法）。

## 2.1 维度一：技术属性（Engineering Dimension）

> **原文（p71）**：
> 「The scientific study of materials – materials science – seeks to understand the fundamental origins of material properties, and, ultimately, to manipulate them. … **The origins of many material properties derive directly from the atomic and electronic structure of the material: among these are density, stiffness, thermal and electrical conductivity, optical transparency, and many others.**」

> **原文（p71）**：
> 「The classification of 4.2 – **Family, Class, Member** – is based, at the first level, on the nature of the atoms of the material and that of the bonding between them (e.g. "polymer"), at the second level on its variants (e.g. "thermoplastic"), and at subsequent levels, the details of its composition. **Each member has a set of attributes that quantify its physical, mechanical, thermal, electrical, and optical behavior – what we will call its technical profile.**」

**图 4.2 Classification of Materials**
A classification of materials based on a scientific understanding of the nature of the atoms they contain and the bonds between these atoms. The final column shows a list of possible attributes for a specific material.

> **★★★ 判据：材料的分类学是 `Family → Class → Member` 三级。**
> **CMF 的动作点永远在 Member 层**（不是"金属"，是"6063 铝合金阳极氧化 15μm 哑光"）。
> 原文的"家庭/类/成员"对应 CMF 语境就是：**大类 → 子类 → 具体牌号+表面状态**。

> **原文（p72，图 4.3 的技术剖面）**：
> 「Open a handbook or search the web, seeking a material by name, and you will find technical data. **This information is largely numeric: values for modulus, strength, toughness, hardness, thermal conductivity and expansion coefficient, electrical resistance, and so forth (4.3), backed up with a little text-based information on corrosion resistance and wear.** These are the data needed for technical design – for the calculation of safe loads, temperatures, heat fluxes, and operating life.」

**图 4.3 Technical Profiles** —— Selected technical attributes for **polypropylene**：

|---|---|---|
| Physical | Density, kg/m³ | 900–910 |
| Mechanical | El. modulus, GPa | 1.14–1.55 |
| | Yld. strength, MPa | 31–35 |
| | Tensile strength, MPa | 33–36 |
| | Comp. strength, MPa | 37–45 |
| | Elongation, % | 100–350 |
| | Toughness, kJ/m³ | 10–11 |
| | Fatigue limit, MPa | 11–15 |
| | Hardness, Vickers | 9.2–11 |
| Thermal | Max use temp., °C | 90–105 |
| | Th. conductivity, W/m·°C | 0.11–0.12 |
| | Th. expansion, /°C·10⁻⁶ | 145–180 |

| Electrical | Dielectric constant | 2.2–2.3 |

| | Resistivity, ohm·cm | 3·10²²–3·10²³ |

> **★★ 判据：一张标准技术剖面里，与 CMF 直接相关的只有三项** ——
> **Hardness（触感硬/耐磨）、Th. conductivity（冷/暖感）、Max use temp.（能不能做外壳）**。
> **其余 13 项对 CMF 决策基本无用。**这是"技术剖面不能直接当 CMF 档案"的量化证据。

## 2.2 维度二：使用属性（Usability / Ergonomics）

> **原文（p77）**：
> 「The design of a product's interface has **three broad aspects**: the first concerns the **matching of the product to the capabilities of the human body**; the second, **matching to the reasoning power of the human mind**; and the last, **matching with the surroundings in which the human lives and works**. Collectively, these are known as human factors and their study is called ergonomics, interface design, or human-factor engineering.」

> **原文（p77）**：
> 「Figure 4.6 lists some of these for European and North American populations. Physically, people vary in size and strength. **As a yardstick, designs often use ranges that bracket 90% of the mature population of working age**, and it is this range that appears in these tables. **They define what might be called the Standard Design Person.**」

**图 4.6 Human Measurements** —— Average attributes of European and North American men and women of working age：

| Characteristic | Man (18–40) | Woman (18–40) |
|---|---|---|
| Standing Height, m | 1.63–1.85 | 1.54–1.76 |
| Seated Height above seat, m | 0.84–0.96 | 0.79–0.91 |
| Seat Width, m | 0.33–0.40 | 0.35–0.43 |
| Forward Reach, m | 0.77–0.92 | 0.60–0.75 |
| Shoulder Width, m | 0.42–0.50 | 0.38–0.46 |
| Eye Height, Standing, m | 1.52–1.74 | 1.44–1.65 |
| Body Mass, kg | 60–94 | 44–80 |
| Head Mass, kg | 1.4–1.7 | 1.25–1.6 |

> **原文（p77–p78，标准人是个笑话）**：
> 「But there is a story, relevant at this point, of the man who invented a shaving machine. You clamped it to your face, pressed the button, and – hey presto – you were shaved. When asked whose face it fitted he said **it fitted the standard face**. But what happened if someone who used the machine did not have a standard face? After they've used my machine, he said, **they will.**」
> 「So the idea of a standard person is a little ridiculous. **For true usability, equipment must be matched to the capabilities of the individual (4.7), not to some average.**」

★★★★ **判据：「标准人」是个笑话，但它仍然是设计的默认基线。**
> **CMF 的相关动作：手部接触面（握把、按键、铰链）的材料与表面必须按"最小手"（女性 5 百分位）而不是均值来定。**

> **原文（p78，这一句是 CMF 与人体工学的接口）**：
> 「**Materials have a role to play here.** Heavy objects, often a source of physical strain when lifted or moved, can be made lighter by the use of light metal alloys, polymers, and foam-cored sandwich structures. **Elastomers can provide grip where it is needed, polymer gels and foams allow soft tactile surfaces, and woven and non-woven fabrics conform to the body shape.** Molded shapes that fit well in the hand and have smooth rounded corners that do not catch on clothing reduce the risk of accidents. Differences in size can be accommodated by using materials that allow adjustability – **Velcro, demountable adhesives, elastomers**.」

★★★★★ **判据：材料在人体工学里承担四个具体功能** ——
**① 减重**（轻合金 / 聚合物 / 泡沫夹芯）
**② 增摩**（弹性体提供 grip）
**③ 软化**（聚合物凝胶与泡沫提供 soft tactile surfaces）
**④ 可调**（Velcro / 可拆胶 / 弹性体）
> **判据：这四条就是 CMF 中 F（Finish）的第一批硬约束** ——
> **手握区域的表面处理不是为了好看，是为了"增摩"与"软化"这两个功能指标。**

## 2.3 维度三：环境属性（Environmental Dimension）

> **原文（p82）**：
> 「What have you discarded lately that still worked or, if it didn't, could have been fixed? Changing trends, pushed by seductive advertising, reinforce the desire for the new and urge the replacement of still-useful objects. **Industrial design carries a heavy responsibility here – it has, at certain periods, been directed toward creative obsolescence: designing products that are desirable only if new**, and urging the consumer to buy the latest models, using marketing techniques that imply that acquiring them is a social and psychological necessity.」

> **原文（p83）**：
> 「But that is only half the picture. A well-designed product can outlive its design life by centuries, and – far from becoming unwanted – can acquire value with age. **People do not throw away products for which they feel emotional attachment. So there you have it: industrial design both as villain and as hero.**」

★★★★ **判据：工业设计同时是环境问题的"反派"与"英雄"。**
> 反派：**有计划地制造过时**（color/finish 一年一换，让去年的款显得旧）。
> 英雄：**情感依附延长寿命**（"人们不会扔掉他们有情感依附的产品"）。
> **CMF 直接站在这个矛盾的刀口上：换色换表面是最便宜的"创新"，也是最快的"淘汰"。**

> **原文（p83，材料会枯竭吗）**：
> 「A market economy may have many faults, but it also contains feedback mechanisms of a restraining nature. **As the natural resources of materials are consumed and ore-grades diminish, prices rise; the lower-grade uses become economically untenable and consumption falls.** A more balanced view is that **we will never "run out" of anything, but that the adjustments imposed by rising cost may be very uncomfortable to live with.**」

> **★★★ 判据：稀缺不是"用完了"，是"变贵了直到用不起"。**
> 对 CMF 的含义：**材料替代的压力来自价格曲线，不来自储量告罄**。
> **所以 CMF 的材料决策要盯"价格趋势"，不是盯"储量报告"。**

## 2.4 维度四/五：美学属性与情感属性（Aesthetics & Emotion）

> **原文（p71，再引一次，因这是本章的分水岭）**：
> 「The characteristics of a material that contributes to industrial design, **though harder to document**, are **as important as** the more technical properties.」

> **原文（p44，四个术语的递进定义 —— 全书最锋利的一段定义）**：
> 「perception is the result of interpreting what is observed. … here it is necessary that we sharpen the definition of four terms we will use to describe, **in increasing order of abstraction**, the attributes of products …」
> 「• **Aesthetic attributes** are those that relate directly to the senses: sight, touch, taste, smell, hearing; those of sight include the **form, color, and texture** of a material or product.」
> 「• **Attributes of association** are those that make a connection to a **time, place, event, person, or culture** – thus a jeep has military associations, gold has associations of wealth, **the color black of death, the color red of weddings**.」
> 「• **Perceived attributes** describe a **reaction** to a material or product – that it is **sophisticated, or modern, or humorous**, for instance; these reactions are a sum of what you initially perceive and are heavily influenced by context and experience.」
> 「• **Emotional attributes** describe **how a material or product makes you feel** – happy, sad, threatened perhaps – **"emotional ergonomics," in the words of Richard Seymour of SeymourPowell, London.**」

★★★★★ **全书级判据：属性按"抽象度递增"分四层 —— 美学 → 联想 → 感知 → 情感。**
| 层级 | 定义 | CMF 的操作面 | 对应 C/M/F |
|---|---|---|---|
| **① 美学属性** | 直接作用于感官（视觉含形态/颜色/纹理） | 直接可控 | **C + F（主）**，M（次） |
| **② 联想属性** | 连接到时间/地点/事件/人/文化 | 靠材料与工艺的历史沉积 | **M + F** |
| **③ 感知属性** | 对对象产生的"反应"（精致/现代/幽默） | 不可直接设定，只能"设计条件" | 三者组合 |
| **④ 情感属性** | 它让你感觉如何（快乐/悲伤/受威胁） | 只能测量，不能承诺 | 三者组合 |

> **★★★ 判据：CMF 能"定"的只有第 ① 层；第 ②③④ 层只能"押注 + 验证"。**
> **这解释了为什么 CMF 提案必须配样件与用户测试 —— 因为它卖的其实是第 ②③④ 层。**

## 2.5 五个维度 → 三维流程的映射（本章落点）

> 下表是本技能全部章节的调度图。**每一行就是一次完整的 CMF 决策必须过的关口。**

| 维度（原文） | 原文数据形态 | CMF 三层归属 | 本技能章节 | 判据形态 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Engineering（技术）** | E, ρ, σ, K_IC, H, λ, α, 价格 | **M（硬约束）** | ch04 | 阈值 / 材料指数 |
| **Product use（使用）** | 人体尺寸、握持力、噪声 dB、振动 m/s² | **C + F（约束）** | ch02 §2.2、ch06 | 尺寸区间 / 表面摩擦 |
| **Environment（环境）** | 含能、碳足迹、回收等级、法规 | **M + F（否决项）** | ch07 §7.5 | 一票否决 |
| **Aesthetics（美学）** | 视觉/触觉/听觉/嗅觉属性量表 | **C + F（主战场）** | ch03、ch05 | 属性轴坐标 |
| **Emotion（情感）** | 感知词对（4.26）、情绪反应 | **全层（验证项）** | ch05、ch07 | 词对打分 |

> **★★★ 判据：CMF 的三层不是"三个部门"，是"五个维度的三种投影方式"。**
> **同一个材料在 M 层表现为"能不能用"，在 C 层表现为"能不能是那个颜色"，在 F 层表现为"能不能是那个表面"。**
> **三层各自通过，五维全部满足，才叫 CMF 定了。**

### 2.5.1 图 4.13（Mirror or Matte）—— 全书唯一一张"表面等级量化表"

> **原文（p82）**：
> 「The reflectivity of a surface measures **the fraction of the light intensity, incident at an angle of 60° to the surface, that is reflected back to an observer at 120°** (that is, an equal angle on the other side). **A black matte surface reflects less than 1%; a high gloss surface reflects 80% or more; a mirror reflects it all.** Reflectivities are listed in 4.13.」
> 「**The reflectivity of a surface depends on the material of which it is made or coated, and the smoothness of this surface: both color and texture are important.** It is controlled by the use of low reflectivity coatings and light absorbing screens.」

**图 4.13 Mirror or Matte?**（Reflectivity of materials and surfaces）—— 两组数据：

| 材料 | 反射率 % | | 表面纹理 | 反射率 % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aluminum | 60 | | Dead matte | <1 |
| Beryllium | 49 | | Matte | 1−10 |
| Carbon steel | 57 | | Eggshell | 15−20 |
| Invar | 41 | | Semi-gloss | 40−50 |
| Magnesium | 72 | | Full gloss | >80 |
| Stainless steel | 70 | | Mirror | >95 |
| Titanium | 56 | | | |

★★★★★ **判据：光泽有官方六级，且带数值区间。**（这是本技能最硬的一条 F 层判据）
> **Dead matte <1% ｜ Matte 1–10% ｜ Eggshell 15–20% ｜ Semi-gloss 40–50% ｜ Full gloss >80% ｜ Mirror >95%**
> 要点三条：
> **① 反射率的测量条件是 60° 入射 / 120° 观测**（不是垂直入射，不要和镜面反射率混用）；
> **② 表面光泽由"材料本身 + 表面光滑度"共同决定，且颜色与纹理都参与**；
> **③ 同一种材料能跨越多级**（不锈钢既可是 Mirror >95%，也可是 Matte 1–10%），**这就是 F 层的自由度所在**。
