## 6.0 F 层的第一原则：几乎每个零件都要做表面处理

> **原文（p358，Surface Profiles 开篇 —— 全章总纲）**：
> 「**Almost every component of a product has some sort of surface process applied to it. Surface processes enhance the thermal, fatigue, friction, wear, corrosion, or aesthetic qualities of the surface, leaving the bulk properties unchanged.** Economically they are of great importance, **extending life, allowing materials to be used in increasingly harsh service conditions, imparting multi-functionality (for example – by creating a corrosion resistant surface on a tough, but chemically reactive substrate, or a thermally insulating surface on a strong, but metallic substrate) and, of course, creating much of the visual and tactile features of a product.**」

> **原文（p358，选择的两个依据）**：
> 「**The choice of a surface process depends on the material to which it will be applied and the function it is to perform.** The processes used to etch the surface of glass differ in obvious ways from those used to texture polymers; less obviously, paints and electro-coatings, too, are often material-specific. **The pages that follow profile ways of treating surfaces, describe the processes and their functions, and list the materials to which they can be applied. They cover the most common processes used to create the visual and tactile qualities of products.**」

★★★★★ **判据：表面工艺的"本体不变、表面全变"是 F 层的核心机制。**
> 原文用了 **"leaving the bulk properties unchanged"** 这个极强的表述。
> **CMF 含义：F 层是"性价比最高"的差异点，因为它不改基材、不改结构、不改模具（多数情况）。**
> **但代价是：表面层是"附加物"，它有附着力、耐磨、耐候三个薄弱环节。见 §6.6。**

> **原文（p107，工艺三族 —— F 层在工艺分类学中的位置）**：
> 「**At the highest level, processes are segregated into the three families suggested above, which we will abbreviate as shaping, joining, and surface.**」

> **原文（p104，为什么要做表面处理 —— 四类理由）**：
> 「**Almost all manufactured parts are given a surface treatment of some sort. The reasons are diverse: to make the surface harder or more scratch resistant, to protect against corrosion or wear, or to enhance the visual and tactile qualities of the finished product.** There are many different ways of doing each of these, shown in the second level of 5.1: **chemical, mechanical, painting, printing, and so forth**, each with many members.」

★★★★ **判据：表面处理的四类理由，前三个是"技术"，第四个才是"美学"。**
> **① 更硬/更耐刮 ② 防腐蚀/防磨 ③ 装饰（visual + tactile）**
> **判据落地：任何 F 层提案都要同时回答"它让表面更硬了吗？更耐腐蚀了吗？"**
> **只讲"好看"的 F 层提案是不完整的 —— 因为它通常会让前两项退化（见 §6.6 的硬度对比表）。**

## 6.1 表面能"表达"什么：正文的五个案例

> **原文（p110，为什么需要一门"表面美学"的学问）**：
> 「**Surface processes, particularly, influence aesthetics and perceptions through color, reflectivity, texture, and feel, and they are also important for ergonomic reasons, creating the visual and tactile communication with the user.**」

> **原文（p110，脚注 1 —— 一句诚实的自白，值得照录）**：
> 「**We have been unable to find any text that deals with surface processes with predominantly aesthetic function – though the later part of this book goes some way to correcting that.**」

★★★★ **判据：本书作者自己承认：在他写这本书之前，没有一本谈"以美学功能为主的表面工艺"的书。**
> **这是 cmf-system 这个技能存在的第二重理由**（第一重见 ch01 §1.0：设计师没有工具）。

> **原文（p111，"完美表面"的社会心理 —— 全章最锋利的一段）**：
> 「**The late 20th and early 21st centuries are addicted to flawless perfection. Makers of earth-moving equipment have long known that – if their products are to sell – they must deliver them with the same high finish that is required for a passenger car. And this, despite the fact that the first thing a purchaser does is to lower the equipment into a hole half full of mud to start digging. It is because the perfection of the finish expresses the perfection of the equipment as a whole; a poor finish implies, however mistakenly, poor quality throughout.**」

★★★★★ **判据：`表面完美度 = 整体品质的代理指标`，即使这个推断是错的。**
> 原文的证据极强：**挖掘机也要做到轿车级表面，尽管买家第一件事就是把它开进泥坑。**
> **判据：F 层的缺陷不是"工艺问题"，是"品质信号问题"。表面上的每一个瑕疵都被读成"这台机器不行"。**

> **原文（p111，脚注 4 —— 与上一段构成一组张力，必须一起引）**：
> 「**Perfection and Imperfection. Surface perfection is violated by the slightest defect – it has no hope of aging gracefully. Better, to make visual imperfection a part of the personality of the product – something that gives it individuality. It is this, in part, that makes natural materials – wood, leather, and stone – attractive.**」

★★★★★ **判据（本技能认为这是 F 层最重要的战略判据）：**
> **原文同时给了两条相反的路线 ——**
> | 路线 | 原文依据 | 适用条件 | 老化表现 |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | **追求完美** | 「must deliver them with the same high finish」(p111) | 工业化量产、品质即卖点 | **「has no hope of aging gracefully」→ 一旦有瑕疵就崩** |
> | **把不完美纳入人格** | 「**make visual imperfection a part of the personality**」(p111 脚注 4) | 天然材料、手工感、长期使用 | **有瑕疵反而成为个性** |
>
> **判据落地：这两条路线不能混。选了完美路线，就必须保证全程无瑕疵（成本高、良率低）；选了不完美路线，缺陷必须"可控且美观"（如木纹、石纹、皮革纹）。**
> **最糟的是"半完美"——有规律但没规律的纹理，会被读成"次品"。**

> **原文（p111，表面工艺的五种表达用途）**：
> 「**So surface processes can serve to attract, as with the digger. It can suggest, sometimes with the aim of deceiving; metalized plastic is an example. It can surprise, adding novelty – a jug kettle with a thermo-chromic surface coating that changes color as the water heats up. It can entertain: holographic surface films can suggest something lurking inside the article to which it is applied. It can add function: non-slip coatings add an ergonomic function, and technologies for printing electronic circuitry onto products give them sensing and information-processing functions.**」

★★★★ **判据：F 层的五种表达功能（原文成串列出）——**
> **① 吸引（attract）② 暗示/欺骗（suggest, sometimes deceiving）③ 惊奇（surprise，热致变色）④ 娱乐（entertain，全息膜）⑤ 增加功能（add function，防滑涂层 / 印刷电路）**
> **判据：第 ② 项"deceiving"是原文自己用的词，且用金属化塑料举例。这说明"仿制"是合法的 F 层手段；见 §6.5 对"诚实性"的处理。**

> **原文（p112，三个具体的表面表达案例）**：
> 「Designers often create expressive elements through surface treatment. **Screen printing is used to distinguish surfboards, board style, and personal style. Bead blasting of a glass surface gives a frosty sense of elegance. The metal oxide coated sunglasses express high-tech fashion through brilliant, reflected color (5.5).**」

> **★★★ 判据：三种工艺对应三种不同的表达类型 ——**
> **丝印 = 身份/风格标识（内容层）；喷砂玻璃 = 质感/优雅（触感层）；金属氧化物镀膜太阳镜 = 高技术与时尚（色彩+反射层）。**

> **原文（p112，Airstream 案例 —— 制造方法本身即风格，全章最有力的整体案例）**：
> 「There are travel trailers (rvs or caravans) and there is Airstream (5.6). Most travel trailers are built like sheds. **Airstreams are built like aircraft. It is not just the choice of material** – they are, of course, made of aluminum and have been for over 70 years – **it is not just the material but also the manufacturing methods that creates the style. The body is a monocoque shell joined not by welding (as in auto construction) but with deliberately visible rivets. The finish exposes the bare metal surface (paint is the norm for automobiles) emphasizing the aircraft antecedents. Here is a product that derives its quality (reflected in its price) from its use of materials and from the choice of forming, joining, and finishing processes used to make it.**」

★★★★★ **判据：风格 = 材料 + 成形方式 + 接合方式 + 表面方式 的四元组（与 ch03 §3.3 的 p131 一致）。**
> Airstream 的四个选择：**铝 + 硬壳（monocoque）+ 故意露出的铆钉 + 裸金属表面（不喷漆）**。
> **判据：每个选择都是"反常规"的** —— 车身一般焊接、一般喷漆。**正是这四个"反常规"合起来才成为风格。**
> **判据落地：想造"风格"，必须至少有一个环节反常规，且其余三个环节配合它。单独反一个环节 = 怪。**

> **原文（p113，Chapter 5 的结论 —— F 层的完整职责）**：
> 「**The aesthetics of a product are created by the materials of which it is made and the processes used to shape, join, and finish them.** Ideas are expressed and perceptions and associations created by the ways in which these are used. The technical requirements of the product impose certain constraints on shape, but within these there is still room for expressing quality, or humor, or delicacy, or sophistication. Joining, too, can be used to suggest craftsmanship, or robustness, or to differentiate parts of the product that have different purposes. **Above all, surface treatments modify color and reflectivity, texture and feel, and can add pattern, symbol, or text to instruct, amuse, or deceive.**」

★★★★★ **判据（F 层的四元组定义，原文明确）：**
> **`surface treatments modify: ① color ② reflectivity ③ texture ④ feel`**
> **并能额外叠加：`pattern, symbol, or text`（图案/符号/文字）**
> **目的：`to instruct, amuse, or deceive`（告知/娱乐/欺骗）**
>
> **判据落地：F 层规格书必须写四行 —— 颜色、反射率（用 p82 六级）、纹理、手感；外加一行"图案/文字"。**
> **五行齐 = F 层完整；缺任一行 = 交给工厂自己决定。**

### 6.2.2 印刷类（Printing）—— 五种

| 工艺 | 表面硬度 HV | 涂层厚度 μm | 曲面覆盖 | 加工温度 °C | 出处 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Screen Printing** 丝网印刷 | **5–10** | **10–100** | **Poor** | **15–25** | p360 |
| **Pad Printing** 移印 | **5–10** | **6–10** | **Good** | **15–30** | p361 |
| **Cubic Printing** 水转印 | **5–10** | **6–10** | **Good** | **15–30** | p362 |
| **Hot Stamping** 烫印 | **5–50** | **1–50** | **Poor** | **150–300** | p365 |
| **In-Mold Decoration** 膜内装饰 | **5–15** | **10–500** | **Avg. to Good** | **125–200** | p366 |

> **★★★ 判据（印刷类五工艺的能力边界）：**
> **① 硬度全部 5–15 HV（除烫印 5–50）—— 印刷是最软的表面。**
> **② 厚度差 80 倍（6μm 到 500μm）—— IMD 最厚，最耐磨、最能盖住基材缺陷。**
> **③ 曲面覆盖只有"移印/水转印/IMD"是 Good 及以上；丝印与烫印 Poor。**
> **④ 温度两极：湿式印刷 15–30°C（冷）；IMD/烫印 125–300°C（热，会限制基材）。**

> **原文（p360，丝印的关键限制）**：「**Multiple colors can be printed, but each requires a separate screen.**」「Epoxy inks give protection against scratching … The capital cost of the equipment is low. **The process is economic for small runs and one-color printing.**」

> **原文（p361，移印的关键限制）**：「**Its excellent quality of detail makes pad printing attractive for flat items. Pad printing is limited to relatively small images compared to screen printing – usually less than 0.1 m².**」

> **原文（p362，水转印的关键限制）**：「**Registration is difficult, so the image is usually applied in a single operation, and designed so that precise registry is not necessary.**」

> **原文（p365，烫印的关键优势）**：「**The pressure from the die creates a recess for marking, which protects the stamped letter from abrasion**」「**Decoration is permanent and resists peeling, scratching, and abrasion.**」「A range of decorating finishes is available, including **metallic foils, wood grain finishes, and multi-colored graphics.**」

> **原文（p366，IMD 的关键优势）**：「**By printing the graphics on the second surface, the design is held within two thin polymer layers, protecting it from wear and tear. Change of decoration simply requires a change of foil, without changing the mold or the material.**」「**Most thermoplastics – including polycarbonate, but not, however, polyethylene – can be decorated by imd without problems.**」

★★★★★ **判据（从原文提炼的两条硬结论）：**
> **① 耐磨装饰的最优解是 IMD** —— 图案夹在两层膜之间（second surface 印刷），且换图案不换模具。
> **② PE 不能做 IMD**（原文明确 "not, however, polyethylene"）—— 而 PE 又"难以印刷"（p258）。
> **判据：选了 PE，就等于放弃了几乎所有印刷类装饰。这是 M 层选择对 F 层的硬约束。**

### 6.2.3 镀覆类（Plating）—— 四种

| 工艺 | 表面硬度 HV | 涂层厚度 μm | 曲面覆盖 | 加工温度 °C | 出处 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Vapor Metalizing** 气相金属化 | **10–40** | **1–80** | **Avg. to Good** | **18–120** | p367 |
| **Electro-Plating** 电镀 | **取决于镀层材料** | **1–1000** | **取决于镀液** | **5–80** | p368 |
| **Electro-Less Plating** 化学镀 | **600–1100** | **20–120** | **Very good** | **20–50** | p371 |
| **Anodizing** 阳极氧化 | **600–1000** | **1–500** | **Good** | **0–40** | p372 |

> **★★★ 判据（F 层硬度的分水岭）：**
> **阳极氧化与化学镀是唯一两个能把表面硬度做到 600 HV 以上的"表面工艺"**（对比印刷 5–10、涂装 10–16）。
> **判据落地：需要"永不刮花"的外观件，只有三条路：阳极氧化（铝）、化学镀（镍）、搪瓷（7k–10k，但要 600–900°C）。**

> **原文（p372，阳极氧化的机理与合金依赖）**：
> 「**Aluminum is a reactive metal, yet in everyday objects it does not corrode or discolor. That is because a thin oxide film -Al2O3- forms spontaneously on its surface, and this film, though invisible, is highly protective. The film can be thickened and its structure controlled by anodizing. The process is electrolytic; the electrolyte, typically, is dilute (15%) sulfuric acid.**」
> 「**Design Notes**｜**Anodizing is most generally applied to aluminum, but magnesium, titanium, zirconium, and zinc can all be treated in this way. The oxide formed by anodizing is hard, abrasion resistant, and resists corrosion well. The oxide is micro-porous, allowing it to absorb dyes, giving metallic reflectivity with an attractive metallic, colored sheen; and it can be patterned.**」
> 「**Technical Notes**｜There are three main types of electrolytes: **"Weak dissolving" baths are based on boric acid; they form a thin (0.1–1 micron), non-porous oxide layer. "Medium dissolving" baths are based on a solution of chromic acid 3–10%, sulfuric acid 20%; oxalic acid 5%. They give a two-layer oxide: a thin, non-porous initial barrier layer with a porous layer on top of it. "Strong dissolving" baths contain phosphoric acid based electrolytes. The ability of the film to accept dyes depends on alloy composition: pure aluminum and Al-5 Mg alloys are well suited.**」
> 「**The Economics**｜Equipment costs are moderately high, but tooling costs are low. **Alternative treatments are chromating and phosphating: the ranked costs are: anodizing > chromating > phosphating.**」

★★★★★ **判据（阳极氧化的三条硬数据，全书对 F 层最有价值）：**
> **① 三种电解液 → 三种膜结构**：弱溶解（硼酸）→ 0.1–1μm 非孔；中溶解（铬酸 3–10% / 硫酸 20% / 草酸 5%）→ 双层（致密阻挡层 + 多孔层）；强溶解（磷酸）→ 更厚。
> **② 只有"多孔层"才能吸染料** —— 这就是"有色阳极氧化"的物理根据。
> **③ 吸色能力看合金**：纯铝与 Al-5Mg 最适合。
> **④ 成本排序：`anodizing > chromating > phosphating`（阳极氧化最贵）。**

> **原文（p368，电镀的曲面覆盖依赖与内应力）**：
> 「**The ability to plate convoluted shapes depends on what is called the "surface coverage" of the plating bath. Some have very poor surface coverage, meaning that only flat or gently curved surfaces can be plated; others – often assisted by mysterious, proprietary additives to the plating bath – can plate complex shapes with reentrant features.**」
> 「**Many electro-platings have internal stresses; these can be reduced by heat treatment.**」
> 「Plating for aesthetic purposes has its origins in the silver and gold plating of tableware and cutlery ("Sheffield Plate"), and in chrome and nickel plating for shiny, durable surfaces. **Most polymers can now be plated, but care must be taken to prevent the coating breaking or peeling in use.**」

★★★★ **判据：电镀的三个已知风险（原文自己点出的）：**
> **① 曲面覆盖不可控**（"poor surface coverage" 只能镀平面/缓曲面；靠专有添加剂才能镀复杂件）；
> **② 内应力**（可能开裂，需热处理消除）；
> **③ 剥落**（塑料电镀要防 coating breaking or peeling）。

> **原文（p368，材料的健康风险 —— 必须照引的安全信息）**：
> 「**Many electro-plating baths pose environmental and health hazards. Some contain disagreeable chemicals – those with cyanogens are downright nasty. … Cadmium is toxic, and now banned as plating in many European countries. Alternatives to chromium plating, one of the more unpleasant of processes, are sought but not yet found. Nickel can cause allergies and should be kept away from skin contact.**」

★★★★★ **判据（CMF 的合规硬底线）：**
> **① 镉（Cadmium）在欧洲多国已被禁用作镀层**；
> **② 铬（Chromium）电镀是"最不愉快的工艺之一"，替代方案"尚未找到"**；
> **③ 镍（Nickel）会引起过敏，必须远离皮肤接触。**
> **判据落地：任何"长期贴身接触"的产品（手表、首饰、可穿戴），镍释放量是第一条否决项。**
> **这是 F 层唯一一条"技术可行但法规否决"的判据。**

> **原文（p371，化学镀的曲面优势与成本）**：
> 「**Electro-less plating is used when it is impossible or impractical to use normal electro-plating – when complex internal surfaces are to be plated, or when dimensional accuracy of the component is critical, for instance.**」「**The uniformity of the coating is good, even on components of very complex shapes.**」
> 「**Special processing steps are needed for polymers because they are non-conductive and do not catalyze the chemical reduction of nickel; in this case adhesion results only from mechanical bonding of the coating to the substrate surface. To improve this, polymers are typically etched in acidic solutions or organic solvents to roughen their surfaces and to provide more bonding sites.**」
> 「**Electro-less plating is more costly than electro-plating: about 50% more for nickel plating.**」

★★★★ **判据：化学镀是本表中曲面覆盖唯一 "Very good" 的工艺，代价是贵 50%。**
> **且塑料做化学镀，附着力靠"机械咬合"（先酸蚀粗化）—— 这意味着附着力本质上是机械的，不是化学的。**
> **判据：塑料+金属镀层的可靠性天花板由"粗化程度"决定，这是 §6.6 的失效根源之一。**

### 6.2.4 抛光类（Polishing）—— 三种

| 工艺 | 表面硬度 HV | 涂层厚度 | 曲面覆盖 | 加工温度 °C | 出处 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mechanical Polishing** 机械抛光 | **Same as Substrate** | **Not relevant** | **Poor** | **0–30** | p373 |
| **Electro-Polishing** 电解抛光 | **Same as substrate** | **Not relevant** | **Average** | **0–90** | p374 |
| **Chemical Polishing** 化学抛光 | **Depends on substrate** | **Not relevant** | **Good** | **55–140** | p375 |

> **★★★ 判据：抛光类不改硬度、不改厚度 —— 它只改"表面几何"。**
> **所以抛光的量化指标不是 HV/μm，而是"能达到的表面粗糙度"。**

> **原文（p373，机械抛光的四步与成本）**：
> 「**"Spit and polish" is a metaphor for hard work, and one that is relevant here. Mechanical polishing is slow and expensive, and should be used only when absolutely necessary.**」
> 「**Polishing can be divided into four steps: roughening, greasing, buffing, color buffing. From the roughing step to the coloring step finer and softer abrasives are used and the pressure is reduced.**」
> 「**The Economics**｜The equipment and tooling cost for manual polishing are low (**capital cost $100–$1000; tooling cost $10–$200**) but the production rate is low. Those for automatic polishing are greater (**capital cost $20,000–$1,000,000, tooling $1000–$10,000**) but with a higher production rate. **Mechanical polishing is expensive; overspecification should be avoided.**」

★★★★★ **判据（原文自己的警告）：`overspecification should be avoided`（避免过度规格）。**
> 这是全书唯一一条"别把表面要求定太高"的判据，且有成本数据支撑：
> **手动 $100–$1,000 vs 自动 $20,000–$1,000,000 —— 差 1000 倍。**
> **判据落地：图纸上写"Ra 0.4 镜面"之前，先问是手动还是自动产线。**

> **原文（p373，机械抛光的精度极限与适用形状）**：
> 「**Polishing is usually applied to conical, cylindrical, flat, or spherical surfaces**, but the parabolic surface of large mirrors is also polished. There are other ways of polishing – electro-polishing, chemical polishing – that give a highly reflective surface, **but if precision is essential, as it is in accurate optical systems or in precision machine components, then mechanical polishing is the only choice.**」

> **原文（p374，电解抛光的精度与覆盖短板）**：
> 「**Electro-polishing is electro-plating in reverse. It offers an escape from the high cost of mechanical polishing, but at the penalty of much lower precision.**」
> 「**The surface, however, has less mirror-like reflectivity than conventionally polished surfaces. More complex shapes can be polished than are possible by mechanical polishing, but less polishing occurs in holes, recesses, and slots than on more prominent surfaces.**」
> 「**The most economical surface finish attainable by the process is 0.4 microns; 0.05 microns can be achieved – but at a higher cost.**」
> 「**Process time for any one component ranges from 3 to 10 minutes plus the time required for cleaning, rinsing** and other preliminary and subsequent steps.」

★★★★ **判据（抛光的"精度-成本"阶梯，三条硬数据）：**
> | 工艺 | 精度极限 | 曲面能力 | 成本 |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | **机械抛光** | **< 0.1 μm**（"ground and polished to a precision of better than 0.1 micron"） | **Poor**（锥/柱/平/球为主） | 高（手动/自动差 1000 倍） |
> | **电解抛光** | **0.4 μm 经济档 / 0.05 μm 高成本档** | **Average** | 中（无专用工装） |
> | **化学抛光** | 原文未给数值 | **Good**（盲孔/凹槽优势） | 低 |
>
> **判据：`精度` 与 `曲面覆盖` 在抛光类里是严格对立的。**

> **原文（p375，化学抛光的原理与短板）**：
> 「**Chemical polishing relies on a similar principle. It is a process whereby a surface is smoothed by controlled chemical dissolution in a bath of acid containing additives that, by creating a surface boundary-layer, cause protrusions to dissolve faster than flat or intruded parts of the surface** – there is no external power supply.」
> 「**Chemical polishing, like electro-polishing, is a way of brightening a surface, enhancing reflectivity, but without the dimensional precision of mechanical polishing. Much cheaper, of course.**」
> 「**The reflectivity of the surface is less than that given by electro-polishing; the chemically polished surface is often anodized subsequently to produce a clear, colorless protective oxide coating.**」

★★★★ **判据（一条极实用的工艺串联）：化学抛光 → 阳极氧化 是固定搭配。**
> 原文明确："the chemically polished surface is **often anodized subsequently**"。
> **判据落地：要"高反射 + 有保护 + 复杂形状"，路径就是 化学抛光 + 阳极氧化（而非机械抛光）。**

### 6.2.5 涂装类（Coating）—— 五种

| 工艺 | 表面硬度 HV | 涂层厚度 μm | 曲面覆盖 | 加工温度 °C | 出处 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Solvent-Based Painting** 溶剂型涂装 | **10–16** | **10–1000** | **Good** | **10–100** | p376 |
| **Water-Based Painting** 水性涂装 | **10–16** | **10–1000** | **Good** | **10–100** | p377 |
| **Electro-Painting** 电泳涂装 | **5–15** | **10–30** | **Good** | **25–230** | p378 |
| **Powder Coating** 粉末涂装 | **10–16** | **50–2000** | **Good** | **125–400** | p379 |
| **Enameling** 搪瓷 | **7k–10k** | **500–1000** | **Good** | **600–900** | p381 |

> **★★★ 判据：涂装类的硬度几乎全部落在 10–16 HV（除搪瓷）。**
> **这是"有机涂层"的物理上限**：树脂本身的硬度就在这个量级。
> **想突破 16 HV，只能换"无机涂层"—— 搪瓷 7k–10k、阳极氧化 600–1000、化学镀 600–1100。**

> **原文（p376，溶剂型涂装：为什么工业界仍离不开它）**：
> 「**Solvent-based paints give the smoothest, most uniform coating and the greatest control of color – the automobile industry and most product designers insist on them.**」
> 「**Metallic paints mix flake aluminum powder in the coating; the trick is to have the coating thin enough that the metal flakes lie in a plane so that the color does not "flip" when viewed from different angles.**」
> 「**Color is determined by the differential absorption and reflection of the various wavelengths of light; the color seen is that at the least absorbed wavelength from the angle of view.**」

★★★★★ **判据："金属漆为什么从不同角度看颜色会变"（color flip）的原文机理 ——**
> **铝粉片必须"躺平在一个平面内"（lie in a plane），且涂层必须足够薄。**
> **判据落地：做金属漆（珠光/金属色）时，"平"与"薄"是两个必须控制的参数；做不到就会 color flip（色相随视角偏移）。**
> **这是 CMF 与涂料供应商对话的唯一技术抓手。**

> **原文（p376，溶剂型的环境死结）**：
> 「**Solvent-based paints are environmentally bad, so bad that their very future is under threat.**」「**Emissions from the evaporating solvents from solvent-based paints (vocs) are toxic, react in sunlight to form smog, and are generally hostile to the environment. Auto manufacturers and others are under increasing pressure to meet demanding environmental standards. The solvents must now be recaptured, burnt, or recycled.**」
> 「**Paints are a $75 billion per year industry.**」

> **原文（p377，水性涂装的短板 —— 与溶剂型的直接对比）**：
> 「**Water-based paints take longer to dry than many organic solvent-based paints, and give a surface finish that is less good.**」「**Water-based paints must be protected from freezing and applied at a minimum temperature of 10 C.**」
> 「**Water-based paints reduce voc emissions and worker exposure to toxic pollutants and present no fire hazard. They are displacing solvent-based paints, although these dry fast and give a better surface finish with better adhesion.**」

★★★★★ **判据（C 层的环保-品质两难，原文给出了完整的三方权衡）：**
> | 工艺 | 表面质量 | 附着力 | 干燥速度 | 环保 |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | **溶剂型** | **最平滑、最均匀、色彩控制最好** | **最好** | 快 | **最差（VOC 有毒、生成光化学烟雾）** |
> | **水性** | 较差 | 较差 | 慢 | **好（低 VOC、无火灾风险）** |
> | **粉末** | 有"wide variety of glosses and textures" | 好 | 快 | **最好（无溶剂、VOC 极低、回收率 99%）** |
>
> **判据落地：CMF 的"环保 vs 外观"是真实取舍，不是宣传话术。要"最漂亮"，溶剂型仍是最优；要合规，只能退一步。**

> **原文（p378，电泳涂装的核心限制 —— 一色一工艺）**：
> 「**The process is largely used to give uniform undercoats on large, complex components. It lends itself to large volume production of products coated with the same color... The paint layer is distributed evenly over the surface, even in those areas that would otherwise be inaccessible. The main disadvantage is the requirement of a single, uniform color.**」

> **★★★ 判据：电泳涂装的唯一缺点就是"只能一色"。**
> 换来的是"唯一能涂到内腔/隐蔽面的工艺"（原文 "even in those areas that would otherwise be inaccessible"）。
> **判据落地：结构件（车架、管件、箱体内腔）的防腐底涂，靠电泳；外观色靠后续喷涂。**

> **原文（p379，粉末涂装的三种变体与量化数据）**：
> 「**It is an efficient, widely used process for applying decorative and protective finishes to metallic or conducting components.**」「**Powder coatings are available in a wide variety of glosses and textures.**」
> 「**Electrostatic powder coating** 的 Coating rate 是 **5–20 μm/sec**；**polymer flame spraying** 是 **10–30 μm/sec**（capital cost low, tooling low, **equipment is portable**, **no limit on the size of component**）；**fluidized-bed coating** 是 **20–50 μm/sec**。」
> 「**Powder coating is the fastest-growing finishing technique, now representing over 10% of all industrial finishing.**」
> 「**The process uses no solvents and releases almost no volatile organic compounds (vocs) … Excess or oversprayed powders are recycled, blended, and re-sprayed with virgin material, which increases utilization up to 99% by weight.**」
> 「**Powder coating has replaced porcelain enamel on many washer and dryer parts.**」

★★★★★ **判据（粉末涂装成为主流的四条硬理由，全部有数据）：**
> **① 涂层最厚（50–2000 μm）—— 是涂装类里唯一能做"厚涂层"的；**
> **② 速度最快（流体床 20–50 μm/sec）；**
> **③ 利用率 99%（过喷粉末回收再用）；**
> **④ 已占工业涂装 >10%，且正在替代搪瓷。**
> **判据：「厚涂层 + 环保 + 高利用率」三者兼得的唯一工艺就是粉末涂装。**

> **原文（p381，搪瓷的极端性能与极端限制）**：
> 「**Enameling is painting with glass. It creates coatings of exceptional durability: the enameled death-masks of the Pharaohs of Egypt look as bright, vibrant, and perfect today as they did when they were created over 3000 years ago** (Tutankhamen, BC 1358–1340).」
> 「**It is a hot process – the object being enameled must be heated to the melting temperature of the glass powder, limiting it to use on metals and ceramics.**」
> 「**The glass used for enameling must flow easily, wet and bond to the metal surface, and have an expansion coefficient that differs only slightly from that of the underlying metal. This is achieved by using a glass rich in boric oxide (up to 35%), which also gives strong bonds and lustrous colors. In practice enameling usually marries two different kinds of glass: the base coat includes cobalt and nickel oxides, which help to form a strong bond with the metal; the second coat carries color and decoration.**」
> 「**Enamel coatings have exceptional hardness, wear and corrosion resistance, and can be made in an unlimited range of colors.**」
> 「**Enameling is not cheap – it is both slow and energy intensive. Its choice relies on the balance between quality and cost – its durability and aesthetic qualities are unequalled.**」

★★★★★ **判据（搪瓷是 F 层的"天花板"，也是"成本墙"）：**
> **性能**：硬度 7k–10k HV，涂层 500–1000 μm，颜色"unlimited"，3000 年不褪色。
> **限制**：**600–900°C 高温**，只能用于金属与陶瓷，且**玻璃与金属的热膨胀必须"只差一点"**（靠 35% 硼氧化物调和）。
> **判据：任何"既要极致耐久又要彩色"的产品，搪瓷是唯一答案；它的边界是"能不能承受 600°C"。**
> **判据落地：搪瓷的失败模式是"崩瓷"（thermal shock），不是"磨损"。见 §6.6。**

### 6.2.6 其他类（Other）—— 两种

| 工艺 | 表面硬度 HV | 涂层厚度 | 曲面覆盖 | 加工温度 °C | 出处 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Etching** 蚀刻 | **7k–10k** | **500–1000 μm** | **Depends on process** | **18–60** | p382 |
| **Texturing** 纹理化 | **Depends on substrate** | **18–30 μc**（原文单位如此，疑为 μm 之误，照录不改） | 原文未给 | 原文未给 | p383 |

> **⚠ 说明（数值不动原则）**：p382 的 Etching 行写的是 "Surface hardness, Vickers **7k–10k**" 与 "Coating thickness, μm **500–1000**"，
> 与搪瓷（Enameling）的数值完全相同。**本版照原样转录，不做修正、不推测为录入错误**，仅提示读者交叉核对。
> p383 的 Texturing 行单位写作 "**μc**"（非 μm），亦照录不改，并标注原书如此。

> **原文（p382，蚀刻的三种方法与环保排序）**：
> 「**There are three basic processes for etching. Chemical etching is widely used to decorate glass, metal, and polymer objects. The areas or patterns to be etched are exposed to an acid or "mordant"; the areas that are not to be etched are protected with a wax resist.** Glass used to be etched by hydrofluoric acid, a particularly nasty chemical that gives off poisonous fumes; today this has largely been replaced by proprietary etch-gels, by sand etching, or by engraving with a diamond tool.」
> 「**Sand etching makes use of a jet of angular sand particles in a pressurized gas stream to erode the surface where it is not protected by a mask. The method works well with metals, ceramics, and polymers.**」「**Electro-etching is a non-toxic way of etching of metals.**」
> 「**Functions**｜Color, texture, patterning, decorating.」「**Design Notes**｜**Etching is widely used to pattern glass and metal. The freedom of design is limited only by the technique for creating the wax pattern of resistance.**」
> 「**The Economics**｜Chemical etching is slow and expensive. **Sand etching and electro-etching are faster and cheaper.**」
> 「**The Environment**｜**Chemical etching presents major environmental problems associated with toxic fumes, aggressive chemicals, and the difficulty of disposing of spent baths. Sand etching and electro-etching have none of these problems.**」

★★★★ **判据：蚀刻的三条路，按"环保/成本"排序明确 ——**
> **化学蚀刻**：图案自由度最高（"limited only by the wax pattern"）但**最慢最贵最脏**（HF 剧毒）。
> **喷砂蚀刻**：快、便宜、无环保问题，**但"less controllable"**。
> **电解蚀刻**：无毒，用于金属。
> **判据：图案精度要求高 → 化学蚀刻（接受成本与环保）；大面积粗纹理 → 喷砂；金属精细 → 电解。**

> **原文（p383，纹理化 —— 全章最重要的一段，因为它把"纹理"的六种来源全列了）**：
> 「**Texture can be created in many ways: by casting or molding in a patterned tool; or by rolling between patterned rolls ("diamond plate", used for stair treads, is made in this way). By crimping, pressing of sheet between patterned dies: pre-plated sheet, up to 0.8 mm thick, can be crimped to give sparkling reflective patterns; it is used, too, to increase bending stiffness and strength, allowing lighter gauge material for boxes and cans. By sand blasting, or abrasive polishing, the microscopic roughening of the surface in a random or patterned way (as in "brushed aluminum"). By laser texturing, usually applied to rolls or dies; the pulsed laser beam creates minute craters on the roll surface in a precisely controlled pattern that is then transferred to the component during the rolling operation. By electro-texturing, a process like electro-discharge machining (EDM), but using shaped graphite electrodes to smooth or give texture to the surface of the component. And by chemical engraving ("etching" and "chemical polishing") either of molds, rolls, and dies or of the component itself.**」

★★★★★ **判据：纹理的六个来源（原文成串列举，本版整理为可为工艺文件直接使用的表）：**
> | # | 来源 | 原文关键描述 | 典型效果 |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | 1 | **在带图案的模具中铸造/模塑** | "casting or molding in a patterned tool" | 注塑件的标准做法（**由模具决定，非二次加工**） |
> | 2 | **花纹辊压** | "rolling between patterned rolls"，**"diamond plate"（花纹钢板/楼梯防滑板）** | 连续板材 |
> | 3 | **压花（crimping）** | 预镀板 ≤ 0.8mm，「**to increase bending stiffness and strength, allowing lighter gauge material**」 | **同时提刚度** |
> | 4 | **喷砂/磨料抛光** | 「microscopic roughening … in a random or patterned way (**as in "brushed aluminum"**)」 | 拉丝铝 |
> | 5 | **激光纹理** | 「applied to rolls or dies; the pulsed laser beam creates minute craters … **then transferred to the component during the rolling operation**」 | 精确可控、可复制 |
> | 6 | **电火花纹理（electro-texturing）/ 化学蚀刻** | 「using shaped graphite electrodes」/「either of molds, rolls, and dies or of the component itself」 | 模具咬花 |

> **原文（p383，纹理化的三大价值 —— 全章对 F 层最重要的一段）**：
> 「**Functions**｜**Color, texture, feel; friction (grip).**」
> 「**Design Notes**｜**Texturing is important in design for both technical and aesthetic reasons. It can improve product appearance, giving a visually interesting surface as well as hiding minor flaws, scratches, and sink marks. It can improve grip and it contributes to the tactile qualities of a product.**」

★★★★★ **判据（本技能认为这是 F 层最实用的一条判据）：**
> **纹理能"hiding minor flaws, scratches, and sink marks"（掩盖轻微缺陷、划痕与缩水痕）。**
> **这是一条"用 F 层救 M 层"的判据 ——**
> **注塑件的缩水凹点（sink marks）、流痕、顶白，都可以靠"有纹理的模具表面"弱化甚至消除。**
> **判据落地：凡是已知会产生缩水/流痕的部位，模具表面不要做镜面，做细纹理（MT/VDI 等级见注塑技能）。**

> **原文（p383，纹理化的工艺边界）**：
> 「**Virtually any material can be textured by one or another of the methods described above. Texturing by patterned rolls or crimping is limited to simple shapes (like sheet); electro-texturing, chemical engraving, and patterned tool methods allow greater freedom of shape.**」
> 「**The Economics**｜The cost depends on the process. **Some, like sand blasting and pattern rolling, are fast and cheap. Others, like laser texturing and chemical engraving, are slower, and consequently more expensive.**」
> 「**Typical Products**｜There are two very broad classes of application: **to impart visual features and to create tactile characteristics on a product.**」

## 6.4 光泽：F 层唯一有公共标尺的量

> **原文（p82，全部内容已在 ch02 §2.5.1 逐字引用，此处只重列判定表）**
> 测量条件：**入射 60°，观测 120°**

| 等级 | 反射率 % | CMF 语汇 | 典型实现工艺（据 ch06 §6.2） |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dead matte** | **<1** | 死哑、吸光 | 喷砂、化学蚀刻、哑光粉末涂装 |
| **Matte** | **1–10** | 哑光 | 模具细纹、哑光漆 |
| **Eggshell** | **15–20** | 蛋壳光 | 半哑光涂装 |
| **Semi-gloss** | **40–50** | 半光 | 标准涂装 |
| **Full gloss** | **>80** | 高光 | 抛光模具、清漆、溶剂型漆 |
| **Mirror** | **>95** | 镜面 | 机械抛光（<0.1μm）、电镀铬、气相金属化 |

> **★★★ 判据：同一种材料可以跨越多级光泽，这就是 F 层的自由度。**
> 原文 p82 的反射率表里，**不锈钢本身是 70%，但经"镜面抛光"可达 >95%，经"喷砂"可落到 1–10%。**
> **判据落地：CMF 讨论"要不要换材料"之前，先问"同一个材料换个表面能不能解决"。**

> **原文（p82，光泽的两个决定因素）**：
> 「**The reflectivity of a surface depends on the material of which it is made or coated, and the smoothness of this surface: both color and texture are important.**」

★★★★ **判据：光泽 = 材料 + 光滑度，且"颜色"与"纹理"都参与。**
> **推论（重要的反直觉）：颜色也影响反射率。** 黑色哑光反射 <1%，白色哑光的反射率会显著更高（因为漫反射的基色变了）。
> **所以"同一个光泽等级"在深色与浅色上，视觉上不是一回事。**

## 6.5 "诚实性"：F 层的伦理边界

> **原文（p101–p102，material honesty 学派的观点）**：
> 「**There is a school of design thinking that holds as a central tenet that materials must be used "honestly", By this they mean that deception and disguise are unacceptable – each material must be used in ways that expose its strengths, its natural appearance, and its intrinsic qualities.** The idea has its roots in the tradition of craftsmanship – the potters use of clays and glazes, the carpenters use of woods, the skills of silversmiths in crafting beautiful objects, each exploiting the unique qualities of the materials with which they work – **an integrity to craft and material.**」

> **原文（p102，本书的立场 —— 反对"只能用得诚实"这一教条）**：
> 「**This is a view to be respected. But it is not the only one. Design integrity is a quality that consumers value, but they also value other qualities: humor, sympathy, surprise, provocation, even shock.** You don't have to look far to find a product that has any one of these, and often it is achieved by using materials in ways that deceive or inspire. **Polymers, as we have said, are frequently used in this way – their adaptability invites it. And, of course, it is partly a question of definition – if you say that a characterizing attribute of polymers is their ability to mimic other materials, then using them in this way is honest.**」

★★★★★ **判据（原文给出了一个漂亮的逻辑出口）：**
> **"诚实"的判定取决于"你把什么定义为该材料的特征属性"。**
> **如果把"能模仿别的材料"本身定义为聚合物的特征，那用它模仿就是诚实的。**
> **判据落地：CMF 提案里出现"仿金属/仿木/仿皮"时，不要争"诚不诚实"，要争"这是不是这个材料的强项"。**
> **聚合物能仿，所以让它仿；金属不能仿木，所以别让它仿。**

> **原文（p102，本章的收束 —— 与可持续性的连接）**：
> 「**Honesty and authenticity are an important part of design, but so is invention and expression. There are moments in the design process when we need to use materials to create structure and surface that is simple and pure. But there are just as many moments when we need to use materials to create iconic behaviors and corporate logos. Both are a necessary part of good design**, and it is these elements of materiality that make a product successful in the market and a classic in the eyes and minds of design history.」
> 「**Gold, silver, platinum, diamond and sapphire have associations of wealth, success, sophistication and lasting value; used in a product they give it the same associations. Carved or polished wood suggests craftsmanship; ceramics suggest elements of luxury or extreme performance requirements. Metals, generally, are recognizable as metals; a product made from them acquires the attributes of industry and strength as part of its personality almost immediately.**」
> 「**But polymers can – at least in appearance – assume the character of almost any material; in particular, they can be made to look like metal, or like wood, or even like glass.**」
> 「**The beauty that designers need to find in plastics is the ability to do something with them that is impossible in any other way. And, as with all materials, we need to be careful and considerate about how they can and will be recycled.**」

★★★★ **判据（F 层的"价值排序"，原文给出）：**
> **金/银/铂/钻石/蓝宝石 = 财富、成功、精致、恒久 → 用于产品即传递这些**
> **雕琢或抛光的木材 = 手艺（craftsmanship）**
> **陶瓷 = 奢华 或 极端性能**
> **金属 = 工业与强度（"almost immediately"）**
> **聚合物 = 唯一的"任意伪装"能力**
>
> **判据落地：材料的"符号价值"是既成事实，不需要论证。CMF 提案要论证的只有一件事 ——「这种符号是否与目标用户的联想一致」。**

> **不是"表面层本身坏了"，是"表面层待不住了"。**
> **判据落地：F 层评审的第一问永远是"这一层靠什么待在上面？"**
> 答案有四类：**机械咬合（塑料+镀层）、化学键（硅烷偶联）、固熔（阳极氧化/搪瓷）、范德华（部分涂装）。可靠度递减。**

### 6.6.2 纹理是"预防性 F 层"（原文唯一的正向防御判据）

> **原文（p383，再引一次，因它是本节的关键）**：
> 「**It can improve product appearance, giving a visually interesting surface as well as hiding minor flaws, scratches, and sink marks.**」

★★★★ **判据：纹理的第一功能不是"好看"，是"容错"。**
> **它把"完美路线"（p111）的容差放宽了 —— 有纹理的表面，细微划痕与缩水痕不可见。**
> **判据落地：良率是 CMF 的现实约束。良率低的部位，加纹理；良率高的部位，才能上镜面。**
