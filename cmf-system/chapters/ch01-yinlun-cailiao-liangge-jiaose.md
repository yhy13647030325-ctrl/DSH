## 1.0 本章解决什么

> **原文（p12）**：
> 「**We live in a world of materials; it is materials that give substance to everything we see and touch.** Our species – homo sapiens – differs from others most significantly, perhaps, through the ability to design – to make things out of materials – and in the ability to see more in an object than merely its external form. Objects can have meaning, carry associations, or be symbols of more abstract ideas.」

★★★ 本书的第一句话就把"材料"放到了"实体的唯一来源"的位置：
**看得见摸得着的每一样东西，其"实体性"（substance）都来自材料**。

> **原文（p14）**：
> 「Thus materials have **two overlapping roles**: that of providing **technical functionality** and that of creating **product personality**. It is here that an **imbalance** becomes apparent. Technical designers have ready access to information of the sort they need – **handbooks, selection software, advisory services from material suppliers** – and to analysis and optimization codes for safe, economical design. **Industrial designers express frustration, both in print and in interviews, that they do not have equivalent support.**」

★★★★ **全书级判据：材料有两个"重叠"的角色——技术功能 与 产品性格。而信息支持严重不对称。**

这是整本书、也是 cmf-system 这个技能存在的理由：
**工程师有手册、有选材软件、有供应商技术支持；工业设计师什么都没有。**
CMF 系统要补的，正是"设计师那一侧缺的工具"。

## 1.1 混合"愉悦的装饰"与"功利的功能"

> **原文（p12）**：
> 「Some of these objects had a predominantly functional purpose: the water wheel, the steam engine, the gas turbine. Others were (and are) purely symbolic or decorative: the cave paintings of Lascaux, the wooden masks of Peru, the marble sculptures of Attica. **But most significantly, there are objects that combine the functional with the symbolic and decorative.** The combination is perhaps most obvious in architecture …」

> **原文（p12）**：
> 「On a smaller scale, **product designers seek to blend the technical with the aesthetic, combining practical utility with emotional delight.** Think of Wedgewood china, Tiffany glass, Chippendale furniture – these were first made and bought to fulfill a functional purpose, but survive and are treasured today as much for their appeal as objects of beauty.」

**图 1.1 Mixing Delightful Decoration and Utilitarian Function**
The Stata Center at MIT（图片来源：Prof. John Fernandez, Department of Architecture, MIT）
**图 1.2 Mixing Delightful Decoration and Utilitarian Function**：**A Colt pistol**

> **★★★ 判据：CMF 处理的从来不是"装饰"，而是"功能与符号的混合体"。**
> 一把柯尔特手枪（图 1.2）既是一件能打死人的工具，也是一件被雕刻、被收藏、被传家的物件。
> **判据：任何 CMF 决策都必须同时过"功能关"和"符号关"，缺一即失败。**

## 1.2 同一份"混合"的第二个说法：灵感

> **原文（p13）**：
> 「With respect to materials and design, it is **the combination of elements of art and science that make it work. Materials are not simply numbers on a datasheet.** And design is not a meaningless exercise in styling and it is not an isolated exploration of technology.」

> **原文（p13）**：
> 「**Advances in materials enable advances in industrial design**, just as they do for technical design – together these advances allow new behaviors, new experiences, new architectures (1.4).」

> **原文（p13）**：
> 「And here we need a word that requires definition: **"inspiration" – the ability to stimulate creative thinking.** New developments in materials and processes are sources of inspiration for product designers, suggesting novel visual, tactile, sculptural, and spatial solutions to product design. Examples drawn from the recent past are **the ability to color and mold polymers to make bright, translucent shapes; the co-molding of elastomers to give soft, tactile surfaces; toughened and textured glass to create transparent walls and flooring; surface coatings that reflect, refract, or diffuse light; carbon fiber composites that allow exceptionally slender, delicate structures** – and there are many more.」

**图 1.3 Function, Use, and Personality**
A bicycle must function (wheels, chain, gears), be designed for use (to carry stuff from one place to another), and have a personality that fits its owner (decoration on the frame, handlebar tape).
**图 1.4 A Virtual Violin**
The form of the violin is an essential part of its personality. In this electronic violin, the ghost-like form both makes the connection to the original and suggests the transmutation that has taken place.（图片来源：Yamaha Corp.）

★★★★ **判据：CMF 的五个"上游能力"（原文成串列举的就是 C 与 F 的历史）** ——
**① 塑料能被染色并模塑成明亮的半透明形状（C+F+M）**
**② 弹性体共模塑（co-molding）给出柔软的触感表面（F 的手感层）**
**③ 钢化并带纹理的玻璃造出透明的墙与地板（M+F）**
**④ 能反射/折射/漫射光的表面涂层（F 的光学层）**
**⑤ 碳纤维复合材料允许异常纤细的结构（M 的形态自由度）**

> **★★★ 判据：这五条全是"材料进步 → 造型语言进步"的实例。**
> **CMF 不是在既有材料上刷漆，而是在"材料能力表"里找还没被用掉的那几格。**

## 1.3 三个层次：功能 / 使用 / 性格

> **原文（p13）**：
> 「People – consumers – buy things because they like them – love them, even. To succeed, a product must, of course, function properly, but that is not enough: it must be **easy and convenient to use**, and it must have a **personality that satisfies, inspires, gives delight** (1.3). This last – personality – depends strongly on the industrial design of a product. **When many technically equivalent products compete, market share is won (or lost) through its visual and tactile appeal, an exploration of other senses or emotional connection, the associations it carries, the way it is perceived, and the experiences it enables.**」

★★★★★ **判据：当技术等价时，市场份额在"视觉与触觉吸引力"上决出。**
> 这句话是 CMF 的经济学根据：**技术同质化越严重，CMF 的权重越高。**
> 原文列出的取胜维度共 5 个：**① 视觉与触觉吸引力 ② 其他感官 ③ 情感连接 ④ 联想 ⑤ 被感知的方式与它带来的体验**。
> **CMF 直接控制 ①，间接控制 ②③④⑤。**

## 1.4 虚拟小提琴：形态作为性格的载体

> **原文（p13，图 1.4 图注）**：
> 「**The form of the violin is an essential part of its personality.** In this electronic violin, the ghost-like form both makes the connection to the original and suggests the transmutation that has taken place.」

> **★★ 判据：形态（form）与材料（material）是"性格"的两条通道，且不可互相替代。**
> 这把电子小提琴拆掉了共鸣箱（功能上不再需要），却**保留了琴的"幽灵形态"**——
> **判据：功能要素可以删，性格要素不可删。CMF 的取舍顺序应是：先删功能冗余，再审性格必需。**

## 1.5 建立品牌（Built Brand）：从材料到身份

> **原文（p15–p16，Kara Johnson 自述）**：
> 「Our consideration of materials stretched from product to space to digital or service experiences. **Materiality is an idea that includes the following:** a living hinge made of molded felt, the structure of expanded polystyrene bead packaging, the rust colored primer that paints the Golden Gate Bridge, patina on copper and any other surface, the texture and pattern of pixels, knit graffiti on the streets of London, **the sweet smell of molded bioplastics**.」

**图 1.5 Built Brand**
Building brands with a focus on product…from the inside-out…starting with materials as the key ingredient to a complete (36) brand experience.

> **原文（p16）**：
> 「**Your brand is manufactured, not managed.** Your brand is a sum of the signature moments you create where people can physically connect to your brand. **Your product is your story, your story is your product. The strategy isn't finished until the design is.**」

> **原文（p16）**：
> 「For some companies, in some industries, for some consumers, **brand is built from the inside-out (1.5), from material to product to identity** and digital experiences to systems and the organization. When brand is built from the inside-out, **elements of materiality and making matter most.** Design plays the role of connecting materials to a bigger story.」

★★★★★ **判据：`品牌是被"制造"出来的，不是被"管理"出来的`。**
> 这条直接决定了 CMF 的层级：**CMF 不是"设计完成后贴上去的风格"，而是品牌的第一物理证据。**
> 原文的顺序是 **材料 → 产品 → 身份**（inside-out），不是身份 → 产品 → 材料。
> **判据：CMF 决策出错时，"品牌故事"整条链断在最底下那一环。**

> **原文（p16，Kara 的三个十年主题）**：
> 「Looking forward, over the next 10 years, the following three themes will drive the work that we do as designers and influences the role of materials: **(1) the resurgence of making and the maker movement, (2) a global market that drives exploration of what it means to be made in a particular place/geography, and (3) the search for a more tangible way of thinking about, talking about, and doing brand.**」

> **★★★ 判据：CMF 的第三个主题是"如何更可触地谈论品牌"**——
> **这正是 CMF 文档（色板、样件、表面样板）存在的意义：它们是"可触的论点"。**

## 1.6 Mike Ashby 的自我定位：三种书，本书是第三种

> **原文（p18）**：
> 「**There are three kinds of materials-related books with a design focus. The first are inspirational, relying on images to showcase materials, accompanied by text that expresses the emotional and aesthetic response of the writer. The second are books on material selection in engineering design, developing systematic methods of matching material choice to the mechanical thermal, electro-magnetic, and environmental constraints set by the technical requirements of the design. The third – of which this book is an example – seeks to connect and blend the approaches of the other two.**」

★★★★ **判据：这个"三分法"就是 CMF 的知识边界图。**
| 类别 | 内容 | CMF 系统中的位置 |
|---|---|---|
| 第一类：灵感书 | 图片 + 作者的情感与美学反应 | 对应 **C/F 的感知层**（不可量化那部分） |
| 第二类：工程选材书 | 力学/热/电磁/环境约束的系统方法 | 对应 **M 的技术层**（Ashby 索引、属性图） |
| **第三类：本书** | **连接并混合前两类** | **= CMF 系统本身** |

> **★★★ 判据：只有 M 没有 C/F → 是"第二类书"（工程选材，产物冰冷）；只有 C/F 没有 M → 是"第一类书"（灵感画册，产物造不出来）。**
> **CMF 的成败，就在这个"连接"上。**
