## 4.1 总纲：自报方法的共同天花板

自报类方法共享一个结构性缺陷，原书在两处独立写明了它：

> **原文（p173，Surveys 一节）**：
> 「Like any **self-report instrument**, surveys **may not be an accurate reflection of true thoughts, feelings, perceptions, or even behaviors**. This argues for the careful design and administration of surveys, and the use of **complementary observations or other methods**.」

> **原文（p48）**：
> 「Unless observed, **most people will summarize their work activities and speak abstractly about tasks**, because typically their processes are **invisible to them**.」

> **★★★★ 判据：自报方法拿到的是"人对自己行为的叙述"，不是行为本身。**
> 两条原文各说一面：p173 说**可能不准**（态度/行为的自陈失真），
> p48 说**不是不愿说而是看不见**（默会知识）。
> **两条合起来＝自报方法在"流程细节"和"真实行为"两类问题上系统性失效。**

> **★★★ 判据：因此自报方法的标准配置是"配一个观察类方法"。**
> 原文在问卷、调查、日记、焦点小组四节都重复了同一个处方（见 4.7 节汇总表）。

## 4.2 日记法（方法 30，p67–p68 PDF）

### 4.2.1 定义与最大适用面

> **原文（p67）**：
> 「Diaries or journals are guiding artifacts that allow people to conveniently and expressively convey personal details about their daily life and events to design teams.」

> **原文（p67）**：
> 「Diary studies are ideal for collecting information from participants **across time**, sampling their thoughts, feelings, or behaviors at **key moments throughout a day, week, or month**.」

> **★★★★ 判据：日记法解决的是"跨时间"问题——这是所有一次性方法（访谈、观察、可用性测试）结构上做不到的。**
> 时间跨度原文给了具体量级：**一天 / 一周 / 一个月**（day, week, or month）。
> 超出这个量级，脱落率与自陈失真会同时上升（原文未量化，见 4.9 空白）。

### 4.2.2 日记本的结构（本节最可执行）

> **原文（p67）**：
> 「Blank journals are issued to participants in person or by mail. The diary must be **designed for portability and ease of use**. An **overview of the topic of interest** is included up front, with **instructions on how and when to complete requested entries**, and a **sample entry**. Participants may be requested to document **each time they engage in a particular behavior, encounter a product or situation, or have specific types of interactions**. Other studies may require **regular entries at particular times of day**, or a **log of items in summary at day's end**. When used within experience sampling, diary entries are made at **random times** when the participant is **signaled by a device or alarm**.」

**日记本必备四件（据原文整理为可执行清单）**：

| # | 要素 | 原文依据 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **前置的主题说明**（overview of the topic of interest） | 「An overview of the topic of interest is included up front」 |
| 2 | **何时写、怎么写的指令** | 「instructions on how and when to complete requested entries」 |
| 3 | **一条示范条目**（sample entry） | 「and a sample entry」 |
| 4 | **便携、易用**（物理形态要求） | 「designed for portability and ease of use」 |

> **★★★ 判据：「一条示范条目」是最容易省、也最不该省的一项。**
> 自报数据的质量取决于被试对"该写多细"的理解，而理解靠示范而非说明。
> 没有 sample entry，你会收到"今天正常"这种句子。

**页面设计（原文给了具体手法）**：

> **原文（p67）**：
> 「**each page entry should be guided with a brief question or prompt**, with **appropriate space for encouraging the desired length of text**. Creative page formats can be used to invite other forms of recording as well, such as **sketches or drawings, symbols, or photographs, text or visuals that can be circled or checked, or the use of provided stickers**. A small set of questions or space for reflections, and a request for **demographic information**, is sometimes placed at the **end** of the diary.」

> **★★★ 判据：「留白的大小」是一种隐性指令。**
> 原文写「appropriate space for **encouraging the desired length of text**」——
> 空格小＝鼓励短答，空格大＝鼓励长答。这是用版式做提问，比文字要求有效。
> **推论：想要细节就给大格；只想打勾就别留大格。**

**三种记录节律（会决定分析方式）**：

| 节律 | 触发 | 原文表述 | 适合 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **事件触发** | 某事发生就写 | 「each time they engage in a particular behavior, encounter a product or situation」 | 低频、高显著性事件 |
| **时间触发** | 每天固定时刻写 | 「regular entries at particular times of day」 | 有固定节律的日常 |
| **日终汇总** | 一天结束写一次 | 「a log of items in summary at day's end」 | 高频、琐碎事件（靠回忆汇总） |
| **随机信号触发** | 设备响就写 | 「random times when the participant is signaled by a device or alarm」 | 见 4.3 经验取样 |

> **★★ 判据：「日终汇总」本质上是回忆性数据，不是即时数据。**
> 原文把它与"事件触发"并列，但**两者证据强度不同**：日终汇总已经掺入回忆重构。
> 引用这类数据时不能与即时条目等量齐观。

### 4.2.3 用途与边界

> **原文（p67）**：
> 「Diary studies are useful tools in **exploratory research**, preparing the designer for further research by contributing to an understanding of participant user groups. While diary studies are typically conducted with a **relatively small sample**, common themes and patterns can emerge. The synthesized information is intended primarily for **inspiration** and to indicate **design implications for generative design**.」

> **原文（p67）**：
> 「However, diaries can also be used in **generative research**. For example, journals are often issued to **sensitize participants** to research topics leading up to participatory design exercises such as collage, flexible modeling, or co-design workshops. In **rare cases**, diaries may be used for usability studies or evaluation, as a means of collecting feedback from users testing products in context over time.」

> **★★★★ 判据：「相对小样本」是原文的原话，但它没给数字——而且它同时把日记法定位为"启发用"。**
> 「typically conducted with a **relatively small sample**」＋「intended primarily for **inspiration**」。
> **定性与目的绑在一起：既然产出是启发而非统计，样本量就按"能否冒出共同主题"来定，而不是按外推来定。**

> **★★★ 判据：「rare cases」是原文对"拿日记做可用性评估"的明确降级。**
> 原文用「In rare cases」限定。**实务含义：日记不是评估工具的主力，别用它替代 ch09 的可用性测试。**

**技术形态**：

> **原文（p67）**：
> 「While traditionally diary studies have been completed with **paper and pen**, technology affords novel forms of entries such as **digital photos, video, and audio** that may be recorded on digital devices, and sent via email or uploaded on provided sites. Digital diaries can also be completed as an **integrated component of online or device interactions**, with entry forms imbedded directly within software interfaces.」

| 方法 | 什么时候用 | 什么时候不能用 | 最少样本量 | 产出物 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **日记法** | 要跨时间（一天/一周/一月）的体验与行为；要"当场"而非"事后回忆"；要为后续共创做预热（sensitize） | 要统计分布；被试读写能力受限；时间跨度远超一月；需要观察真实动作 | **原书未给具体数字**。原文只有定性表述「typically conducted with a **relatively small sample**」 | 日记本/数字日记、条目化事件记录、主题与模式、设计含义、共创预热素材 |

## 4.3 经验取样法（方法 37，p81–p82 PDF）

### 4.3.1 定义：随机信号 + 即时记录

> **原文（p81）**：
> 「**Experience sampling** allows the designer to collect **snapshots** of behaviors, interactions, thoughts, or feelings from people who **self-report in real time when signaled at random or timed intervals**.」

> **原文（p81）**：
> 「Experience sampling requires that the participant record or document something specific **when signaled**, typically by a **device alarm**. In the past, this signal was sent to a **pager** carried by the participant, hence the common reference to the method as a "**beeper study**." Current technology allows other signaling opportunities, including **new smartphone applications** that can be programmed to alert the participant when it is time to make or send an entry.」

> **★★★ 判据：随机信号的作用是打断"回忆重构"，逼出"当下状态"。**
> 这一条把它与日记法的"日终汇总"彻底分开（见 4.2.2 节判据）。
> **代价是干扰生活**——原文未讨论干扰成本（见 4.9 空白）。

### 4.3.2 条目设计：从"泛"到"具体"

> **原文（p81）**：
> 「The behaviors, interactions, thoughts, or feelings of interest to the design researcher are guided by **clear instructions issued in advance**, and are entered into a **preestablished form**, often a diary or journal. The entries may be **general**, such as "**document your feelings right now**," or quite **specific**, such as "**list the communication products you are currently using**." The journal should be well designed for **portability and ease of use** in documenting the required items of interest.」

> **★★★ 判据：条目可以从"记录此刻感受"到"列出此刻在用的通信产品"——后者已经是可计数的行为数据了。**
> 这个梯度很实用：**越具体 = 越好分析 = 越少意外发现**。
> 选哪端取决于你要"发现"还是"计数"。

### 4.3.3 形象与文字必须可对应

> **原文（p81）**：
> 「Often experience sampling will require the participant to document surroundings or relevant artifacts with **quick sketches or photography**. In the case of photography, **care must be taken to match images to text entries**—a simple matter in the past with **Polaroid cameras coupled with pen and paper notations**—which is **more challenging with digital images**. However, new technology affords the possibility of documenting (and sending) both photo and text entries through smartphones, or to use **audio entries instead of text**.」

> **★★ 判据：这是数字化带来的一个隐性退步——图文配对变难了。**
> 拍立得时代，照片和纸本笔记天然同处一处；数码时代，两个流是分开的，
> 事后要对齐时间戳。**这是方法设计必须预先解决的操作问题，不是技术细节。**

### 4.3.4 它与设计人种志的关系（原文最精辟的一句）

> **原文（p81）**：
> 「Experience sampling is a form of **design ethnography**, because it **condenses the more traditional time required for extended immersion** through the collection of **strategic samples** of behaviors, interactions, thoughts, or feelings. When done well, these samples can constitute a **more comprehensive whole across time or individuals**, giving the designer a relatively complete picture of behaviors of interest for any particular design study.」

> **★★★★ 判据：经验取样是"用抽样换时间"的人种志——它把"长期泡在现场"压缩成"战略性采样"。**
> 与 ch03 §3.2.1 的判据完全咬合（设计人种志＝时间抽样）。
> **这也说明二者的短板相同：都拿不到"长期变迁"。**

| 方法 | 什么时候用 | 什么时候不能用 | 最少样本量 | 产出物 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **经验取样法** | 要"当下状态"与情境相关性；要在真实环境中捕捉瞬时体验 | 需要长时间不被打断的任务；被试不能随身带设备；要长期趋势 | **原书未给具体数字**（未给信号频率、每人条目数、人数） | 即时条目集、图文配对记录、跨时间/跨个体的行为快照、频率分布 |

## 4.4 照片研究（方法 64，p135–p136 PDF）

> **原文（p135）**：
> 「**Photo studies** invite the participant to photo-document aspects of his or her life and interactions, providing the designer with **visual, self-reported** insights into user behaviors and priorities.」

> **原文（p135）**：
> 「Photo studies are common in **exploratory research** as a method for understanding the world of users, **particularly when engaging in territory unfamiliar to the designer**.」

### 4.4.1 怎么下指令

> **原文（p135）**：
> 「Participants are given **general instructions on what to document** through images, for example, to take pictures of **each technology interaction during the day**, or **objects of significance**, or **items associated with scheduling appointments and meetings**. They may also be instructed to take images of their **surroundings when they are feeling a particular way** (energized, sad, overwhelmed), or, **in rare circumstances**, of human interactions.」

> **★★★ 判据：指令可以是"物"（objects of significance）、"事"（technology interaction）、也可以是"情绪触发"（feeling a particular way）。**
> 三种指令类型对应三种分析难度：**物最好归类，事最好对齐时间线，情绪最难编码但发现力最强。**

### 4.4.2 伦理红线

> **原文（p135）**：
> 「When documenting human interactions, **caution needs to be exercised** in requesting photos of **uncomfortable situations such as work encounters**, or **photographing the personal details of others**.」

> **★★ 判据：拍"他人"和拍"不舒适的场合"是两个需要额外谨慎的点。**
> 原文用「in rare circumstances」限定拍人际互动，不是常规操作。

### 4.4.3 必须配回访（本节最重要的一条）

> **原文（p135）**：
> 「To synthesize findings from photo studies, the designer may rely **solely** on the photographs and simple notes provided by the participant. However, it is **more common, and recommended**, to have the participant **explain his or her photos in follow-up interviews**, possibly to include **sorting or collage** of the images or detailing them along a **time line** or other axis, such as **positive–negative interactions**.」

> **★★★★ 判据：照片本身不是数据，照片＋当事人解释才是数据。**
> 原文用「recommended」明确站边：**只看照片是"可以做但不推荐"的做法**。
> 原因不难推：一张照片能有一百种解读，作者意图只有当事人知道。

> **原文（p135）**：
> 「Because the output of photo studies is primarily used for **exploratory purposes**, the research is summarized as **visual support for understanding and inspiration**, **not for specific meaning through formal analysis**. However, **patterns and themes might emerge** across an inventory of several photos from multiple participants, providing insight for design implications.」

**一个反直觉的观察（原文给了原因）**：

> **原文（p135）**：
> 「Like many creative methods, participants are **more likely to enthusiastically engage** in requests to complete a photo study of their personal lives **than they are to traditional means of behavior survey**.」

> **★★ 判据：照片研究的应答率高于传统行为调查——这是它被低估的实用优势。**
> 同样是"报告你的行为"，拍照比填表更受欢迎。**在低应答率是主要风险的项目里，这条足以让天平倾斜。**

| 方法 | 什么时候用 | 什么时候不能用 | 最少样本量 | 产出物 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **照片研究** | 进入陌生领域；要理解"什么对用户重要"；要视觉素材支撑沟通 | 要精确行为频次；无法做回访解释（只剩照片=不推荐）；涉及他人隐私 | **原书未给具体数字** | 照片集、后续访谈中的照片解释、按时间线/正负轴的排序、拼贴、视觉素材库 |

## 4.5 文化探针（方法 24，p55–p56 PDF）

### 4.5.1 定义：它刻意"不"做正式分析

> **原文（p55）**：
> 「**Cultural probes** are **provocative instruments** given to participants to inspire new forms of **self-understanding and communication** about their lives, environments, thoughts, and interactions.」

> **原文（p55）**：
> 「Cultural probes consist of any number of materials designed to **inspire people** to thoughtfully consider personal context and circumstance, and respond to the design team in **unique, creative ways** facilitated by the provocations. Cultural probe studies have used **postcards, maps, journals, cameras, recording devices, and various pieces of text and imagery** to guide personal responses.」

> **★★★★ 判据：「provocative（挑动性）」是方法名的一部分，不是形容词。**
> 探针的目的不是**收集**，是**挑动**。这与问卷、访谈的目标方向相反。

### 4.5.2 边界：明确"不正式分析"

> **原文（p55）**：
> 「As an exploratory research method, cultural probes are **not intended to be formally analyzed**, but rather to serve as **inspirational pieces** identifying key patterns and themes that might emerge from a participant group or culture. They serve to **begin a conversation about possibilities** that might exist by design, **in tandem with other informative research methods** such as observations, site visits, interviews, and secondary sources.」

> **★★★★ 判据：文化探针的产出不能单独作为设计依据——原文写死了"不打算做正式分析"。**
> 这条是本技能里少见的"作者主动限制自己方法效力"的表述。
> **实务含义：如果上级问"探针数据怎么支撑这个决策"，正确答案是"探针不承担这个职责"。**

### 4.5.3 一个完整案例（Gaver 等，可作为设计参照）

> **原文（p55）**：
> 「In a study of interaction techniques to increase the presence of the elderly in three European communities, **Bill Gaver et al.** created cultural probe kits to gain **impressionistic views** of participant cultures, preferences, beliefs, and desires. **Postcards** contained obscure images and were preaddressed for return to the design team, posing open questions about the cultural environment, life, and technology. Several **maps** printed on various papers asked the elderly to mark zones for **meeting others, being alone, dreaming, and going somewhere they could not**. **Disposable cameras** were provided to take images of both assigned and self-selected things, and to use these in telling a story in a small album included in the kit. Finally, a **media diary** asked about technology interactions and communication.」

> **★★★ 判据：注意那张地图的四个分区——「见人 / 独处 / 梦想 / 去不了的地方」。**
> 前三项是常规，**第四项「去不了的地方」是探针之所以"挑动"的典型手法**：
> 它问的不是现状，是"被限制的愿望"。这类问题用问卷问不出来。

### 4.5.4 设计原则

> **原文（p55）**：
> 「Cultural probes are **specifically casual and informal**, yet **thoughtful in their aesthetic craft, message, and delivery**, created to **inspire delight and respect, response and return**. The materials created for probe kits should be **varied and imaginative**, designed to elicit responses that are relevant to the particular design inquiry. When done well, cultural probes will gain **respectable response rates comparable to or exceeding traditional methods**, with investment in the exercise by enthusiastic participants, and rich information to inspire great design.」

> **★★ 判据：探针的"随意"是设计出来的随意——craft 必须考究。**
> 原文用了一个很好的矛盾表述：「casual and informal, yet thoughtful in their aesthetic craft」。
> 做得糙 ≠ 随意；做得糙只会降低寄回率。

| 方法 | 什么时候用 | 什么时候不能用 | 最少样本量 | 产出物 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **文化探针** | 探索期；要灵感与可能性；要理解"愿望与限制"；无法长期在场 | 要可辩护的决策依据；要统计分析；项目周期紧（寄出寄回耗时）；被试不愿创作 | **原书未给具体数字**。原文只给"应答率可与传统方法相当或更高"的定性表述，无人头数 | 回收的探针包（明信片/地图/相机/日记）、印象式理解、主题线索、设计对话的起点 |

## 4.6 焦点小组（方法 43，p93–p94 PDF）

### 4.6.1 定义：力量来自群体动力学

> **原文（p93）**：
> 「The dynamic created by a **small group of well-chosen people**, when guided by a **skilled moderator**, can provide deep insight into themes, patterns, and trends.」

> **原文（p93）**：
> 「The power of focus groups lies in the **group dynamic** that it creates. When properly recruited, and under the guidance of an experienced moderator, participants can quickly **accept one another as peers**. In a **peer setting** (where the **fear of being judged is diminished**), participants are more likely to share experiences, stories, memories, perceptions, wants/needs, and fantasies. A well-moderated focus group will leverage the **nonthreatening group dynamic** to get past generalizations and start to peel back what is valuable and important to the group, and what makes the group unique.」

> **★★★★ 判据：焦点小组的机制是「peer setting 降低被评判的恐惧」——因此它天然不适合"有高低差"的人群组合。**
> 一旦组内有身份差（上下级、医患、师生），peer setting 就崩了，
> 剩下的只有"公开场合该说的话"。**这是选人规则，不是技巧问题。**

### 4.6.2 六类它擅长的问题（可直接当"该不该用焦点小组"的检查表）

> **原文（p93）**：
> 「A good moderator can get everyone in the group to provide more insight regarding any of the following design-related inquiries:
> • **reviewing processes that take place over an extended period of time**
> • **explanations of what is not desirable about the current state**, or about common misunderstandings with other "personalities" who are tangential to the process
> • **uncovering the underlying emotions** the participants feel while going through a given process (fear, uncertainty, frustration, anxiety)
> • **work-arounds and hacks** participants have invented in order to get a process to work better
> • learning how members **establish social capital** with one another
> • understanding **constructs and mental models** shared by group members」

| # | 擅长的问题类型 | 为什么小组比一对一更好 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 长周期流程的回顾 | 互相提醒补全时间线 |
| 2 | 现有方案的"不受欢迎之处" | 集体吐槽降低个体压力 |
| 3 | 底层情绪（恐惧/不确定/挫败/焦虑） | 有人先开口，其他人敢跟进 |
| 4 | 用户自创的变通与"黑客做法" | 会互相攀比、越说越多 |
| 5 | 群体内部如何建立"社交资本" | 只有群体在场才能观察到 |
| 6 | 群体共享的构念与心智模型 | 语言与隐喻在对话中自然浮现 |

> **★★★ 判据：第 4 项「work-arounds and hacks」是实体产品设计最该抓的一类。**
> 用户自制的改装、替代用法、临时修补，直接指向设计的缺口。
> 且这类内容在小组里会被"攀比心理"放大——**是一对一访谈拿不到的密度**。

### 4.6.3 分析要点

> **原文（p93）**：
> 「When analyzing focus group data, **revisit the logic that participants use to arrive at conclusions**. Also, pay particular attention to **stories they tell, the metaphors and analogies they use**, and how they describe their experiences, preferences, and memories. By looking for **recurring topics and themes that produced strong responses**, you can analyze for trends.」

> **★★★ 判据：焦点小组的分析单位是"逻辑/隐喻/强烈反应"，不是"票数"。**
> 因为小组里存在从众，**计票会把群体压力当成共识**。
> 原文的对策是看"哪些话题引发了强反应"和"他们用什么比喻"。

### 4.6.4 两条硬边界

> **原文（p93）**：
> 「Based on these trends, a skilled moderator will be able to **generate a hypothesis** that will usually **require more evaluation and inquiry**. Focus groups should **always be supplemented with well-chosen quantitative and qualitative methods** that continue to investigate attitudes and behaviors, and allow you to **observe people in the actual context** for which your product or service will be used.」

> **原文（p93）**：
> 「Results from focus groups should **never be extrapolated for how the population in its entirety feels**.」

> **★★★★ 判据：两句连续的禁令——必须补方法 + 永不外推。**
> 「always be supplemented」与「never be extrapolated」是全技能语气最强的两句。
> **焦点小组的产出上限是"假设"，不是"结论"。**

### 4.6.5 环境偏差（图注里的一条自我批评）

**图 p93「Bird's-eye view of a focus group」**（原图文字可读）：
图中标注：`video recording device / moderator / name cards / participants / microphone`

> **原文（p94，图注）**：
> 「A common setup for a focus group involves people sitting around a table, with **name cards**, and an **unobtrusive microphone and camera** recording the session. Oftentimes, there is a **side room with flatscreens or a one-way mirror** where observers and stakeholders can watch the session as it plays out. **One of the criticisms of focus groups is the sterile, formal environment** in which the sessions often take place. It is important for researchers to be aware of the **bias that the setting can introduce**, and how it may influence the responses of the participants and, as a result, the analysis of the research data.」

> **★★★ 判据：单面镜＋录像＋姓名牌 = 一套"正式化装置"，它会系统性地把回答推向"得体"。**
> 原文自己把这条列为"对焦点小组的批评"。
> **补救不在本章（原文未给），但识别信号在此：所有回答都很体面、没有人抱怨、没有人说脏话。**

| 方法 | 什么时候用 | 什么时候不能用 | 最少样本量 | 产出物 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **焦点小组** | 探索期生成假设；要群体共享的心智模型/隐喻；要挖变通做法与情绪 | 敏感话题；组内有身份差；要外推总体；要观察真实行为 | **原书未给具体数字**。原文只说「a **small group** of well-chosen people」，**未给每组人数、组数或总人数** | 假设清单、主题与趋势、隐喻与逻辑、工作流变通清单、强反应话题表 |

## 4.9 本章的空白

| 缺什么 | 书中情况 | 该去哪查 |
|---|---|---|
| **日记法的人数与时长** | **只给「relatively small sample」与「day, week, or month」两个定性范围，无具体数字** | 通用用户研究教材；本技能 ch06 汇总 |
| **经验取样的信号频率与条目数** | **完全未给**：未说每天几次、共几天、每人多少条目 | 经验取样法专著（原书 p81 脚注列了 Hektner/Csíkszentmihályi 的 *Experience Sampling Method*） |
| **焦点小组规模** | **只给「a small group」，未给每组人数、组数** | 焦点小组专著（原书 p93 脚注列了 Krueger & Casey、Morgan 两本） |
| **日记的脱落率与激励设计** | **未展开**：未讨论被试中途放弃怎么办、报酬怎么给 | 通用田野研究实务；本书未涉及 |
| **焦点小组的主持技巧** | **只强调「skilled moderator」重要，未给技巧** | Krueger & Casey（原书脚注所引）；本技能不展开主持术 |
| **单面镜环境的补救** | **只指出偏差，未给解法** | 原文未展开；可考虑改用非正式场地（本技能不给未经验证的建议） |
| **文化探针的回收率数据** | **只给定性**「comparable to or exceeding traditional methods」，**无百分比** | 原书未展开；需查 Gaver 等 1999 原文 |
