## 3.1 总纲：观察是一种可以训练的"看"，不是"在旁边待着"

> **原文（p121）**：
> 「A fundamental research skill, observation requires **attentive looking and systematic recording** of phenomena—including people, artifacts, environments, events, behaviors and interactions.」

> **★★★ 判据：观察有两个必须同时满足的条件——"专注地看"与"系统地记"。**
> 只看不记 = 体验；只记不看 = 抄写。原文用 "attentive" 和 "systematic" 两个词卡住两端。

### 3.1.1 形式化程度的两个档

> **原文（p121）**：
> 「For design purposes, observational methods may be characterized by their **degree of formality**, based on the level of **prestructuring** of the observations and recording methods, and their intended use.
> **Semistructured, or casual observation**, typically describes ethnographic methods in the exploratory phase of the design process, where the intent is to collect baseline information through immersion, particularly in territory that is new to the designer. The researcher may have a **guiding set of questions**, but is primarily **observing with an open mind**, and departures from the plan are allowed in response to unexpected events during observation.
> **Structured, or systematic observation**, is formalized by the degree of prestructure imposed upon research sessions, utilizing **worksheets, checklists, or other forms** for codifying behaviors or observed artifacts and events. Structured coding is ideal where environmental or behavioral elements are targeted and **well defined**, often through previous semistructured pilot observations.」

> **★★★★ 判据：结构化观察必须"先做过非结构化观察"才能上。**
> 原文写「often through **previous semistructured pilot observations**」——
> 先探索、后编码。跳过探索直接做编码表，等于用自己猜的类别去量世界。

### 3.1.2 结构化观察的三个陷阱（本节最实用）

> **原文（p121）**：
> 「Examples of prestructure include **regular time intervals** for observations, **predetermined types of interactions or behavioral categories** for coding observations, or **counted successes and errors** when observing use of an interface, prototype, or product. Caution should be exercised to avoid the natural tendency to "**find what you are looking for**," or for artificially assigning observations to preset categories. **The inclusion of an "other" category is therefore recommended.** If sample sizes are large enough, results can be quantified for analysis, otherwise it is common to look for patterns or trends across observations.」

> **★★★ 判据：编码表必须留一个「其他（other）」桶。**
> 这是一条极具体的防御措施：预设类别一定会遇到装不进去的行为，
> 没有 other 桶，观察者就会硬塞——硬塞就是原文说的 "artificially assigning"。

### 3.1.3 事实与推断必须分栏

> **原文（p121）**：
> 「Observations should differentiate between **factual behaviors witnessed, and inferences**, speculating the meaning and motivations behind actions. **Inferences can be verified through interview questions with participants** during or following observations.」

> **★★★ 判据："事实"和"我以为"必须写在两栏，且推断必须回去验证。**
> 原文给的闭环是：观察产生推断 → 用访谈验证推断。
> 这正是 ch02 情境访谈"Interpretation 原则"（当场核对理解）的同一条机制。

### 3.1.4 观察记录的六问（图注还原）

**图 p121「Essential elements of environmental behavior observation」**（原图为照片，其文字标注完整可读，逐项录出）：

> **原文（p122，图注）**：
> 「"Each observation comprises a relationship between **an actor and a significant other** to which the **physical setting** contributes in some way."」

图中六个提问（原图文字）：

| # | 图内问题 |
|---|---|
| 1 | **who?** |
| 2 | **doing what?** |
| 3 | **relationship?** |
| 4 | **with whom?** |
| 5 | **context?** |
| 6 | **setting?** |

> **★★ 判据：六问里"relationship"是唯一容易被漏掉的一栏。**
> 多数观察记录写"谁在做什么、在哪"（who/doing what/context/setting），
> 但漏掉"和谁、什么关系"——而这恰恰决定了很多产品行为（家庭共用、代际差异、协作分工）。

## 3.2 设计人种志（方法 27，p61–p62 PDF）

### 3.2.1 它是"方法族"，不是"一个方法"

> **原文（p61）**：
> 「**Design ethnography** approximates the immersion methods of traditional ethnography, to deeply experience and understand the user's world for **design empathy and insight**.」

> **原文（p61）**：
> 「While true ethnographers may immerse themselves in a culture or specific population for **months or years** at a time, designers are more typically seeking **sufficient information from time-sampled observations** of behaviors. For example, designers conducting immersive ethnographic research may "**sample**" real experiences of participants through the experience sampling method, diary and photo studies, cultural probes, contextual inquiry, and various forms of observation, including modified versions of participant observation.」

> **★★★★ 判据：设计人种志与人类学人种志的差别是"时间尺度"，不是"严谨度"。**
> 原文明确给了对照：人类学家论"月/年"，设计师要的是"time-sampled"（时间抽样）的充分信息。
> **所以"设计人种志做了两周"不是偷工减料，是这个学科分支的定义本身。**
> 但代价是：**它拿不到"长期变迁"类结论**（婚姻、职业、代际），只能拿"当下状态"。

> **★★★ 判据：设计人种志没有专有技术，它是若干方法的组合名。**
> 原文列出的"抽样"手段就是它自己的工具箱：经验取样、日记与照片研究、文化探针、
> 情境访谈、各种观察（含改良版参与式观察）。**这意味着"用人种志"不是一个可执行的动作，
> 必须落到本章其余具体方法上。**

### 3.2.2 一句可以直接当规训用的引文

> **原文（p61，转引 Fetterman）**：
> 「"The ethnographer enters the field with an **open mind, not an empty head**. Before asking the first question in the field, the ethnographer begins with a problem, a theory or model, a research design, specific collection techniques, tools for analysis, and a specific writing style."」

> **★★★★ 判据：open mind ≠ empty head。进现场前必须已有问题、模型、研究设计、采集技术、分析工具、写作体例。**
> 这条直接反驳"先去看看再说"。原文把进场前的六件准备列成了清单，
> **六件里没有一件是"方法新奇"**，全是**结构化的准备**。

### 3.2.3 分析产出

> **原文（p61）**：
> 「Analyses of design ethnography depend on the specific methods used, but are generally focused on a comprehensive view of the users and design territory under investigation, built from deciphering **patterns and themes** emerging from research materials, and articulated in a set of **design implications or guidelines** in preparation for generative research and concept development.」

| 方法 | 什么时候用 | 什么时候不能用 | 最少样本量 | 产出物 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **设计人种志** | 进入陌生领域；要为后续生成式研究打共情底子 | 要长期变迁结论；要可外推的分布；时间只有 2–3 天（不足以做时间抽样） | **原书未给具体数字**。原文只给时间尺度的定性对照（人类学家「months or years」vs 设计师「time-sampled」），**未给天数、也未给人数** | 设计含义（design implications）/ 设计准则清单 + 模式与主题 |

## 3.3 观察（方法 57，p121–p122 PDF）

已在 3.1 展开其形式化程度与陷阱。此处补"用哪种观察"的选择依据。

> **原文（p91）**：
> 「When choosing observational methods, let **appropriateness for the situation and the research question** at hand guide you. For example, fly-on-the-wall might be appropriate when you are observing **public places and activities**, or when you are studying work processes that may be **unduly influenced if interrupted or inconvenienced**. Any time you believe that people may **edit their speech and actions** if observations are intrusive, or the observer's presence will change behaviors, fly-on-the-wall may be a good choice of methods.」

> **★★★★ 判据：选择观察方式的核心变量是"我的在场会不会改变被观察的行为"。**
> 会改变 → 退到飞墙观察；改变不了（或必须追问）→ 上前做参与式/情境访谈。
> 这不是"亲密程度"的偏好，是**效度权衡**。

| 方法 | 什么时候用 | 什么时候不能用 | 最少样本量 | 产出物 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **观察（半结构化）** | 进入新领域、建基线认知、允许跑偏 | 需要量化比较；需要可复现 | **原书未给具体数字**（原文只说"If sample sizes are large enough, results can be quantified"，未给阈值） | 现场笔记、照片、草图、原始视频；模式与主题 |
| **观察（结构化）** | 目标元素已明确（来自先前试点）；要计数/编码 | 元素尚未定义；样本小到无法量化 | **原书未给具体数字**（同上，仅"large enough"） | 编码表、频次表、成功/错误计数 |

## 3.4 参与式观察（方法 59，p125–p126 PDF）

### 3.4.1 定义与人类学渊源

> **原文（p125）**：
> 「**Participant observation** is an immersive, ethnographic method for understanding situations and behaviors through the experience of **membership participation** in an activity, context, culture, or subculture.」

> **原文（p125）**：
> 「Participant observation, or PO, is a foundational method of **anthropology**, adapted for design use. Whereas anthropologists may immerse themselves as participants in a context or culture for extended periods of time, design researchers will typically have a more **time-limited engagement**. However, the intent is the same, for the designer-researcher to **actively participate in the community**, forming deep connections and empathy with the people and the things that are important to them, **experiencing events in the same way as the people** they are studying.」

> **★★★ 判据：参与式观察的不可替代性在于"同感"（experiencing events in the same way），不在"看得更细"。**
> 看得更细可以靠录像；"同样地经历"只能靠亲自在场。这是它贵得有理由的地方。

### 3.4.2 两个参与档：边缘 vs 完全

> **原文（p125）**：
> 「John Zeisel discusses observations from the vantage point of the observer, identifying **two levels of participation**.
> **Marginal participants** blend into an environment as natural observers of an activity or event. For example, researchers may **ride the bus to watch transit commuters**, or attend a soccer game to observe audience behaviors.
> **Full participants** become complete members of a group, subculture, or culture, in extreme cases through **infiltration or a covert role**. This is **not a typical role adopted by design researchers**, because of **ethical considerations**, and the significant investment of time and risks involved… However, designers may take on a membership role in more limited capacities or for shorter durations to approximate full participation, or they may already occupy a role that allows for full participation.」

> **★★★★ 判据：完全参与（含隐蔽）在伦理上被原书判定为"设计研究者非典型角色"。**
> 原文理由两条：**伦理考量** + 时间与风险的投入。这不是"不建议"，是"不典型"。
> **实务含义：设计研究默认走"边缘参与"，要走向完全参与必须补伦理程序。**

> **原文（p125）**：
> 「Researchers engaged in participant observation need to stay vigilant to **retain some measure of objectivity**, and to avoid **undue influence on member behaviors**.」

> **★★★ 判据：参与越深，两个反作用力越强——失去客观性、改变被观察者。**
> 原文给的是"保持警觉"（stay vigilant），没有给量化阈值。这是本方法固有的代价，只能管理不能消除。

### 3.4.3 一个极端案例（可作为"完全参与"的边界标尺）

**图 p125「Full participant observation」**（原图文字可读）：

> **原文（p126，图注）**：
> 「In an exceptional case of full participant observation, industrial designer **Patricia Moore**, at age 26, was **prosthetically altered** to approximate the abilities and appearance of women in their 80's, traversing **more than 100 cities from 1979 to 1982** in an extensive **empathy experiment**.」

> **★★ 判据：这是"完全参与"的极端样本——3 年、100+ 城市、物理改造。**
> 它的价值是给出上限参照：当研究目标是"共情"而非"数据"时，
> 参与的深度可以到这个量级。**日常项目不应拿它当标准，但应拿它当"共情可以被设计到什么程度"的想象边界。**

| 方法 | 什么时候用 | 什么时候不能用 | 最少样本量 | 产出物 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **参与式观察（边缘）** | 要理解群体/亚文化行为；要自然状态下的行为 | 需要代表性与可外推；现场危险或需许可 | **原书未给具体数字** | 现场笔记、事件记录、语言与动机记录、共情叙述 |
| **参与式观察（完全）** | 共情是首要产出；身份可合法获得 | 需隐蔽身份（伦理不通过）；时间不足；医疗等不可进入场景 | **原书未给具体数字**。原文明确"完全参与"因伦理与投入而「not a typical role」 | 深度共情叙述、成员视角的动机与语言 |

## 3.5 影子跟踪（方法 76，p159–p160 PDF）

> **原文（p159）**：
> 「**Shadowing** provides key insight into a participant's activities and decision patterns as the researcher **follows him or her closely throughout his or her daily routines**.」

> **原文（p159）**：
> 「Shadowing is an observational method that involves **tracking someone in his or her role** to experience the situations of his or her daily life or work **in parallel with him or her**, collecting insights through the detailed nuance of **firsthand, real-time exposure**.」

> **★★★ 判据：影子的定义性特征是"平行"（in parallel）与"实时"（real-time）。**
> 事后回访、录像回看都不算影子。**影子的成本就花在这两个词上**。

### 3.5.1 样本量：本节唯一的相关表述

> **原文（p159）**：
> 「Ideally, **several team members** will complete shadowing exercises **across representative users**, to begin crafting a general picture of patterns that describe the population being studied.」

> **★★★ 判据：原文给的是"多人分担"而非"多被试"——这是本节最容易被误读的地方。**
> 「several **team members**」指**研究团队派人**，不是被试人数；
> 「across representative **users**」只说"覆盖代表性用户"，**未给数目**。
> **不能把 "several" 读成样本量。**

### 3.5.2 边界与变体

> **原文（p159）**：
> 「Variations on shadowing include **ride-alongs**—joining professionals such as police officers or Emergency Medical Services (EMS) personnel on their shift work. Obviously in shadowing certain professions or roles, **special clearances may be necessary**, and the risks and dangers associated with the research need to be carefully weighed against the value of the outcomes.」

> **原文（p159）**：
> 「Even in simple shadowing of typical work roles or people in their daily lives, **cooperation needs to be obtained**, and a **respectful distance** maintained to avoid interruption to natural routines, or participant behavior change as a result of being observed. However, as long as these stipulations are kept in mind, shadowing **may involve interactions** with the person being shadowed, asking pertinent questions or engaging in conversation.」

> **原文（p159）**：
> 「Shadowing is **not intended to be a covert research method** used to follow people without their knowledge or consent. However, subtle instances of covert shadowing observations might be completed of people in **public spaces**, for example, following students during class changes to determine common pathways on a college campus, or tracing shoppers in a mall to observe activity patterns.」

> **★★★★ 判据：影子默认公开；隐蔽影子只在"公共空间"这一窄口子成立。**
> 原文的两级：**明示同意（默认）** / **公共场所的隐蔽（例外）**。
> 判据落在"是否公共空间"与"是否涉及个人可识别信息"上，不是"有没有被察觉"。

> **★★ 判据：「respectful distance」不是礼貌用语，它是防偏差手段。**
> 原文把"保持距离"的目的一语道破：避免「professional behavior change as a result of
> being observed」（= Hawthorne 效应，见 3.6）。

| 方法 | 什么时候用 | 什么时候不能用 | 最少样本量 | 产出物 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **影子跟踪** | 日常routine 是研究对象的全部；决策发生在一连串小动作里 | 涉及高度敏感/危险职业；用户不同意贴身；需要跨人群分布 | **原书未给具体数字**。原文：「several **team members**…across representative users」——**团队人数，不是被试数** | 时间轴行为记录、决策点清单、照片/笔记/音频、日常模式图 |

## 3.6 飞墙观察（方法 42，p91–p92 PDF）

### 3.6.1 定义与取舍

> **原文（p91）**：
> 「**Fly-on-the-wall observation** allows the researcher to **unobtrusively** gather information by looking and listening **without direct participation or interference** with the people or behaviors being observed.」

> **原文（p91）**：
> 「Fly-on-the-wall is differentiated from other types of observation, such as participant observation, because it **intentionally removes the researcher from direct involvement**. Fly-on-the-wall attempts to **minimize potential bias or behavioral influences** that might result from engagement with users. However, it may also **reduce the researcher's ability to connect empathically** with people and **probe further into motivations** behind participant behaviors.」

> **★★★★ 判据：这是全章最干净的一对权衡句——抽离降偏差，同时也切断共情与追问能力。**
> 换句话说：**飞墙观察能告诉你"发生了什么"，几乎不能告诉你"为什么"。**
> 想要"为什么"，要么换成参与式，要么事后补访谈（原文 p121 已给"观察后访谈验证推断"的闭环）。

### 3.6.2 两种"局外人"

> **原文（p91）**：
> 「**Secret outsiders** are distant observers, with a vantage point that removes them from participants, minimizing any influence the presence of the researcher or recording equipment may have on behaviors. This form of observation may be **limited in capturing individual nuances of interaction and personal depth**.
> **Recognized outsiders** have the nature of their research and role as observer **made known** to the participants being observed, although like a fly-on-the-wall, they position themselves in a natural and unobtrusive way within the environment under study.」

### 3.6.3 霍桑效应（原文在此给了完整来历）

> **原文（p91）**：
> 「despite best efforts to remain distant and unobtrusive when observing, a disadvantage of this method may still be the tendency for people to **change their behaviors when they know they are being studied or observed**, also known as the "**Hawthorne effect**," stemming from a landmark study where this influence was first identified.」

> **原文（p91，脚注 2）**：
> 「The Hawthorne effect is the recognized influence on the behaviors of people because they are under observation or study. The term originates from a study of worker productivity in response to manipulations of **lighting levels** in the Hawthorne Works of the **Western Electric Company in Chicago, in the 1920s and 1930s**. Productivity was seen to increase **regardless of degree of lighting manipulation** and other workplace changes, and **drop when studies were concluded**, leading to the belief that the intervention itself, or the interest being shown in workers, was responsible for short-term increases in productivity.」

> **★★★ 判据：霍桑效应的原始发现是"无论怎么改都提升，研究结束就回落"——即"被关注"本身有效，与具体改动无关。**
> 这个细节很重要：常被误传为"灯光变亮所以产量升"。
> **原文的版本恰恰说明，任何"我们在关注你"的干预都会污染数据。**

> **原文（p91）**：
> 「Another caution is **perceived partisanship**, if the researcher is associated with particular factions (such as **management**) within the environment or organization being observed.」

> **★★ 判据：旁观者被"站队"时，飞墙观察失效。**
> 在组织里，研究者被当成"管理层的人"，现场就会给你演一遍合规流程。
> 对策不在本章，但**识别信号在这里**：观察到的流程异常整洁、没有人犯错。

| 方法 | 什么时候用 | 什么时候不能用 | 最少样本量 | 产出物 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **飞墙观察** | 公共场所与公共活动；被打断就会被改变的工作流程；人不愿被贴身 | 要动机与缘由（它结构上拿不到）；要个体互动细节；组织内被视为某派系 | **原书未给具体数字** | 行为记录、路径与停留记录、视频/延时摄影、推断栏（待验证） |

## 3.7 行为地图（方法 06，p19–p20 PDF）

> **原文（p19）**：
> 「**Behavioral mapping** is used to **systematically document location-based observations** of human activity, using **annotated maps, plans, video, or time-lapse photography**.」

### 3.7.1 两种地图：以地点为中心 vs 以人为中心

> **原文（p19）**：
> 「**Place-centered mapping** is based on observations of people at a site-specific location. Architectural plans may be used as the underlay for documenting observations, but more commonly researchers will construct their own measured diagram, including the basic space layout and architectural features, signage, and any furniture, fixed or portable items that may affect behaviors or interactions. Behaviors may be **precoded** for ease of recording, for example, with **symbols, numbers, or abbreviations** assigned to anticipate actions such as **standing, sitting, walking, and talking**. Alternately, flexible observations may begin with descriptive note taking and annotations of actions as they are witnessed. Maps created from several observations at different times are typically **aggregated** to indicate summary concentrations of people, place, and feature usage and activities. Common uses of place-centered maps are the analysis of **retail stores and service centers, parks, and other public spaces**, revealing **traffic patterns and key points of interaction** to determine or improve space design or service flow.
> **Individual-centered mapping** follows the travel and activities of a **specific individual or individuals over time and location**. Whereas the emphasis of place-centered mapping is on assessing use of a particular space, the focus of individual-centered mapping is on **learning about people**, for example, their social behaviors and interactions. This method of behavioral mapping is **more intrusive** than place-centered mapping, and therefore **may require the consent of participants**.」

> **★★★ 判据：两种地图的分界线是"要评估空间"还是"要理解人"。**
> 同一个工具，两个目的：前者产出"动线优化"，后者产出"人的社会行为"。
> **选错会同时浪费成本和拿错结论。**

> **★★★ 判据（反偏差操作）：个体地图要"允许被试先习惯被观察"，甚至主动丢弃最初的记录。**
> 原文：「To minimize reactivity, **allow time for the participant to become accustomed to being observed**, which may include **disregarding initial observations** until participants are comfortable.」
> 这是全章唯一给出"可执行的降反应性动作"的地方——**扔掉开头那一段数据**。

### 3.7.2 固有局限与三种补救

> **原文（p19）**：
> 「A noted limitation of the method is that often the **motivations or reasons for behaviors remain unknown** to the observer. In individual-centered mapping, the method can often be **supplemented with interviews or debriefing conversations** to understand more about behaviors. Alternately, in **retrospective mapping**, individuals are asked to **map their paths and behaviors in a space** by simple indications on a floor plan or map, and can simultaneously **reveal their motivations for actions**.」

> **★★★★ 判据：「回溯绘图」是唯一能在同一动作里同时拿到"路径"和"动机"的做法。**
> 研究者画 = 只有路径；被试自己画 = 路径 + 他说出口的理由。
> **代价是准确性让位于解释力**——所以原文用 "alternately"（二选一），不是"叠加"。
