> **UMoD（PDF 索引）PDF p13–p14、p51–p52、p99–p100、p117–p118、p133–p134、p142–p143、p197–p198（14 页，全实读）**
> **Goodman（EPUB）DOC 13 §105–§127、DOC 14 §107–§109、DOC 16 §61、DOC 17 §27–§39、DOC 18 §42–§47、DOC 19 §219–§258、DOC 11 §163**
> 页码口径：**双源并列**

> · Goodman 用 **EPUB 章节号 + 段落号**（`DOC N §M`）。该书为 EPUB，**无可靠 PDF 页序，故不写 pN**

> 每个数字都注明来源与单位，**单位混淆是本领域最高频的错误**。

## 6.1 总纲：两本书给数字的方式完全不同

| 维度 | UMoD（Martin & Hanington 2012） | Goodman（Goodman/Kuniavsky/Moed 2012） |
|---|---|---|
| **给数字的风格** | 偶发、藏在操作细节里 | **成体系**：先讲原理，再给表/区间 |
| **典型单位** | 混合（现场数、事件数、评估者数） | **人数**（参与者/评估者）为主 |
| **是否解释原理** | 几乎不解释 | **解释**（抽样误差、置信度） |
| **可执行度** | 中等（要自己换算） | **高**（可直接照抄招募量） |
| **最大缺口** | 大多数方法不给 | 未覆盖木马、认知走查、参与式观察等 |

> **★★★★ 判据：回答"要多少人"时，先问"这是哪本书、哪个单位"。**
> 本技能实读范围内，**UMoD 只有 3 处给人数**，而 Goodman 仅可用性测试一章就给了完整招募区间。
> **混用两本的数字而不标来源，是本技能最需要防的错误。**

## 6.2 Goodman 给出的样本量（可执行清单）

### 6.2.1 可用性测试：每轮至少 5 人，招募 6~10 人

> **原文（DOC 18 · Ch.11 "Usability Tests" §42）**：
> 「How many participants do you need? Unfortunately, there's no simple answer. **More evaluators means catching more potential problems**—but at a certain point the cost of recruiting and the extra effort needed to run the tests and analyze the results lead to **rapidly diminishing returns**. For many years, the conventional choice, **following a study by Jakob Nielsen, has been at least five evaluators per simple test**. Larger groups still produce useful results, especially for complex tests with many different elements, but **after eight or nine users, you may find that the majority of problems appear sever**[al times over]…」

> **原文（DOC 18 · Ch.11 §45）**：
> 「To offset no-shows, it's a good idea to **schedule a couple of extra people beyond the basic five**. And to make absolutely sure you have enough people, you could **double-book every time slot**. This doubles your recruiting and incentive costs, but it ensures that there's minimal downtime in testing. So, for focused task-based usability testing, you should **recruit from six to ten people to make sure you have at least five evaluators for each round of testing**.」

| 项 | 数值 | 原文依据 |
|---|---|---|
| **每轮最低被试数** | **至少 5** | 「at least five evaluators per simple test」（引 Nielsen） |
| **建议招募数** | **6~10** | 「recruit from six to ten people」 |
| **收益递减点** | **8~9 人之后** | 「after eight or nine users…majority of problems appear several times over」 |
| **备用被试** | 多约 2 人 | 「a couple of extra people beyond the basic five」 |

> **★★★★ 判据：「至少 5 人」是每轮下限，「6~10」是招募量——两者不是一回事。**
> 差额用于抵消 no-show。**把"招募 6~10"直接写成"样本量为 6~10"是误读**：
> 原文明确「to make sure you have **at least five** evaluators for **each round**」。

> **★★★ 判据：「8~9 人后问题重复出现」是原文给的饱和信号——超过这个数边际收益骤降。**
> 注意这是"简单测试"场景；原文紧接着说复杂测试用更大组仍有用。

**排期换算（原文给了每日产能）**：

> **原文（DOC 18 · Ch.11 §47）**：
> 「Schedule interviews at times that are convenient to both you and the participant and **leave at least half an hour between them**… With **60-minute interviews**, this means that you can do **four or five in a single day, and sometimes as many as six**. With **90-minute interviews**, you can do **three or four** evaluators…」

| 单场时长 | 每日产能（原文） |
|---|---|
| **60 分钟** | **4~5 场，有时可达 6 场** |
| **90 分钟** | **3~4 场** |
| 场间缓冲 | **至少 30 分钟** |

> **★★ 判据：这两个数可以直接把"样本量"翻译成"排期天数"。**
> 需要 10 人、每场 60 分钟 → 约 2 天。

### 6.2.2 焦点小组：每组 6~8 人

> **原文（DOC 14 · Ch.7 "Focus Groups" §107）**：
> 「You need to decide two things when determining the scope of the focus groups you're going to run: **how many groups you're going to have** and **how many people you're going to have per group**.」

> **原文（DOC 14 · Ch.7 §109）**：
> 「Likewise, User experience research focus groups are **generally smaller** than their traditional marketing counterparts. Walking through a scenario, demonstrating a prototype, or having people perform a collage or mapping exercise takes time. Asking people for detailed responses—and then explanation of their responses—to these kinds of stimuli takes more time. Encouraging conversation takes even more time. **Reducing the size of the group can help the moderator avoid a rushed and superficial discussion. Six to eight is a good size**, balancing the collection of fine detail with a breadth of perspectives…」

> **★★★★ 判据：UX 焦点小组的每组人数是 6~8，且原文给了理由——不是"人少好管"，是"每项活动都耗时"。**
> 因果链：场景走查 / 原型演示 / 拼贴或制图练习 → 要详细回答还要追问解释 → 还要鼓励对话。
> **人数一多，讨论必然"匆忙而表面"（rushed and superficial）。**

**组数（原文给了一个完整项目的实例）**：

> **原文（DOC 11 · Ch.4 "Research Planning" §163，Company X 研究计划）**：
> 「If no additional usability testing is needed before launch, we will conduct **a series of three focus groups with six to eight users apiece** from **two key segments** of the user base…」

| 项 | 该实例的数值 |
|---|---|
| 组数 | **3 组** |
| 每组人数 | **6~8 人** |
| 覆盖细分 | **2 个**（member researchers / shoppers） |
| 合计规模 | 约 **18~24 人次**（本技能据"3 组 × 6~8 人"的算术，原文未直接写合计） |

> **★★★ 判据："3 组 × 6~8 人"是最可用的一组焦点小组配置，但它出自一个具体项目的计划书，不是通则。**
> 原文把它写在"Company X 研究计划"里，**属案例而非处方**，引用时必须标为实例。

### 6.2.3 田野访问（观察）：每个重要因素 4 人

> **原文（DOC 16 · Ch.9 "Field Visits" §61）**：
> 「First, decide how many people you want to visit. The number will depend on **how much time you have allocated to the research and the resources available**. We think designer **Kim Goodwin's suggestion of four people per important factor** in your analysis of the target audience is a **good starting point for a quick project**. So if you're interested in showering, you might **interview four people who shower a lot and four people who don't like to shower at all**.」

| 项 | 数值 | 依据 |
|---|---|---|
| 每个"重要因素" | **4 人** | Kim Goodwin 的建议，原书认同为"快速项目的起点" |
| 示例 | 爱洗澡的 4 人 + 不爱洗澡的 4 人 = **8 人** | 原文举例 |
| 决定因素 | 时间与资源 | 「depend on how much time…and the resources」 |

> **★★★★ 判据：「每个因素 4 人」是可组合的乘数，不是固定总数。**
> 两个因素 = 8 人。**这与 UMoD p117「每个任务型细分至少 4 人」在数值上一致**——
> 两本独立著作给出同一个 4，这个数字的可信度因此显著提高。

### 6.2.4 日记研究：约 10 人，多招 2 人备份

> **原文（DOC 17 · Ch.10 "Diary Studies" §29）**：
> 「**A typical diary study has about 10 participants**, so a careful researcher will **recruit two extras** if possible.」

> **原文（DOC 17 · Ch.10 §27）**：
> 「A typical study has **10 participants and seven days of activity**. If there are **three diary exercises per day**, you will have **210 entries**.」

| 项 | 数值 | 依据 |
|---|---|---|
| 典型参与人数 | **约 10 人** | 「about 10 participants」 |
| 备用 | **多招 2 人** | 「recruit two extras」 |
| 典型时长 | **7 天** | 「seven days of activity」 |
| 每日练习数 | **3 次** | 「three diary exercises per day」 |
| 条目总量 | **210 条** | 10 × 7 × 3 = 210（原文直接给出该乘积） |

> **★★★★ 判据：日记研究的"工作量"是可算的——10 人 × 7 天 × 3 次 = 210 条待审条目。**
> 原文紧接着警告：「do not underestimate how much time it will take to **monitor** an online diary study」。
> **样本量之外，监控工作量才是日记研究的真实成本。**

**最低条目数的定性判据**：

> **原文（DOC 17 · Ch.10 §36）**：
> 「What is the duration of the study? There should probably be **at least a half dozen diary entries for trends to be observable**（though the exact number, of course, depends on many factors）. **If your topic of interest happens once a week, the study will need to go on for about two months** to be able to measure a change. However, **if there will be a relevant event every day, then a week may be sufficient** to see changes in people's use.」

| 事件频率 | 所需时长（原文） |
|---|---|
| 每周 1 次 | **约 2 个月** |
| 每天都有 | **1 周可能就够** |
| 最低条目数 | **至少 6 条**（a half dozen）才能看出趋势 |

> **★★★ 判据：「至少 6 条/人」是全章最具体的一个"能看出趋势"门槛。**
> 且时长由**事件频率**反推，不是拍脑袋定几周。

### 6.2.5 问卷调查：分层查表（原文给了完整表）

> **原文（DOC 19 · Ch.12 "Surveys" §245）**：
> 「Let's start with the assumption that your entire user population has **10,000** people in it… by asking **300** of them, you can be **95% confident** that the answers you get from them will fall in a **10% range**（"plus or minus 5%," as they say on TV）of what you'd get if you asked everyone.」

> **原文（DOC 19 · Ch.12 §246）**：
> 「Three hundred people is fine, it's not such a big number, but what if your audience has a million people in it? Thirty thousand is a lot of people to have in a survey. Fortunately, the relationship between the number of people in your population and the number of people you need to talk to is **not proportional** in that way. The way the math works, you need to sample only **2000**…」

**表 12.x「Approximate Sample Sizes」（逐字录出，含原表脚注）**：

| Population | Sample Size |
|---|---|
| **1000** | **150** |
| **10,000** | **300** |
| **100,000** | **800** |

> **原表脚注（逐字）**：「∗ Assuming **5% standard error**, **95% confidence**, and **normal variation**.」

> **★ 原文的自谦，必须一并引用（否则会高估该表的精度）**：
> 「These numbers are a **very rough minimum** number of responses necessary for you to be able to extrapolate a survey statistic to the population at large. **Statisticians will chastise me for butchering the statistics involved**（and, believe me, they are butchered）, but the numbers are close enough to make some educated guesses.」

> **★★★★ 判据：这张表的前提写在脚注里——5% 标准误、95% 置信、正态分布。换任一条件，表就不适用。**
> 且作者自陈"非常粗略的最低值"、统计被"砍过"。**引用时必须连这句话一起引。**

**分层规则（最容易漏、后果最严重）**：

> **原文（DOC 19 · Ch.12 §258）**：
> 「To complicate things a bit more, **these numbers don't apply to your whole sample size, but to any single group you're going to be studying**. If you're going to be segmenting your audience into subgroups（such as by gender or by experience or by region）and doing calculations on the subgroups, then **each subgroup needs to be that big**. For example, if your product has 10,000 unique users and you're planning to analyze frequent users separately from infrequent users, **both sets of data have to have at least 300 entries. This means you'll have to have at least 600 responses in your total sample.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：表中数字是「每个待分析子组」的下限，不是总样本量。**
> 两个子组各 300 ⇒ **总量 600**。原文自己把这条算例写出来了。

**预调查（pretest）样本**：

> **原文（DOC 19 · Ch.12 §219）**：
> 「A pretest is run **just like the real thing**, using all the same software, the same recruiting methods, the same data collection methods, and the same analysis methods as the final survey. You should **even make a pretest report** using your report template, drawing conclusions just as you would with the final data. The difference is in terms of sample size. **A pilot sample contains 5–10 responses** from people who are, ideally, representative of the group who will be responding to the survey…」

| 项 | 数值 |
|---|---|
| 预调查样本量 | **5~10 份回答** |
| 要求 | 流程与正式调查**完全相同**，含出报告 |

> **★★★ 判据：预调查的 5~10 人不是"小正式调查"，是"跑通流程"——连报告模板都要走一遍。**
> 原文用「just like the real thing」定义它，差别**只在样本量**。

**不足时的处置规则**：

> **原文（DOC 19 · Ch.12 §382）**：
> 「If the total number of responses in any independent variable group is **less than the minimum sample size** you calculated at the beginning of the survey, **you should not draw any conclusions about that variable**. The results are **insignificant and should be marked as such, or left out of the report entirely**. You can, however, **merge groups to create larger groups** that have the requisite sample size. When you do that, you should **label the new "supergroup" clearly**.」

> **★★★★ 判据：样本不足时的正确动作是"不结论 / 标记不显著 / 合并并明确标注"，不是"小心解释"。**
> 这条给了三条合法出路，堵死了"将就着说"这条。

### 6.2.6 招募筛选：什么叫"合资格"

> **原文（DOC 13 · Ch.6 "Universal Tools: Recruiting and Interviewing" §117）**：
> 「• Have created **at least five eCalendar events** in last month」
> **原文（§127）**：「• Has **at least five scheduled appointments per week**」

> **★★★ 判据：筛选条件是"可核查的行为门槛"，不是"你觉得他像用户"。**
> 这两个例子（每月建 5 个日程事件 / 每周 5 个预约）说明：
> **合格线要写成能对着事实核对的数量。**

> **原文（DOC 13 · Ch.6 §105）**：
> 「• **Prioritize helpful participants.** There is nothing more frustrating than an interview with someone who hates talking or a usability test with someone who won't follow directions. **Including open-ended questions can help the recruiter guess whether a person will be responsive and articulate.**」

> **★★ 判据：招募问卷里放开放题，是为了预判"这人愿不愿意说话"。**
> 这是把"表达能力"变成可筛选项的低成本手段。

## 6.5 没有数字时的四条替代判据（两源合并）

| # | 停止条件类型 | 判据 | 出处 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **饱和型** | 新访谈不再产生新主题即可停 | UMoD p67「common themes and patterns can emerge」；Goodman DOC 18 §42「after eight or nine users…appear several times over」 |
| 2 | **覆盖型** | 每个细分/因素都覆盖到 | UMoD p47「different user segments」、p117「for each segment…at least four」；Goodman DOC 16 §61「four people per important factor」 |
| 3 | **产出量型** | 每人 60~120 条行为 / 50~100 条观察 / 至少 6 条日记 | UMoD p117、p13；Goodman DOC 17 §36 |
| 4 | **阈值型** | 连续 N 次无新失败（RITE 用 5） | UMoD p142–p143 |

> **★★★★ 判据：「覆盖型」优先于「饱和型」——两本书在这一点上一致。**
> UMoD 把顺序写死（先覆盖细分才能综合）；Goodman 的"每因素 4 人"本质也是覆盖。
> **一个细分访 8 人、另一个 0 人，是无效样本，无论总数多大。**

## 6.7 本章概念-论点表（共 12 条）

| # | 概念 | 原文主张 | 支撑案例 | 我的提炼 | 出处 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **无简单答案** | 「there's no **simple answer**」 | 可用性测试人数 | 权威著作的第一句是"没有简单答案"，警惕任何"标准人数" | DOC 18 §42 |
| 2 | **收益递减** | 「**rapidly diminishing returns**」 | 超过 8~9 人 | 样本量的上界由成本决定，不是由严谨度决定 | DOC 18 §42 |
| 3 | **下限 vs 招募量** | 「recruit from six to ten…to make sure you have **at least five**」 | 排期与 no-show | 两个数不能混为一谈 | DOC 18 §45 |
| 4 | **每日产能** | 60 分钟→4~5 场；90 分钟→3~4 场 | 测试排期 | 样本量可换算成天数 | DOC 18 §47 |
| 5 | **人数因耗时而定** | 「**Six to eight is a good size**」 | 焦点小组 | 因为每个活动都要时间，人多必致匆忙表面 | DOC 14 §109 |
| 6 | **每因素 4 人** | 「**four people per important factor**」 | 洗澡习惯访谈 | 可组合乘数，不是固定总数 | DOC 16 §61 |
| 7 | **日记的乘法** | 「10 participants and seven days…**three diary exercises per day**…**210 entries**」 | 在线日记 | 样本量之外，监控工作量才是真实成本 | DOC 17 §27 |
| 8 | **频率反推时长** | 「if…**once a week**…**about two months**」「if…**every day**…**a week**」 | 日记时长 | 时长不是拍的，是事件频率的函数 | DOC 17 §36 |
| 9 | **查表法** | 1000→150 / 10000→300 / 100000→800 | 调查抽样 | 前提在脚注：5% 标准误、95% 置信、正态 | DOC 19 §247–§256 |
| 10 | **分层陷阱** | 「these numbers don't apply to your whole sample size, but to **any single group**」 | 两子组⇒600 | 最高频的误用点 | DOC 19 §258 |
| 11 | **不足即不结论** | 「you should **not draw any conclusions** about that variable」 | 子组偏小 | 三条合法出路，堵死"将就着说" | DOC 19 §382 |
| 12 | **双源印证** | UMoD「**at least four** people」（p117）／Goodman「**four people per important factor**」（DOC 16 §61） | 心理模型图 + 田野访问 | 两书独立给出同一数字，可信度最高 | 双源 |
