## 8.1 总纲：交付物不是"研究结果的包装"，是"设计的长期参照"

> **原文（p133）**：
> 「Personas are then used as a **lasting human reference by teams throughout all phases of the project**.」

> **原文（p197）**：
> 「The hands-on, inclusive design activity that brings **all decision makers together** can go a long way in ensuring that the user journey map becomes a **living document** for the organization.」

> **★★★★ 判据：交付物的成败标准是"它还在被用吗"（living），不是"它做得漂亮吗"。**
> 原文两处独立强调持续性：画像「**lasting** human reference」、旅程图「**living** document」。
> **挂在墙上再没人翻的画像，与没做过研究等价。**

## 8.2 人物画像（方法 63，p133–p134 PDF）

### 8.2.1 它为什么存在：三种失败方案的对照

原文没有直接定义画像，而是先否定三种替代品：

> **原文（p133）**：
> 「For user-centered design, you need to understand people. However, **attempting to design for everyone results in unfocused or incoherent solutions**, so some level of **consolidation** is needed. **Surveys and quantitative methods tend to result in abstracted and dehumanized caricatures.** **Traditional market segments don't work because they describe demographic populations rather than aggregates of behavior.** Crafted from information collected from **real users through sound field research**, personas provide an ideal solution by capturing **common behaviors in meaningful and relatable profiles**.」

| 替代方案 | 原文的否定理由 | 失败形态 |
|---|---|---|
| **为所有人设计** | 「results in **unfocused or incoherent** solutions」 | 什么都照顾，结果什么都不突出 |
| **靠问卷/定量** | 「**abstracted and dehumanized caricatures**」 | 有数据没人味，团队无法共情 |
| **传统市场细分** | 「describe **demographic populations rather than aggregates of behavior**」 | 按年龄收入分，但同一年龄的人做事方式完全不同 |

> **★★★★ 判据：「人口统计不是行为」——这是画像存在的根本理由。**
> 原文的对照极锋利：市场细分描述「demographic populations」，
> 画像描述「**aggregates of behavior**」。
> **判据化：如果两个画像的差别只是年龄和收入，那它不是画像，是市场细分。**

### 8.2.2 怎么造：从行为模式聚类

> **原文（p133）**：
> 「Once you have enough information collected to describe several users, **look for behavior patterns and themes that constitute commonalities**. To arrive at consolidated descriptions, it may be useful to employ **affinity diagramming or similar methods**. The **similarities across users can then be clustered** to begin forming **synthesized, aggregate archetypes**. **Personas should be limited in number, for example, three to five for any given project**, to maintain a **manageable design focus** and **avoid targeting extreme outliers**.」

> **★★★★ 判据：画像是亲和图的产物——不是"访谈完了坐下来编"。**
> 原文指明路径：行为模式 → 亲和图聚类 → 聚合原型。
> **这条把 ch07 的分析与本章的交付焊死了：没有 ch07 的聚类，就没有合法的画像。**

> **★★★ 判据：3~5 个/项目的两个理由——「保持焦点」与「避开极端离群者」。**
> 注意第二条：**画像不是用来覆盖边缘个案的**。极端用户重要，但不该进画像集。

### 8.2.3 画像的形态（原文给了具体构成）

> **原文（p133）**：
> 「Personas are typically presented in **page-length or shorter** descriptions, providing a **name** for the person, a **photograph** (**use stock photography to avoid connection to a real identity**) or **sketch**, and a **narrative story** describing in detail **key aspects of his or her life situation, goals, and behaviors relevant to the design inquiry**. **Supplementary images** may be used to add a compelling impression of the persona lifestyle, including **typical spaces, objects, and activities**.」

| # | 构成要素 | 原文要求 | 为什么必要 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **长度** | 「page-length or shorter」 | 一页以内才有人读 |
| 2 | **姓名** | 「a **name** for the person」 | 便于团队日常引用 |
| 3 | **照片或草图** | 「**use stock photography** to avoid connection to a real identity」 | **避免指向真实个人（伦理 + 防止把个案当代表）** |
| 4 | **叙事故事** | 覆盖「life situation, **goals**, and **behaviors** relevant to the design inquiry」 | 三要素：处境、目标、行为 |
| 5 | **补充图像** | 「typical **spaces, objects, and activities**」 | 强化生活方式印象 |

> **★★★★ 判据：图片必须用「图库照片」（stock photography），不能用真实被试的照片。**
> 原文给了明确理由：「to avoid connection to a **real identity**」。
> **这是硬规则**：用真实被试照片做画像，等于把一个具体的人包装成"代表"。

> **★★★ 判据：叙事故事必须覆盖「目标」与「行为」，且都要「与设计探究相关」。**
> 「relevant to the design inquiry」是限定词——**画像不是人物小传，多余的生活细节是噪音。**

### 8.2.4 画像的四种用法

> **原文（p133）**：
> 「They are helpful in **developing, discussing, and presenting** product or system design in the **definition and ideation phase**. They are also used to **check scenarios of use, highlighting positive experiences and potential breakpoints**. In addition to personas being a useful **working tool within the design team (including distributed teams)**, personas provide a **persuasive human reference when communicating research summaries and scenarios to clients**.」

| # | 用法 | 时机 | 原文关键词 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 开发/讨论/呈现设计 | 定义与构思期 | 「definition and ideation phase」 |
| 2 | **检查使用场景** | 方案成形期 | 「**check scenarios of use**」「highlighting **positive experiences and potential breakpoints**」 |
| 3 | 团队内部工作参照（含分布式团队） | 全程 | 「working tool within the design team（**including distributed teams**）」 |
| 4 | 对客户沟通的说服性人证 | 汇报期 | 「**persuasive human reference**」 |

> **★★★★ 判据：第 2 项"检查场景"是画像最被低估的功能——它是情景推演的裁判。**
> 原文：「highlighting **positive experiences and potential breakpoints**」。
> **操作化：每提出一个方案，用每个画像走一遍场景，标出"顺"和"断"的位置。**

### 8.2.5 溯源与红线

> **原文（p133，脚注 1）**：
> 「The seminal text that first introduced personas to the world of interaction design is: Cooper, Alan. *The Inmates Are Running the Asylum*… Personas originated from **Alan Cooper's need to synthesize and communicate design research**, for software development.」

> **原文（p133，脚注 2）**：
> 「Personas may be **entirely fictionalized**, but **this is not recommended unless part of an intentional approach**. For example, Gaver et. al. propose a method of "**design for extreme characters**" (the drug dealer, the fictitious Pope) with **exaggerated emotional attitudes**, to expand creative possibilities…」

> **★★★★ 判据：全虚构画像"不推荐，除非是有意为之的方法"。**
> 原文用「not recommended **unless part of an intentional approach**」——留了口子但设了门槛。
> **门槛是什么？** 原文举的 Gaver 例子是"极端角色"法，**目的明确是"扩展创意可能性"**，不是"代表用户"。
> **所以：虚构画像可以用于发散创意，不能用于代替用户证据。**

| 方法 | 什么时候用 | 什么时候不能用 | 最少样本量 | 产出物 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **人物画像** | 要把行为模式固化成团队长期参照；要检查场景；要对客户沟通 | 只做了定量/人口统计；样本不足形成不了行为簇；只需要一次性的结论 | **3~5 个画像/项目**（p133，产出数）。造画像的访谈量**原书未给**；唯一实证配比是 **35 访→6 画像**（p197，约 5.8:1，单案例） | 一页以内画像（姓名、图库照片/草图、叙事故事：处境/目标/行为、补充图像） |

## 8.3 用户旅程图（方法 95，p197–p198 PDF）

### 8.3.1 定义与核心价值

> **原文（p197）**：
> 「A **user journey map** is a **visualization of the experiences** people have when interacting with a product or service, **so that each moment can be individually evaluated and improved**.」

> **原文（p197）**：
> 「A user journey map tells a story about an individual's **actions, feelings, perceptions, and frame of mind**—including the **positive, negative, and neutral** moments—as he or she interacts with a **multichannel product or service over a period of time**.」

> **★★★★ 判据：「每个瞬间可被单独评估」是旅程图的定义性功能——它把连续体验切成了可操作的单元。**
> 原文的因果很明确：「so that each moment can be **individually evaluated and improved**」。
> **如果一张旅程图画完，团队说不出"哪个瞬间最该修"，它就只是流程图。**

> **原文（p197）**：
> 「By documenting the series of events and interactions that a person experiences, the user journey map can **shift an organization's focus from an operational, system-centered view to the larger context** in which products and services are used in the real world. It also helps teams **pinpoint distinct moments that elicit strong emotional reactions and are ripe for redesign and improvement**.」

> **★★★ 判据：旅程图的首要作用是把组织视角从"系统中心"扳到"真实情境"。**
> 原文用「shift…from an operational, system-centered view」。
> **这是组织政治的活，不是绘图的活**——所以旅程图必须进决策会议，不能只进设计文档。

### 8.3.2 必须与画像成对

> **原文（p197）**：
> 「Solid user journey maps are usually created **alongside, or immediately following, personas and scenarios** documents. **All three deliverables should be heavily informed by direct contact with the customers** who use the product or service. **Rich, qualitative data that is a result of primary research is the only way to be sure to craft deep, compelling narratives** that reflect people's actual needs, feelings, and perceptions that occur **before, during, and after** product interactions.」

> **★★★★ 判据：画像、场景、旅程图是三件套，且都必须由一手研究喂养。**
> 原文用「the **only** way」形容一手定性数据——这是全章语气最强的一句。
> **推论：用二手报告或团队脑补画旅程图，画得再漂亮也是无效交付物。**

> **原文（p197）**：
> 「**Each map should represent a journey specific to a persona**, as well as **include a description of the persona**.」

> **★★★ 判据：一画像一旅程图，不可合并。**
> 原文理由：「as each persona will have **different tasks and goals**, and will experience **different breakdowns and successes** on their journey」。

### 8.3.3 内容要求（可当检查表）

> **原文（p197）**：
> 「For the benefit of the internal team, the map should also **articulate the event it illustrates**: this can be either an **entire relationship life cycle**, or can be **limited to a specific scenario**. The map should be an **honest representation of an experience**, and include moments of **indecision, confusion, frustration, as well as delight and closure**. **Multiple maps will need to be created for multiple personas**, as each persona will have different tasks and goals, and will experience different breakdowns and successes on their journey.」

| # | 内容要求 | 原文表述 | 检查问题 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **界定范围** | 「entire relationship life cycle」或「limited to a specific scenario」 | 这张图覆盖的是全生命周期还是单个场景？（必须二选一写清） |
| 2 | **标注画像** | 「include a description of the persona」 | 图上有没有写这是谁的旅程？ |
| 3 | **覆盖三个时态** | 「**before, during, and after** product interactions」 | 有没有画接触前和接触后？ |
| 4 | **诚实包含负面** | 「**indecision, confusion, frustration**, as well as **delight and closure**」 | 有没有画犹豫、困惑、挫败？只有高光的是宣传册 |
| 5 | **正负中三态** | 「the **positive, negative, and neutral** moments」 | 中性时刻有没有被漏掉？ |

> **★★★★ 判据：「诚实」（honest representation）被原文写成了技术要求，而且点名要包含犹豫、困惑、挫败。**
> 这是对"作品集式旅程图"的直接否定：**只画顺畅路径的图，是失真。**

### 8.3.4 用法：贴墙、可涂写、共创

> **原文（p197）**：
> 「The early versions of the document can then serve as a **springboard for discussion** on the team. **Print out an early version of the map on large-format paper, pin it up on a board, and hold a review session where everybody can get up close to the document and mark it up with questions, ideas, and suggestions for improvement.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：早期版本要"打印大图、钉上墙、让大家上手涂写"——这是让它变成 living document 的操作手段。**
> 注意原文说的是「**early versions**」——不是定稿后展示，而是**半成品就上墙**。
> **判据：等图画完美了再给大家看，就失去了"共创"的机会。**

### 8.3.5 完整案例（p197，与 ch06 同一案例）

> **原文（p198，Symetra 案例）**：
> 「In 2010, **POP**, a Seattle-based digital agency, was engaged by **Symetra Financial** to craft a multi-year digital strategy centered on the Symetra.com website. Symetra's products—employee benefits, annuities, and life insurance—are somewhat **intangible** and are often perceived as **complex**. Due to the nature of the products, as well as **regulatory requirements of the insurance industry**, each product may have **several similar variations** available to different clients and demographics in different states… POP conducted **35 phone interviews** with Symetra customers and sales representatives as part of the project's research phase. Interviews resulted in the creation of **six personas and journeys** that reflected the diversity of Symetra's user base and online and offline behaviors. Through these deliverables, POP and Symetra were able to **identify the types of content, features, and functionality that would effectively support each persona** and provide greater workflow efficiencies via the refreshed website.」

> **★★★ 判据：这个案例的产出是"每种画像需要什么内容/功能"，不是"画像长什么样"。**
> 「identify the **types of content, features, and functionality** that would effectively support each persona」。
> **这是画像+旅程图的正确落点：从描述人，推到"给人配什么"。**

| 方法 | 什么时候用 | 什么时候不能用 | 最少样本量 | 产出物 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **用户旅程图** | 体验横跨多渠道/多时点；要指认"哪个瞬间该修"；要把组织视角扳到用户侧 | 只有单点接触（画不出旅程）；无一画像（无从归属）；无一手定性数据 | **原书未给具体数字**。仅给"一画像一图"的结构要求；案例为 35 访→6 图（p197） | 大幅旅程图（含画像描述、范围界定、接触前中后、正负中三态、情绪强点标记）、可涂写版本 |

## 8.4 任务分析（方法 84，p175–p176 PDF）

### 8.4.1 定义与传统来源

> **原文（p175）**：
> 「**Task analysis** breaks down the **constituent elements of a user's work flow**, including **actions and interactions, system response, and environmental context**.」

> **原文（p175）**：
> 「Traditional task analysis holds a **scientific view of human interactions within systems**, isolating **key elements of human behavior, product or system behaviors and responses to human actions, the provision of system feedback, and the context in which tasks occur**. Such analyses are usually expressed formally in **flowcharts or other structured visuals**, indicating **tasks and subtasks, key decision points, and human-system response cycles**. This analytic process stems from **time and motion studies in industrial engineering, scientific management, and early human factors**.」

> **★★★ 判据：任务分析出身于"时间与动作研究"——它的基因是"拆解与计时"，不是"理解意义"。**
> 原文点名来源：「time and motion studies in industrial engineering, scientific management, and early human factors」。
> **这解释了它为什么长成流程图：它的目的是"找出多余的步骤"，不是"听用户讲故事"。**

### 8.4.2 设计师用的"宽定义"

> **原文（p175）**：
> 「While this level of formal task analysis can play a critical role in understanding user behaviors in context, designers will more typically employ a **broader definition** of the method, incorporating a **blend of qualitative and quantitative approaches** to gain insight into user and task processes. **Task** in this sense is not isolated to mean **completion of a specific job**, but rather encompasses a **broad definition of any physical actions and mental processes as activities used to achieve goals, and information flows within the system environment**. Task analysis is useful for **all design disciplines**, with relevant applications ranging from **navigation of print documents and software device interactions to wayfinding in the built environment**.」

> **★★★★ 判据：「任务」不只是"完成一件工作"，而是"为达目标的身体动作与心智过程 + 系统内的信息流"。**
> 这个宽定义把任务分析从软件扩展到了**实体产品与空间导视**——原文明确列举
> 「navigation of print documents」「wayfinding in the built environment」。

### 8.4.3 与情境访谈的分界（本节最实用的一句）

> **原文（p175）**：
> 「Task analysis is **similar to contextual inquiry**, because they employ many of the same methods, including **observation and interviews**. However, **the difference is in focus**, with contextual inquiry being **more inclusive of general aspects of user behavior, decision making and interactions within the wider context**, and task analysis **concentrating solely on the task at hand**.」

| 对比项 | 情境访谈（20） | 任务分析（84） |
|---|---|---|
| 采集手段 | 观察 + 访谈 | **相同**（观察 + 访谈） |
| **焦点** | 「**general aspects** of user behavior…within the **wider context**」 | 「**solely on the task at hand**」 |
| 边界 | 越宽越好 | 越窄越好 |

> **★★★★ 判据：两者手段相同、焦点相反——这是最容易混淆的一对方法。**
> **判据化：如果你在追问"这个任务之外还有什么"，你在做情境访谈；如果你在追问"这一步到底怎么完成的"，你在做任务分析。**

### 8.4.4 具体要看什么（可直接当访谈提纲）

> **原文（p175）**：
> 「Interviews and observations specifically target **user options, tools available, and choices made; decision points; identification of common mistakes and corrections; process inputs and outputs; frequency and importance of the tasks; and risks of failure**.」

| # | 要采集的内容 | 原文 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 可选方案与可用工具、做过的选择 | 「user options, tools available, and choices made」 |
| 2 | 决策点 | 「decision points」 |
| 3 | 常见错误与纠正方式 | 「common mistakes and corrections」 |
| 4 | 流程的输入与输出 | 「process inputs and outputs」 |
| 5 | 任务频次与重要性 | 「frequency and importance of the tasks」 |
| 6 | 失败风险 | 「risks of failure」 |

> **★★★ 判据：第 3 项「常见错误与纠正」是产品设计最直接的改进输入。**
> 它同时对应 ch08 §8.5 认知走查要发现的"每一步会不会失败"。

### 8.4.5 两种拆解法 + 一个轻量替代

> **原文（p175）**：
> 「Task analysis can be deciphered using **task decomposition**, breaking the task down into component actions, and **Hierarchical Task Analysis (HTA)**, identifying tasks and subtasks, **categorizing them, and checking the accuracy of the model**. Actions in task decomposition can be usefully organized by categories, such as **purpose, cues, objects, method, and options**. Putting the actions that constitute a task into a hierarchy ordinarily results in the familiar **tree diagram or other forms of flowcharting**, which can then be **verified by walk-through tests by those familiar with the task**. A **simpler yet less thorough version** of task analysis can be completed using **sticky notes** to identify and organize basic task actions, **similar to the process of constructing affinity diagrams**.」

| 路径 | 做法 | 严格度 | 原文评价 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **任务分解（task decomposition）** | 拆成组成动作，按 **purpose / cues / objects / method / options** 分类 | 中 | 原文给了这五个分类词 |
| **层级任务分析（HTA）** | 识别任务与子任务、分类、**校验模型准确性** | 高 | 产出树状图/流程图，并由熟悉任务者走查验证 |
| **便签法** | 用便签识别与组织基本任务动作 | 低 | 「**simpler yet less thorough**」 |

> **★★★★ 判据：HTA 的"校验"环节不可省——「checking the accuracy of the model」＋「verified by walk-through tests by those familiar with the task」。**
> 任务分析的最大风险是**研究者自己编了一套流程**。
> 原文给的校验方式是让人**走一遍**（walk-through），这与 §8.5 认知走查同源。

> **★★★ 判据：便签法被原文明确降级为「更简单但不那么彻底」——用在时间紧时，但不能谎称做了 HTA。**

| 方法 | 什么时候用 | 什么时候不能用 | 最少样本量 | 产出物 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **任务分析** | 流程有明显步骤可拆；要找出多余步骤与失败风险；要为界面/器件/导视做结构依据 | 任务边界模糊（此时用情境访谈）；要理解意义与情绪；用户无法描述步骤（需先观察） | **原书未给具体数字** | 任务分解表（purpose/cues/objects/method/options）、HTA 树状图、流程图、决策点清单、错误与纠正清单、失败风险表 |

## 8.5 认知走查（方法 13，p33–p34 PDF）

### 8.5.1 定义：它评的是"提示的顺序"，不是"界面好不好看"

> **原文（p33）**：
> 「**Cognitive walkthrough** is a method that evaluates whether the **order of cues and prompts** in a system **reflect the way people cognitively process tasks and anticipate "next steps"** of a system.」

> **原文（p33）**：
> 「The cognitive walkthrough is a **usability inspection method** that evaluates a system's **relative ease-of-use in situations where preparatory instruction, coaching, or training of the system is unlikely to occur**. In these situations—when a person must **actively engage with an interface to know what to do next**, rather than relying on **preexisting knowledge** of the system—each step of the interaction with the system can be assessed as a step that either **moves the individual closer to or further from his goal**.」

> **★★★★ 判据：认知走查的适用条件是"没人教、只能自己摸索"——这是它的定义性前提。**
> 原文写死了：「in situations where **preparatory instruction, coaching, or training**…is **unlikely to occur**」。
> **有培训、有说明书、有专人指导的产品，用它价值有限。**

> **原文（p33）**：
> 「Cognitive walkthroughs are particularly well suited for evaluating "**walk-up-and-use**" systems that are primarily **audio- or display-based**—such as **ATMs, automated parking garage or subway ticketing systems, and automated voice-response phone systems**.」

> **★★★ 判据：「walk-up-and-use」（走近就用）是这类系统的名字——首次使用者、无学习机会。**
> 原文的例子全是公共自助设备：ATM、停车/地铁售票机、自动语音电话系统。
> **对实体产品设计的直接映射：任何"第一次上手就得会用"的产品都在此列**（自助终端、家电面板、公共设施、包装开启方式）。

### 8.5.2 四个问题（全章最可执行的一段）

> **原文（p33）**：
> 「The method's focus on how people solve problems requires that evaluators ask the **same four learning theory-based questions** for **each step** in the action sequence.
> • Will users **want to produce** whatever effect the action has?
> • Will users **see the control** (button, menu, label, etc.) for the action?
> • Once users find the control, will they **recognize that it will produce the effect** that they want?
> • After the action is taken, will users **understand the feedback** they get, so they can confidently continue on to the next action?」

| # | 问题 | 检查的心理环节 | 对应的设计缺陷 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **用户想不想要这个效果？** | 动机 | 步骤本身是设计者需要、用户不需要的 |
| 2 | **用户看不看得见这个控件？** | 感知 | 可视性不足（对比诺曼的"可视性"原则） |
| 3 | **用户知不知道这个控件会产生他要的效果？** | 匹配/理解 | 概念模型错误、标签误导 |
| 4 | **用户做完知不知道成功了？** | 反馈 | 反馈缺失或不可解读（对应诺曼的"评估鸿沟"） |

> **★★★★ 判据：四问的顺序就是"行动七阶段"的执行-评估链：动机→感知→匹配→反馈。**
> 第 1 问是**目标层**（愿不愿意），第 2–3 问是**执行层**（找得到、看得懂），
> 第 4 问是**评估层**（知道成了没有）。
> **四问中任何一问失败，那一步就是缺陷步骤。**

### 8.5.3 任务必须从用户视角写

> **原文（p33）**：
> 「A series of **representative tasks** should be selected, **all written from the user's vantage point**, and outlined in a **believable sequence of action steps**. The method then sets out to **critique each step in the action sequence** and evaluate **whether it is the right step at the right time**. The success of the interface can be judged based on whether the **system feedback either helps or hinders users to achieve their goals**.」

> **★★★ 判据：「from the user's vantage point」＋「believable sequence」是任务书写的两条硬要求。**
> 写"点击设置菜单→选择语言"是从系统视角写；写"我想把界面换成中文"是从用户视角写。

### 8.5.4 与可用性测试的关系（关键边界）

> **原文（p33）**：
> 「Because it **cannot be assumed that users will be available for testing every step of the way** along the iterative design process, **expert reviews like cognitive walkthroughs ensure better use of participants' time**. However, because cognitive walkthroughs and usability testing **tend to uncover different classes of design issues and usability problems**, **using them together—rather than in lieu of one another—is always recommended**.」

> **★★★★ 判据：「different classes of design issues」——两者发现的是不同类别的问题，所以不能互相替代。**
> 原文的用词是「**rather than in lieu of one another**」（而不是互相取代），且用「**always** recommended」。
> **这决定了本技能与 `usability-testing` 技能的分工：认知走查是"专家先扫一遍"，可用性测试是"用户实测"，顺序上前后衔接。**

### 8.5.5 一个完整走查实例（图注还原，极具体）

**图 p33「An example of a cognitive walkthrough task」**（原图文字完整，逐项录出）：

> **原文（p34，图内）**：
> 「**Task**: **Pay for 2 hour parking with a credit card**
> **Action 1**: **Select 2 hour option**
> 1. Will the users be trying to achieve whatever effect Action 1 has?
> 2. Will the user see the correct action is available?
> 3. Will the user associate the correct action with the effect (s)he is trying to achieve?
> 4. If the correct action is taken, will the user understand that progress is being made toward the desired solution?」

图内界面文字（一台真实停车缴费机的面板，逐项录出）：

| 类别 | 图内文字 |
|---|---|
| 费率与时段 | `$2/HR` `8 a.m-8 p.m.` `Monday-Saturday` |
| 支付方式 | `Credit Card` / `Coins` / `Insert credit card and remove quickly` / `Take receipt` |
| 硬币操作 | `1. Insert coins to add time` / `1 hr` `1 hr` `1 hr` |
| 刷卡操作 | `1. Insert credit card` / `1a. Press pay button` / `2. Press green button to validate transaction and print receipt` |
| 显示与按键 | `01010 0101 0101` / `John Q Public 11/16` / `Cancel` / `Print Receipt` / `Español` |

> **★★★ 判据：这个实例把"四问"落到了具体的一步上——Action 1 是"选 2 小时"。**
> 四问依次检验：想不想付 2 小时？看不看得见选项？知不知道这是"付 2 小时"？按了之后知不知道成了？
> **注意界面上的混乱：硬币流程编号是"1."，刷卡流程也是"1."和"1a."，"1a. Press pay button" 与后面的"2. Press green button"重复表达同一件事——这是典型的"顺序提示不反映用户心智"。**

| 方法 | 什么时候用 | 什么时候不能用 | 最少样本量 | 产出物 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **认知走查** | walk-up-and-use 系统；首次使用、无人指导；迭代期想省用户时间；界面步骤可枚举 | 需要培训的产品；步骤无法枚举（探索型任务）；要发现视觉美感问题 | **原书未给具体数字**。注意：**它不需要用户**（评审者是团队/专家），故"用户样本量"不适用；**评估者人数本书亦未给**（对比 p99 启发式评估给了 3~5 人） | 逐步四问评估表、失败步骤清单、改进建议 |

## 8.7 本章的空白
