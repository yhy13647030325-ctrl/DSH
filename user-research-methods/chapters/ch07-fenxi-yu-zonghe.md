## 7.1 总纲：分析的起点是"材料还在人脑里"

> **原文（p13）**：
> 「As long as research data is stored as **tacit knowledge in people's minds** or **buried in interview transcripts**, teams will experience difficulty synthesizing what has been observed and learned.」

> **★★★★ 判据：分析的第一步不是"思考"，是"外化"（externalize）。**
> 原文把两种失败状态点得很准：材料在**人脑里**（默会）或**埋在逐字稿里**（未提取）。
> 这两种状态下的"讨论"都不是分析，是各说各话。

> **原文（p13）**：
> 「Affinity diagramming helps designers capture research-backed insights, observations, concerns, or requirements on **individual sticky notes**, so that the **design implication of each can be fully considered on its own**. Notes are then **clustered based on affinity**, which form into **research-based themes**.」

> **★★★ 判据：一张便签只放一条，是为了"让每条含义被单独考虑"。**
> 原文的理由是「so that the design implication of each can be **fully considered on its own**」。
> **把两件事写在一张便签上，就破坏了整个方法的前提。**

## 7.2 亲和图（方法 03，p13–p14 PDF）

### 7.2.1 核心是"自下而上"（inductive）

> **原文（p13）**：
> 「In both variations, affinity diagramming is an **inductive exercise**—which means that **instead of grouping notes in predefined categories**, the work is done **from the bottom up**, by first clustering specific, small details into groups, which then give rise to the **general and overarching themes**.」

> **★★★★ 判据：归纳（inductive）＝ 先聚细节、后长主题；不是先定类别、再分装。**
> 这条判据是对 ch03 §3.1.2「其他桶」的呼应，也是 ch07 全章的总纲：
> **类别必须从数据里长出来，不能从研究者的脑子里压下去。**

### 7.2.2 便签的四层颜色结构（图注还原，极具体）

**图 p14「亲和图便签分层」**（原图文字完整可读，逐项录出）：

| 便签颜色 | 原文定义 |
|---|---|
| **黄色（Yellow）** | 「represent a **single observation, insight, concern, or requirement firmly rooted in research data**. These are the **building blocks** of the affinity diagram.」 |
| **蓝色（Blue）** | 「describe **aspects of an issue** revealed by **clusters of yellow notes**.」 |
| **粉色（Pink）** | 「describe **specific issues within an area of concern**.」 |
| **绿色（Green）** | 「describe an **overarching area of concern within the work practice**.」 |

> **★★★★ 判据：四层颜色的价值在于"每一层都标注了它是从哪一层长出来的"。**
> 黄→（聚成簇）→蓝→粉→绿，**层级关系是被记录的，不是被记忆的**。
> 这使亲和图可追溯：**任何一条绿色结论，都能倒推回若干张黄色原始观察。**

**图内还给了具体的黄色便签实例（原文编号格式可直接照用）**：

> **原文（p14，图内便签）**：
> 「**U2 221** prints calendar several times a day and hangs them next to her computer」
> 「**U7 743** transfers meetings from email to wall calendar」
> 「**U3 351** likes getting an email with tasks rather than a phone call so she can print it」
> 「**U3 302** likes the prioritization format in her day planner」
> 「**U5 518** makes a report for group with day's hot tasks every day」
> 「**U1 38** checks things off her to-do list as she finishes them」
> 「**U5 523** has his email set so only urgent mail is automatically opened」
> 「**U1 12** keeps her inbox behind her so she won't be interrupted」

> **★★★ 判据：便签编号「U2 221」是「被试号 + 原始记录序号」——可回溯的锚。**
> 原文在正文里写明了这个要求：「be sure that notes **reference their original interview transcript**,
> in case a question comes up about it」。
> **没有这个编号，亲和图就退化成"便利贴艺术"。**

**这张图同时暴露了三个上层主题**（图内文字）：
`organizing my information` / `show me what I have to do` / `don't interrupt me with non-critical stuff`

> **★★ 判据：三个主题全部是"第一人称"写的（我/别打断我）。**
> 这与人物的表述方式一致——**主题句用用户的口吻，比用研究者的名词更抗"过度概括"。**

### 7.2.3 两个变体（用途完全不同）

**变体 A：情境访谈的亲和图**

> **原文（p13）**：
> 「**Affinity Diagramming for Contextual Inquiry**: Once researchers have conducted interviews of typical workers from **four to six different work sites**, there should be enough representative data to complete an affinity diagram. Before the affinity diagramming session, record on average **50–100 observations of each person interviewed**. Each observation should be on its own sticky note (be sure that notes reference their original interview transcript, in case a question comes up about it). Once created, notes are posted on a wall that is **covered in sheets of large-format paper** (which allows the affinity diagram to be **moved**, if necessary), and the team can begin the rigorous process of **interpreting notes and considering the underlying significance of each**. Notes that share a **similar intent, problem, or issue**—or that share an affinity—are clustered together. Out of this work, **a story emerges about people, their tasks, and the nature of their problems**.」

> **★★★ 判据：贴在大幅纸上而不是直接贴墙，是为了"整张图可以被搬走"。**
> 这个细节说明亲和图是一份**可长期在场的实物**，不是一次性研讨。

**变体 B：可用性测试的亲和图（颜色 = 被试）**

> **原文（p13）**：
> 「**Affinity Diagramming for Usability Tests**: Prior to each usability test session, the research team agrees on a **different color sticky note for each participant**. Once the usability test is in progress, the team (which **can include stakeholders, developers, designers, and other researchers**) watches the evaluation from an observation room. As the participant talks through the tasks, the team captures **specific observations and quotes** on the sticky notes, and posts them on a wall or whiteboard. Over the course of a few usability tests, **common issues and problems in the interface will emerge**. The categories that have usability issues will **show many colored sticky notes**—indicating several people experienced the same problem. **Fixes and priorities to the interface can then be determined: whatever aspect of the design has the most issues is the first to get fixed and retested.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：颜色编码把"多少人遇到同一问题"变成了看图就能读出的量。**
> 颜色多 = 多人中招 = 优先修。**这是一个不需要统计工具的频次可视化。**

> **★★★ 判据：这个变体同时是"团队统一认知"的手段。**
> 原文允许「stakeholders, developers, designers」一起贴便签。
> **他们贴的不是结论，是观察——这让开发者在讨论前先看见问题。**

### 7.2.4 亲和图的定位

> **原文（p13）**：
> 「Once complete, the affinity diagram should be referred back to **not as a prop, but as the voice of the customer**, and **a partner in design**.」

> **★★★★ 判据：「不是道具，是用户的声音，是设计的合作者」——这是原书对交付物的最高表述。**
> 它同时是一条使用规范：图挂在墙上再没人看，它就只是 prop。

| 方法 | 什么时候用 | 什么时候不能用 | 最少样本量 | 产出物 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **亲和图** | 要把分散材料变成主题；要团队共创理解；要可追溯的层级结论 | 材料太少（凑不出簇）；团队未受训（容易先定类别）；需要统计外推 | 数据量门槛：**每人 50~100 条观察**（p13）；情境访谈变体需**4~6 个工作现场**（现场数，非人数）。**人数原书未给** | 四层便签图（黄/蓝/粉/绿）、主题清单、可追溯的观察-主题链 |

## 7.3 内容分析（方法 17，p41–p42 PDF）

### 7.3.1 定义与它要解决的问题

> **原文（p41）**：
> 「**Content analysis** is the **systematic description of form and content** of written, spoken, or visual materials expressed in **themes, patterns, and counted occurrences** of words, phrases, images, or concepts.」

> **原文（p41）**：
> 「Qualitative research methods that collect rich descriptions such as open-ended responses, narrative descriptions, and visual expressions are often characterized as an "**attractive nuisance**." On the one hand, the material contains **deep accounts of compelling information** critical to design inquiry; on the other hand, lengthy text, interview transcripts, and ambiguous images can be **challenging and time consuming to analyze**.」

> **★★★★ 判据：「诱人的麻烦」（attractive nuisance）是全书对质性数据最准确的形容。**
> 材料越丰富越有用，也越难分析——**这不是执行力问题，是这类数据的固有属性**。
> 内容分析就是针对这个属性的制度化对策。

### 7.3.2 归纳 vs 演绎（原文明确站边）

> **原文（p41）**：
> 「Two primary approaches to content analysis are **inductive** and **deductive**, **the former being preferred and more common**. In **inductive** content analysis, the categories or codes are **derived from a systematic reading of a sample set** of the materials to be analyzed, gradually establishing the categories that will be used for subsequent analysis of all the materials.」

> **原文（p41）**：
> 「In **deductive** content analysis, the codes or categories are **derived prior to analysis**, often based on a **theoretical framework**. For example, in a study of product advertising, codes could be established from **Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs**, looking for textual and visual instances that exemplify physical or social needs, safety, or self-actualization.」

| 路径 | 编码从哪来 | 原文评价 | 什么时候用 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **归纳（inductive）** | 从样本的系统阅读中逐渐确立 | 「**preferred and more common**」 | 默认选它 |
| **演绎（deductive）** | 分析前从理论框架导出 | 未否定，但排在次位 | 已有成熟理论可套（如马斯洛需求层级） |

> **★★★★ 判据：归纳优先——这与亲和图的"自下而上"是同一条原则的两次表述。**
> 本章两节独立给出同一结论（p13 与 p41），可信度高。

### 7.3.3 产出形态：可数，也可只数主题

> **原文（p41）**：
> 「The outcomes of content analysis can be **quantitative**, most often **counting simple occurrences of the units of analysis**: words, phrases, images, concepts; but it may also satisfy the needs of the particular analysis to **merely identify the common themes and patterns** that emerge from the data, supported by a **general indication of how dominantly they are represented**.」

> **★★★ 判据：内容分析可以"不计数"——只要能说明"多普遍"。**
> 原文给了两个层级：① 精确计数（words/phrases/images/concepts）
> ② 只识别主题 + 大致说明其显著程度。
> **很多项目卡在"要不要数"，其实原书允许不数。**

### 7.3.4 还要分析"形式"（不只是内容）

> **原文（p41）**：
> 「In addition to **content**, the analysis method examines **form, or structure of communication**; for example, the **scale and location of images** or the **font and type size of text** on a page or screen or within a document, and the **relationships between texts and images**.」

> **★★★★ 判据：对实体产品设计极关键——「形式」本身是数据。**
> 版面的字号、图像位置、图文关系，都是可分析的对象。
> **这条把内容分析从"文本工具"扩展成了"任何人工制品的形式分析"**，
> 直接可用于包装、说明书、界面、标签的设计审计。

### 7.3.5 工具分界

> **原文（p41）**：
> 「For **smaller** sample sets, content analysis can be done **manually**; for **larger** information sets, **software** is available for sophisticated analysis and communication of results.」

> **★★ 判据：原文只说"小样本手工、大样本软件"，未给分界阈值。**
> 见 §7.7 空白。

| 方法 | 什么时候用 | 什么时候不能用 | 最少样本量 | 产出物 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **内容分析** | 有大量文本/图像材料需系统处理；要主题 + 出现频次；要审"形式"（版式/图文关系） | 材料量很小（直接读即可）；需要"为什么"的因果解释 | **原书未给具体数字**（仅"smaller→manual / larger→software"，无阈值） | 编码表、主题清单、词/短语/概念频次、形式分析（字号/位置/图文关系）、可视化（原文提到词树等） |

## 7.4 KJ 法（方法 49，p105–p106 PDF）

### 7.4.1 它解决的是"会议失败"，不是"数据分析"

> **原文（p105）**：
> 「When the **traditional meeting format fails** to achieve group consensus, the **KJ Technique** can be used to help teams work through a problem space and **prioritize what should be focused on first**.」

> **原文（p105）**：
> 「In traditional meetings, there is **rarely enough time for a problem space to be described, let alone be better understood**. This is **not a symptom of team dysfunction**; rather, it is a **limitation of the traditional meeting format**. The KJ Technique is designed to **succeed in ways that typical meetings fail** because it focuses the team on **one focus question**, and then sets everyone to work on the **same task at the same time**.」

> **★★★★ 判据：会议开不好，多半不是人的问题，是"一次只能一个人说"的格式问题。**
> 原文用「not a symptom of team dysfunction…a limitation of the traditional meeting format」
> 明确为团队开脱，并把矛头指向格式。**这条判据本身就有很高的组织价值。**

### 7.4.2 四个机制（本节是全章最可操作的一段）

> **原文（p105）**：
> 「**The KJ Technique is silent.** Everyone in the group is provided with **blank sticky notes and markers**, and then asked to **write as many problems, insights, data, or opinions as they can think of—in silence**. This way, everyone is provided with an **equal opportunity to express his or her points of view**, and has the assurance that **their issues are being represented and shared**.
> **The KJ Technique makes effective use of time.** In traditional meetings, **only one person can speak or draw on the white board at a time**. With the KJ Technique, **all of the sticky notes are posted simultaneously**, opening up the opportunity for a **holistic assessment** of the problem space. This process helps everyone to understand that it is **not about "my opinions" versus "your opinions"**, but rather "**how do my opinions relate to yours, and how do our concerns paint a broader picture of our challenge?**"
> **Group pressure won't affect outcomes.** The KJ Technique promises **equal representation**, regardless of the politics and personalities involved. It doesn't matter **who has the most power or who can most eloquently argue** their point of view. By providing a framework where everyone silently works together as a team, **decisions are made democratically, with little or no opportunities for coercion.**」

| # | 机制 | 手段 | 消除的具体偏差 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **静默** | 先各自沉默书写，不讨论 | 消除"谁先开口谁定调" |
| 2 | **并行** | 便签同时上墙 | 消除"一次只能一人说"的带宽瓶颈 |
| 3 | **平等** | 不署权力、不靠口才 | 消除等级与表达力差异 |
| 4 | **关系化** | 关注"我的和你的什么关系" | 消除"我的观点 vs 你的观点"的对立 |

> **★★★★ 判据：「静默」不是气氛要求，是防偏差装置。**
> 原文的因果是：静默 → 每人都能表达 → 确保"我的议题被呈现和共享"。
> **一旦允许边写边议，第一个说话的人就劫持了整个问题空间。**

### 7.4.3 时长与产出

> **原文（p105）**：
> 「Within **one to two hours**, a team can organize their notes into an **affinity diagram**, which is a **relational visual representation** of a team's observations, knowledge, concerns, and ideas. Although results of the KJ Technique are **subjective and qualitative**, it is a powerful way for teams to **come together, solve problems, and prioritize next steps**.」

> **★★★ 判据：KJ 的产物就是亲和图——它是"人驱动的亲和图"，与"数据驱动的亲和图"共用同一载体。**
> 两者的区别在材料来源：KJ 来自**团队脑中的关切**，亲和图变体 A/B 来自**研究数据**。
> **混用会导致"团队意见伪装成用户证据"** ——这是本节最重要的警示（推论，非原文）。

| 方法 | 什么时候用 | 什么时候不能用 | 最少样本量 | 产出物 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **KJ 法** | 会议卡住、要快速收敛、要平等表达、要排优先级 | 需要用户证据（KJ 收的是团队意见，不是用户数据）；需要严谨统计 | **原书未给人数**；只给时长「**one to two hours**」 | 亲和图（团队版）、优先级、共识记录 |

## 7.5 主题网络（方法 86，p179–p180 PDF）

### 7.5.1 三层主题结构（原文给得极清楚）

> **原文（p179）**：
> 「Thematic networks have **three classes of themes**:
> **Basic Themes** are **text segments derived directly from the textual data**, and they represent the **most obvious concepts that recur within a text**. Because basic themes are simple, they often **cannot communicate anything meaningful when they stand on their own**. They need to be considered within the context of other basic themes to flesh out a fuller story. As they are combined and begin to illuminate one another, basic themes form **organizing themes**.
> **Organizing Themes** are a **middle-order theme**, and they serve to **organize basic themes into clusters of similar issues**. As an organizing theme takes a group of basic themes under its umbrella, it also seeks to **connect to other organizing themes** so that together they can form a **higher order premise**. As separate organizing themes come together, they begin to take on an **argument, position, or assertion** about a given situation or reality. The **macro theme** that emerges is the **global theme**.
> **Global Themes** distill the **overarching point of the text into a single statement**, and are the **most abstracted representations** of the textual data. Global themes serve as a **summary** for the underlying text, and they articulate the **deeper meaning and complexity** of the data. The global theme can be seen as the **heart of the thematic network**, and it is through the identification of a global theme that a thematic network can be **finalized**.」

| 层级 | 定义 | 数量特征 | 能否单独成句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **基本主题（Basic）** | 直接从文本段提取 | 多 | **不能**——「cannot communicate anything meaningful when they stand on their own」 |
| **组织主题（Organizing）** | 中层，把基本主题聚类 | 中 | 半——开始带上立场 |
| **全局主题（Global）** | 单句概括全篇 | 少（通常 1 个收口） | **能**——「into a single statement」 |

> **★★★★ 判据：三层的关键不是"层层归纳"，而是"基本主题单独说不通"。**
> 原文的理由很硬：基本主题「often **cannot communicate anything meaningful** when they stand on their own」。
> **所以报告里只列基本主题＝没做分析**，只是把文本切碎了再抄一遍。

### 7.5.2 什么时候用

> **原文（p179）**：
> 「Use thematic networks once the **rich, textual data has been collected** (e.g., from **diary studies, directed storytelling, or interviews**), and when you need a **step-by-step method** to help you tease out the challenges that come with analyzing textual data. The technique can help to systematically **break down texts into simpler, manageable clusters** of patterns and themes, and then help you explore **relationships between themes** so that the most unifying message can be visualized.」

> **★★★ 判据：主题网络的价值是"给了步骤"，而不只是"给了概念"。**
> 原文强调「a **step-by-step** method」。当团队在"怎么从逐字稿往前走"卡住时，选它。

### 7.5.3 一个完整的主题实例（图注还原）

**图 p179「Thematic network: 育儿与烹饪」**（原图三栏文字完整，逐项录出）：

| 层级 | 图内实例 |
|---|---|
| **Global Themes（4 条）** | 「**Cooking is a measure of motherhood.**」「**Cooking is a form of bonding.**」「**Nutrition is emotional.**」「**Cooking as control**」「**Cooking is a domestic chore.**」 |
| **Organizing Themes（片段）** | `beneficial / psychological pressure / motherly urge to nourish / mutual mother-child well being / bonding / sense of accomplishment / intrinsic motivation / pleasure / satisfaction / peace of mind / good mom-bad mom` … |
| **Basic Themes（片段）** | `children's basic needs must be met / food as building blocks of health / nutrition is essential / cooking is more time away from kids / gender inequity in the home / frustration / domestic chores / exhaustion / obligation-duty / guarantee of food quality / work-life balance / shopping decisions / daily dinner decisions` |

> **注**：原图 Global Themes 栏视觉上排布了 **5 条陈述**（其中"Cooking as control"与"Cooking is a domestic chore."各占一条），
> 与"global theme 应收口为单句"的定义存在张力。此处**照录原图，不替它调和**。

> **★★★★ 判据：全局主题全部是"断言句"（X 是 Y），不是名词短语。**
> 「Cooking is a measure of motherhood.」——**有主语有谓语有立场**。
> 这给了一条可检查的写作规范：**如果你的全局主题写成"烹饪习惯"，那不是主题，是类别名。**

| 方法 | 什么时候用 | 什么时候不能用 | 最少样本量 | 产出物 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **主题网络** | 已收集丰富文本（日记/讲故事/访谈）；需要分步法；要可视化主题关系 | 材料很少（用不上三层）；要统计结论；时间极紧（三层编码耗时） | **原书未给具体数字** | 三层主题结构（基本/组织/全局）、主题网络图、单句全局主题 |

## 7.6 三角验证（方法 91，p189–p190 PDF）

### 7.6.1 定义与最常见的组合

> **原文（p189）**：
> 「**Triangulation** is the **convergence of multiple methods on the same research question**, to corroborate evidence from several different angles.」

> **原文（p189）**：
> 「The **primary reason** for triangulation is to **ensure accuracy of information**, by **combining sources and mitigating the weaknesses of any single method or source**.」

> **原文（p189）**：
> 「The most common occurrence of triangulation is to **combine observational methods with self-report methods** such as questionnaires or interviews.」

> **★★★★ 判据：三角验证的目标是"抵消单一方法的固有缺陷"，不是"多收点数据"。**
> 原文的动词是 mitigate（减轻）——**它是为方法的弱点配对，不是为提高数据量。**

### 7.6.2 自陈与观察会打架（原文给了具体情境）

> **原文（p189）**：
> 「Often observations serve to **verify self-reports**. However, in cases where participants **falsely portray their behaviors to align with policies, social norms, or research expectations**, observations may reveal **contrary evidence**. Similarly, following the maxim "**actions speak louder than words**," self-reported attitudes or opinions may also be **contradicted by behaviors**.」

> **★★★★ 判据：当自陈与观察冲突时，不要"取平均"——冲突本身是发现。**
> 原文点名了失真的三个来源：**政策、社会规范、研究期望**。
> 「falsely portray…to align with」说明这不是撒谎，是**对齐期待**。
> **这类冲突往往是设计机会所在**（用户说 A 做 B，说明 A 是规范、B 是现实）。

### 7.6.3 四种三角（Denzin 分类，原文脚注给全）

> **原文（p189，脚注 2）**：
> 「Although **methodological triangulation** is most common, Denzin (2006) describes **four different forms**:
> • **Data triangulation**, data gathered across a **variety of times, situations, people**.
> • **Investigator triangulation**, **multiple researchers** to gather and interpret data.
> • **Theoretical triangulation**, **multiple theoretical positions** in interpreting data.
> • **Methodological triangulation**, **multiple methods** for gathering data.」

| # | 类型 | 变的是什么 | 治什么偏差 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **数据三角** | 时间/情境/人 | 单一时点的偶然性 |
| 2 | **研究者三角** | 研究者 | 单人解释偏见 |
| 3 | **理论三角** | 解释框架 | 单一理论的盲区 |
| 4 | **方法三角** | 采集方法 | 单一方法的固有缺陷（**最常用**） |

> **★★★ 判据：多数团队只做第 4 种（方法三角），漏掉前三种。**
> 而**研究者三角**成本最低、收益明显：同一批材料给第二个人编码，就能暴露解释偏见。

### 7.6.4 大小样本配对与实时三角

> **原文（p189）**：
> 「Triangulation can be effective when **comparing larger sets of data collection with more focused research** using a select number of participants. For instance, it is common to **pair a large survey with in-depth interviews, observations, or participatory design activities** held with a **small subset** of the survey population… The combination may also result in a **healthy mix of quantitative and qualitative data**, mutually informative to the same inquiry.」

> **原文（p189）**：
> 「The triangulation of methods can be **separated by time, or converged simultaneously**. For example, in **usability testing**, concurrent recordings are typically made of **keystroke/mouse** or other input operations, **facial expressions**, and **verbalized actions** expressed through a **think-aloud protocol**.」

**图 p190「微软客厅电视研究的三路数据」**（原图文字可读）：

| 数据流 | 原文标注 |
|---|---|
| 观察数据 | 「**OBSERVATION DATA: VIDEO CAPTURE**」 |
| 自报数据 | 「**SELF-REPORT DATA: INTERVIEWS**」 |
| 行为数据 | 「**BEHAVIORAL DATA: RECORDED TV INTERACTIONS**」 |

> **★★★ 判据：三路数据同时录、事后一起看——这是"实时三角"最干净的形态。**
> 原文 p189 还说这些「"naturalistic observations" were then used as **memory triggers** and evidence
> for review and analysis with participants during interviews」。
> **录像不只是证据，还是唤起被试记忆的触发器**——这是双重用途。

| 方法 | 什么时候用 | 什么时候不能用 | 最少样本量 | 产出物 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **三角验证** | 结论要经得起质疑；单一方法有明显短板；大小样本要配对 | 时间/预算只够一种方法；两个方法问的不是同一问题（原文要求「on the **same** research question」） | **原书未给具体数字**（只说"大样本 + 小样本子集"，无比例） | 汇聚/冲突的证据对照表、置信度说明、"自陈 vs 行为"差异清单 |

## 7.8 本章的空白

| 缺什么 | 书中情况 | 该去哪查 |
|---|---|---|
| **分析所需材料量** | 亲和图给了数据量门槛（每人 50~100 条观察）；**内容分析、主题网络、KJ 全部未给** | 原书 p117 的「60~120 行为/人」可作代理指标（见 ch06） |
| **编码者间一致性（信度）** | **完全未展开**：未提 Kappa、未提双人编码校验 | 质性研究方法教材；本章 §7.6.3「研究者三角」是最接近的近似做法 |
| **样本量（分析类方法）** | **本章 5 个方法全部无人数** | 见 ch06 §6.3 总账 |
| **内容分析的软件阈值** | 只说「smaller→manual / larger→software」，**无分界数字** | 原书 p41 脚注引 QSR/NVivo |
| **主题网络的分步流程细节** | 原文强调「step-by-step」，但**本书正文只给三层定义，未给逐步操作** | Attride-Stirling 2001（原书 p179 脚注 2 明确指向它） |
| **亲和图的"簇"的判定标准** | 只说「similar intent, problem, or issue」 | 未展开；实操依赖团队训练 |
| **三角验证冲突的裁决规则** | 只说冲突会出现，**未给"信谁"的规则** | 混合方法研究教材 |
