# 术语表 · 包容性设计 / 无障碍设计

> 源：Clarkson《Inclusive Design: Design for the Whole Population》(2003) ＋ Coleman《Design for Inclusivity》(2007)

## A · 核心概念

### accessibility（可及性／无障碍）
【原书术语】书中用法有明确的历史层次：

| 层 | 含义 | 页码 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 建筑与公共环境的**无障碍**（barrier-free）——坡道、加宽门、转弯半径 | Clarkson p603（术语表） |
| 2 | 扩展到**感官**进入建筑与服务 | 同上 |
| 3 | 再扩展到**感官与认知信息**（ATM、网站、盲文账单、手语动画） | 同上 |
| 4 | **Universal Design 把它进一步扩到产品** | 同上 |

> 原书术语表原话：`Universal Design specifically extends the concept to include access to products.`（Clarkson p603 首字 OCR 为 `Universal`，原文接续前页）
> **注意**：在本技能中，「无障碍」对应第 1–3 层，「包容性设计」对应第 4 层 + 过程。**二者不是同义词**。

---

### barrier-free（无障碍设计／无障碍的）
【原书术语】
> `Original focus of disability campaigners and architects (e.g. Selwyn Goldsmith in UK, Ron Mace in US) was on barrier-free access to building and public environments.`（Clarkson p603）

关键词：**期望阶梯的第一级**（ch01 §1.2.1）。遗产是 `kerb cuts and textured paving, ramped entry, wider doorways and corridors, and the wheelchair 'turning circle'`。

---

### capability loss as a continuum（能力损失的连续统）
【原书术语，**这是本技能最重要的一处术语澄清**】
> `the incorporation of the concept of capability loss as a continuum`（Coleman p221）
> `the continuum version of disability, whereby the severity of capability loss ranges from very slight (category 1) to very severe (category 1 0)`（Clarkson p373）

**用途不是分等级，而是证明"每个人都在线上"**：
> `Treating disability as a continuum raises awareness among designers, and society as a whole, that everyone has the material to be excluded from using a product at some point`（Clarkson p381）

---

### capability spectrum（能力谱）
【通行叫法 —— **原书没有这个词**】
**检索结果**：`capability spectrum` / `ability spectrum` / `spectrum of capability` 在两本书中**均 0 命中**。
**原书的实际表述**：`capability loss as a continuum`（Coleman p221）、`continuum version of disability`（Clarkson p373）。
**书中仅有的 `spectrum` 用法**（**都不是**指能力谱）：
- `a spectrum of user / product interaction challenges, from total or partial exclusion, to difﬁ culty or frustration`（Coleman p187）← 指**排除程度的谱**
- `the extreme end of the user spectrum`（Coleman p112）
- `the extreme end of the usability needs spectrum`（Clarkson p544）

> **引用纪律**：写「能力谱（原书称 capability loss as a continuum）」，**不写**「原书的 capability spectrum」。

---

### capability demands（能力需求）
【原书术语】产品对用户施加的能力要求。与 `user capabilities`（用户能力）构成 `Demand > Capability` 的两端。
> `by identifying the capability demands placed upon the user by the features of the product, it is possible to establish the end-users who cannot use the product irrespective of the cause of their functional impairment`（Clarkson p109）

---

### critical users（关键用户）
【原书术语，Clarkson ch31 专有概念】
**定义**：年轻的、残障程度较重的主流产品用户，被有意选来推动设计思维。
> `For the DBA Design Challenge, the focus shifted generationally to younger users with disabilities that are more severe in nature whom we term 'critical'users. It was a deliberate strategy.`（Clarkson p542）

**与 extreme users 的区别**：

| | critical users | extreme users |
|---|---|---|
| 出处 | Clarkson ch31（RCA / DBA Challenge） | Coleman ch30（IDEO 方法卡） |
| 年龄定位 | **刻意选年轻**（设计者的同辈） | 未限定年龄 |
| 筛选依据 | **残障程度较重** | **分布在两端**（可为任何维度的极端） |
| 核心机制 | 同代人共情 + 强表达能力 | 需求权重放大 + 破除刻板印象 |
| 共同点 | 都不是"代表性用户"，都用于早期启发 | 同 |

> **两处界定不同，本技能并列保留，不擅自统一。**

---

### design exclusion（设计排除）
【原书术语，全书核心】
**最简形式**：`If Demand > Users' Capability then Exclusion`（Coleman p187）
**展开定义**：`Design exclusion arises when the demands of using a particular product, within a given environment, exceed the capabilities of the user.`（Coleman p186）
**工作定义（判据）**：`An inclusively designed product should only exclude the end-users who the product requirements exclude.`（Clarkson p111）

**术语表原话**：
> `Term (or approach) developed by the i-design team as a way to give substance to the idea of inclusive design, by focusing attention on those excluded by particular design of products, service or environments.`（Clarkson p604）
> `The key point made is that some people will always be excluded by any specific design, and that such decisions should be taken knowingly and with justification.`（Clarkson p604）

---

### disability（失能／残疾）
【原书术语】**四层链中的最外一层，且责任在环境**：
> `Disability, which is caused by products and surroundings, occurs when the capabilities required to use a product or service surpass those of the user.`（Coleman p219）

**术语表原话**：
> `the current move is towards understanding disability as the result of a mismatch between individuals and their social and physical environment`（Clarkson p602）

**用词差异**：`In the US the prefered term is 'people with disabilities'. in Europe 'disabled people' tends to be used.`（Clarkson p602）

---

### disorder / impairment / capability / disability（四层链）
【原书术语，Coleman ch13 专有辨析】
| 层 | 术语 | 定义 | 例 |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | **disorder** | 医学状况 | 骨关节炎 |
| ② | **impairment** | 身体部位受影响的方式 | 软骨坑洼粗糙脆弱 → 疼痛与僵硬 |
| ③ | **functional capability loss** | 能力受限 | 手/臂活动范围与力量受限 |
| ④ | **disability** | **产品与环境造成** | 拧不动那个太紧的旋钮 |

> 原书原话（l. 219）：`a disorder is a medical condition such as osteoarthritis ... This disorder may cause an impairment ... These symptoms of impairment may affect a person's functional capabilities ... Disability, which is caused by products and surroundings`
> **只有第 ③ 层可被模拟、可被设计**（ch07 §7.1）。

## B · 方法

### exclusion audit（排除审计）
【原书术语】
> `An exclusion audit is used to estimate quantitative measures of design exclusion, indicating how many people can use a product and how many are excluded from its use.`（Coleman p211）

**完整形态 = 三件事合一**：排除分析（量化）+ 专家交互分析 + 真实用户试验，且用户必须是 boundary users（Coleman p198）。

---

### extreme users（极端用户）
【原书术语，IDEO 方法卡正式条目】
> `One of our method cards is entitled 'Extreme users'. It describes how we frequently select the people we observe from the extreme ends of a distribution, rather than people who might be described as 'typical' or 'average'.`（Clarkson p534）

**两个收益**：① 需求权重放大到肉眼可见；② 破除刻板印象（Stanley / Margot 案例）。
**产出不是"他们要什么"，而是"这件事的本质是什么"**（快递员靠水站导航 → 理解所有人的移动性需求）。

**与另一处界定的差异（并列保留）**：
> Coleman p213 把极端用户的功能定位为"概念期用，逼出 `how can a blind user use this device?` 这类提问"；
> Clarkson p534 把极端用户定位为"**观察对象**的选择策略（选分布两端）"。
> **前者是阶段分工，后者是抽样策略 —— 两处不冲突但不等价。**

---

### boundary users（边界用户）
【原书术语】
> `Boundary users, that is those with moderate capability loss and on the edge of being able to use a product, can most easily identify points of difﬁ culty and exclusion in existing and proposed products.`（Coleman p213）

> **判据/招募标准**：能力分数**接近**产品需求分数的人。
> **为什么是这一档**：严重者早已发展出替代策略（说不清哪里卡住），健全者碰不到边界 —— **只有刚好在边缘的人能准确报告"就差这一点"。**
> 排除审计的成败取决于是否用了 boundary users（Coleman p198）。

---

### informance（信息化表演）
【原书术语，Coleman ch9 三例之一】
> `Informance is a short word for 'informative performance'. It is a set of techniques in which actors and/or researchers study what is known about consumers and role-play potential consumers. Informance subsumes both ethnography and empathy.`（Coleman p165）

**三步**：人种志研究 → 共情解释 → 表演转化。
**验收判据**：`These consumers become 'we' rather than 'they'`（Coleman p165）。
**两个必需场景**：全新品类；用户与设计者差异极大（`30-year-old designers creating products for people who are 70`）。

---

### knowledge loop（知识回路）
【原书术语，Clarkson ch26】
> `Translating user-information into inclusive products and services`（图 26.1 标题，Clarkson p451）

五环：`Know the user` → `capture and represent end-user information` → `Verify the data/assess information representation acceptability` → `Verify the product/assess product acceptability` → `use the information to provide products/services`。
**四类表征**：`Profiles` / `Models` / `Multimedia 'stories'` / `Tables`。
**关键约束**：`Only once those summary representations are available can the product be truly validated`（p451）。
**可从任意点进入**（p450）。

---

### 7-Level approach（七层设计法）
【原书术语，Clarkson ch26】
把三阶段中的 Stage 2（solution development）展开成三层，共成 2+3+2：
Stage 1 = L1 用户期望、L2 用户需求｜Stage 2 = L3 感知、L4 认知、L5 机动｜Stage 3 = L6 实用可接受性、L7 社会可接受性（图 26.3，p457）。
> 每层是 **aim** 不是 **step**：`each level represents an aim, but the method of achieving that aim can vary`（p456）
> **可迭代但不可逆序**（p459）
> **只有 L6 使用 `essential`**：`Formal user trials are essential at this point`（p458）

---

### persona（人物画像）
【原书术语，属 Representation 类】
> `Personas are hypothetical archetypes, representing real people throughout the design process.`（Coleman p162）
**要素**：`typical work ﬂ ow, attitudes, skills, frustrations, goals, and environment`
**数量**：`in general between two and seven`（p162）
**用途**：`to get everyone thinking in a uniﬁ ed way from a user's perspective`（p163）
> **补充判据（本技能据 CITD 七口之家归纳）**：第一张卡应是"**无身体损伤但处境受限**"者（如单亲母亲的时间压力），而非残障者。

---

### PTR / HTA / task analysis（任务分析三件套）
【原书术语】
| 缩写 | 全称 | 说明 | 页码 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TA** | Task analysis | 收集"为达成目标需做什么"的细节；**横跨"问"与"看"两类** | Coleman p157 |
| **HTA** | Hierarchical Task Analysis | 按层级分解子任务，由 **stopping rule** 决定停在哪 | Coleman p158 |
| **PTR** | Personalised Task Representation | 手绘流程 + 颜色编码（时间/视听输入/信息可得性） | Coleman p158 |

---

### WINIT（五层人口模型）
【原书术语，字母缩写】
| 字母 | 词 | 定义 | 页码 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **W** | Whole population | `everyone` | Clarkson p112、p114 |
| **I** | Ideal population | `the maximum achievable`；剔除法定的/安全的/不可弥补的（必须 solution-neutral） | p112 |
| **N** | Negotiable max population | `everyone included by the specification` | p113 |
| **I** | Included population | `those who can actually use the product` | p112 |
| **T** | Target population | `those who were intended to use the product`；`an arbitrary definition` | p114 |

> `At no point in the above definitions is the 'target' population referred to`（p113）—— 因为 T 由管理层选择，不含客观内容。

## C · 模型与图

### inclusive design cube / IDC（包容性设计立方体）
【原书术语，Keates et al. 2000】
> `Each axis on the cube represents user capability and the enclosed volumes reflect population coverage.`（Clarkson p116）

三轴 = 感官 / 认知 / 机动（图 5.3）。三种设计策略占据不同体积：**user-aware design**（主导）/ **customisable-modular** / **special purpose**（图 5.1，p108）。
> **注意**：三轴等权是**几何建模假设**（`Applying equal weight`，p115–p116），**不是重要性判断**。

---

### user pyramid（用户金字塔）
【原书术语，**专有方法名**】
> `universal design ... dominates US, Japanese and Australian approaches ... whereas Europe has generally tended to develop other methods, such as the User Pyramid Approach (Benktzon, 1993).`（Clarkson p107）
> `e.g. Ergonomi De ign Gruppen's User Pyramid`（Clarkson p605，术语表）

**来源**：瑞典 Ergonomi Design Gruppen（Maria Benktzon & Sven-Eric Juhlins）。

> **⚠ 本技能使用该词时必须区分两个近亲**：
> ① **Benktzon 的 User Pyramid Approach / `pyramid of needs`**（Clarkson p33）—— 方法；
> ② **i~design 团队的 user pyramid 图**（Clarkson p439）—— 十级严重度 × 人口百分比 × 产品排除率叠加的**呈现图**。
> 谈方法时指 ①，谈排除率图时指 ②。

**金字塔分档**（Clarkson p439）：1–2 轻（34%）/ 3–6 中（45%）/ 7–10 重（21%）；底数 8,582,200 人。

---

### inclusive merit（包容度）
【原书术语】
> `when developing a design approach for inclusivity it is necessary to consider the measure of success, i.e. the point at which the design is considered to have met the stipulated requirements. This shall be referred to as the inclusive merit of the product.`（Clarkson p110）

**两个自变量**：`the merit of the requirements that define it; and its merit when judged against those requirements`（Clarkson p111）。
**六种比值**见 ch03 §3.4。**最重要的是 Included ÷ Ideal**（Clarkson p115）。

## D · 原则与标准

### 七原则（The Seven Principles of Universal Design）
【原书术语，Center for Universal Design 1997】
1. **Equitable use** — useful and marketable to people with diverse abilities
2. **Flexibility in use** — accommodates a wide range of individual preferences and abilities
3. **Simple and intuitive to use** — easy to understand regardless of experience/knowledge/language/concentration
4. **Perceptible information** — communicates necessary information effectively regardless of ambient conditions or sensory abilities
5. **Tolerance for error** — minimises hazards and adverse consequences of accidental/unintended actions
6. **Low physical effort** — can be used efficiently and effectively with a minimum of fatigue
7. **Size and space for approach and use** — appropriate size and space for approach, reach, manipulation and use regardless of body size, posture or mobility

（Clarkson p30 给出七条；p332–p338 给出每条的指南条目，共 24 条，见 ch01 §1.4.2）

> **原书对这七条的自我批评**：`these principles may seem somewhat prescriptive in the context of thinking about design supporting lifestyle goals and aspirations`（Coleman p45）
> **故七原则适合作检查表，不适合作目标函数。**

---

### 三原则（"Suitable Aesthetic; Explicit Navigation; Accessible Controls"）
【原书术语 —— **但只出自一个项目**】
**检索结果**：`three principles` 在两本书中 **0 命中**；唯一命中为 `Three core design principles`（Coleman p80）。
> `Three core design principles were established through the research, and all three principles can be thought of as 'inclusive design', whether cultural, cognitive or sensory. They are: Suitable Aesthetic; Explicit Navigation, and Accessible Controls.`（Coleman p80）

**出处**：Vodafone Simply 手机项目（IDEO）。
> **[!] 这不是通行的"包容性设计三原则"。** 通行且被原书完整给出的是上面的**七条**。
> 这三条的实际结构是"三轴 + 一条社会可接受性"的压缩写法（见 ch01 §1.5）。

---

### inclusive design（包容性设计）
【原书术语】
**定义**：
> `inclusive design is seen as a progre sive, goal-orientated process - an aspect of business strategy and design practice - rather than a genre of design or a performance measure.`（Clarkson p20）

**工作定义（判据形式）**：`An inclusively designed product should only exclude the end-users who the product requirements exclude.`（Clarkson p111）

**术语表原话**：`a framework and growing body of practice within which business decision-makers and design practitioners can understand and respond to the needs of diverse users, with the ultimate aspiration of developing products and services that can meet the needs of the whole population within the context of a consumer society`（Clarkson p27）

> **关键**：它是**过程**，不是流派、不是达标线。这是它区别于 universal design 的地方。

---

### universal design（通用设计）
【原书术语，美国路径】
**定义**：
> `the design of products and environments to be usable by all people, to the greatest extent possible, without adaptation or specialised design`（Clarkson p30）

**提出者**：Ron Mace（1985 起；1989 建立 Center for Accessible Housing，后更名 Center for Universal Design）。
**七原则**见上。

> **原书对它的批评（重要）**：`universal design is more of an ethos than a rigorous, systematic design approach. There are very few structured descriptions of the implementation of universal design in more detail than broad design objectives`（Clarkson p107）
> **这解释了本技能为何以"可算的过程"（WINIT + 排除率 + 7-Level）为主体，而非原则清单。**

---

### design for all（为所有人设计）
【原书术语，欧洲路径】
> `Design for all` 是欧洲对 universal design 的对应概念，**强调 ICT**。
> `The term universal access (or access for all) is used in a similar way to Universal Design, but with an emphasis on information and communications technology (ICT).`（Clarkson p603）

---

### transgenerational design（跨代设计）
【原书术语，James Pirkl，美国 Syracuse】
> `Proposes that in an era of population ageing, designs should work for people of a wide range of ages and capabilities. Differs from Universal Design and Design for All in that it does not place the same emphasis on disability, but takes a market-led approach.`（Clarkson p603）

**三选项判据**（Clarkson p33–p34）：① 什么都不做 → 问题延续；② 做专门的老人产品 → `will immediately become stigmatised. however, and be rejected by the very market for which they were intended`；③ 一开始就为跨代人群设计。

---

### gerontechnology（老年技术学）
【原书术语】
> `Concept developed at Technical University of Eindhoven (TUE) in the Netherlands ... Emphasis is on the development of age-friendly technology and on deploying technology to address age-related factors. Key focus is consumer pull as opposed to technology push`（Clarkson p603）

---

### BS 7000-6（包容性设计管理标准）
【原书术语，British Standards Institution，2005 年 2 月】
> `BS 7000-6 Guide to Managing Inclusive Design was published in February 2005 to help all organisations (private, public and not-for-profit) to evolve such a professional stance.`（Coleman p62）

**五条商业理由**（Coleman p63）：
1. 适应市场变化（人口老龄化、新立法、技术、竞争者已采用）
2. 理解消费者期望与生活方式变化
3. 通过人体工学与人因原则提供以用户为中心的设计
4. 维持员工忠诚（老龄 workforce）
5. 通过创新建立企业声誉

> **为何是"过程标准"而非"指标标准"**：指标标准会被当作"最低即最高"的上限；过程标准可随期望演化（见 ch01 §1.2.2、Clarkson p595）。
