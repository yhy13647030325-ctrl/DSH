## 8.0 总纲：这三章处理的是"动机"，不是"知识"

第 6、7 章都在讲工具。本章处理一个更根本的问题：**工具齐备、数据齐全，团队为什么还是不去做？**

> **原文（Clarkson p537）**：
> 「**It is not sufficient just to know about 'inclusive design' and to have access to resources that guide and inform; designers also need to be motivated to do something about the problem.**」

> **★★★★ 判据（全章核心）：知识充足 ≠ 行动发生。本章全部内容都在解决"动机"这一层。**
> 原书给出的解法不是说服、不是规范、不是KPI，而是**让设计者与真实的人发生情感连接**：

> **原文（Clarkson p537）**：
> 「**we believe that emotionally engaging with people makes their inclusion unarguable**」

> **★★★★ 判据：`emotionally engaging ... makes their inclusion unarguable` —— 情感连接的作用是把"要不要包含"从可辩论的问题变成不可辩论的问题。**
> 这是本章唯一的一句机制陈述，也是全书最大胆的一句。它的含义是：**当团队认识了一个具体的人之后，"这个群体是否值得考虑"这个问题在心理上就消失了。**

**本章三个主产地：**

| 章节 | 主题 | 核心动作 |
|---|---|---|
| **ch28**（p486–p500） | 共情的方法谱系 | 从"沉浸三年"到"两小时用户论坛"的连续谱 |
| **ch30**（p527–p538） | 极端用户法 | 选分布两端的人；不追求共识；原型化体验 |
| **ch31**（p539–p548） | critical users | 用年轻重度残障者逼出创新 |

## 8.1 共情的方法谱系：从三年代入到两小时论坛

### 8.1.1 谱系的一端：Patricia Moore 的三年

> **原文（Clarkson p490）**：
> 「**Over a three-year period from 1979 she travelled throughout the United States and Canada, visiting over 100 towns and cities disguised as a woman in her eighties.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p490）**：
> 「**With her body altered to simulate the physical and sensory changes associated with ageing, she was able to respond to people, products, and environments as an older woman and find out first hand just how inhospitable the world is to older people.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p490）**：
> 「**One of the key things that she came to realise from this extended immersion in an older persona was that the norms of ergonomics, the standards and guidelines designers work to, and the consumer profiles marketers and advertisers use are all based on a subset of 'normal' users, and do not encompass those who fall outside this category.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：Moore 三年实验的核心发现不是"老人有困难"，而是"规范本身只基于一个子集"。**
> 这个发现比"某产品不好用"高一个层级：**问题不在个别产品，在标准、指南、消费者画像全部建立在一个子集之上。**
> **这与第 1 章"平均人的构成"是同一条判断，但 Moore 是用身体验证出来的，不是推理出来的。**

> **原文（Clarkson p490）**：
> 「**As a result of her experiences she came to the view that rather than divide the world into able-bodied and disabled, it was important to recognise that we are all 'differently abled', and that our capabilities change throughout our lifetimes. There are times when all of us are disabled, and life expectancy is now such that we can all aspire to live long enough to suffer some age-related impairment as her mother did.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p490）**：
> 「**She also realised that disability is not something people are born with or acquire through accident, illness or as a result of ageing, it is something that arises from a mismatch between the environment and their abilities.**」

> **★★★ 判据：`differently abled` 与"能力随时间变化"是 Moore 从体验中得出的，而第 2 章的能力谱是它的量化形式。**
> 二者是同一命题的两种表述：**体验版（每个人都会经历）与量化版（十级严重度分布）。**

> **原文（Clarkson p491）**：
> 「**It stands at one extreme of the range of empathic models and methods that are open to designers, and within the reality of everyday professional life, it is rarely feasible to take this approach.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：`It stands at one extreme of the range` —— Moore 的方法被明确定位为"谱的一端"，不是"最佳实践"。**
> 这条极重要，因为它防止了一个常见错误：**把"必须有深度沉浸才算真共情"当作标准，结果团队什么也不做。**
> 原书紧接着给出另一端：`practicing designers respond best to flexible approaches that give quicker insights`。

### 8.1.2 谱系的中间：IDEO 的五步流程

> **原文（Clarkson p491）**：
> 「**International design consultancy IDEO uses a sequential research and development process - understand, observe, visualise, evaluate and implement - to deliver a close fit between user needs, product functionality, visual and tactile qualities and joy in use**」

> **原文（Clarkson p491）**：
> 「**Key to this is understanding the full range of users likely to interact with a product, including sales representative, dealer, purchaser, operator, installer, end user, and service/maintenance staff.**」

| 步 | 英文 | 做什么 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **understand** | 理解 |
| 2 | **observe** | 观察 |
| 3 | **visualise** | 可视化（场景构建） |
| 4 | **evaluate** | 评估 |
| 5 | **implement** | 实现 |

> **★★★★ 判据：`the full range of users` 明确包含六类角色 —— 销售、经销商、购买者、操作者、安装者、终端用户、服务/维护人员。**
> 这是本章对"使用者 ≠ 购买者 ≠ 操作者"的正式列举。**其中"安装者"与"维护人员"最常被漏，而第 6 章已证明安装与维护正是排除高发段（Clarkson p480）。**

### 8.1.3 谱系的另一端：两小时的用户论坛

> **原文（Clarkson p542）**：
> 「**With the context established, a two-hour project-specific forum of'critical' users is organised for each design team.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p492）**：
> 「**Central to all these initiatives has been direct contact between designers and 'critical' users, where designers learn from a direct engagement with user experience which gives insight into emotional and aspirational factors, and stimulates an empathic desire to understand and respond to quality of life issues rather than just physical problems**」

> **★★★★ 判据：完整谱系是"三年沉浸 —— 数周观察 —— 两小时论坛"，而两小时版已被证明能产生变革性效果。**
> 原文对两小时论坛的效果判断（Clarkson p541）：`the experience of working closely with 'critical' users will result in a transformation of their working practice or, more modestly, a raised consciousness`。
> **实操结论：不要再等"有条件做深度研究"。两小时的直接接触已经是有效剂量。**

### 8.1.4 一个反转：目标不是"解决问题"，而是"扩大期望"

> **原文（Clarkson p488）**：
> 「**For example, when one of the authors was designing a new kitchen for a friend in a wheelchair, it emerged that what the friend desired above all else was for the neighbours to be jealous (Coleman, 2001). This realisation inverted the design priorities, by setting 'normal' desires and identity factors on a par with the ergonomic factors, and in many senses over and above them.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p488）**：
> 「**If the ergonomic factors are ignored or not adequately solved then the design will fail, but the key to success lies beyond the quality of the 'fit' with the user's capabilities. Equally important is the emotional fit with the desires of the user, and it is the marriage of physical and emotional fit that is the ultimate goal.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：人机适配是"失败的必要条件"（不做必败），情绪适配是"成功的充分条件"（只做人机也不够）。**
> 「让邻居嫉妒」这个案例之所以重要，是因为它把**身份与欲望**放在了与人体工学**并列甚至更高**的位置。
> **产品含义：一个"符合所有可及性规范但难看"的产品，在逻辑上仍未通过验收。**

> **原文（Clarkson p487）**：
> 「**Young designers, and in particular students, tend to design for themselves and their peers. College projects may feature older or disabled people, but the focus is normally on special needs rather than lifestyle and aspirations.**」

> **★★★ 判据：只看到"特殊需求"而看不到"生活方式与期望"，是设计教育造成的系统性偏差。**
> 这与第 1 章"特殊需求三重失败"的商业版是同一问题的教育版。

### 8.1.5 共情能被"教会"吗？原书的答案是能

> **原文（Clarkson p488）**：
> 「**Young designers are highly sensitised to the desires and aspirations of their peers, but, quite understandably, lack the same empathy with other people because they have little contact with them. What is encouraging, however, is that when given the opportunity to work with older and disabled people, young designers move rapidly towards acquiring a deeper understanding of individual desires and frustrations.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p488）**：
> 「**Close contact leads to an empathy that acts as a spur to creativity.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：`close contact leads to an empathy` —— 共情不是天赋，是接触的产物。**
> 因此组织动作很明确：**不要试图通过培训"培养共情"，而要安排接触。**
> 原文还给了心理机制：`Young designers enjoy the experience, and derive considerable satisfaction from feeling they have done something useful and valuable - not worthy, but worthwhile`。
> **注意 `not worthy, but worthwhile` 这个区分：有效感（worthwhile）比道德感（worthy）更能驱动人。**

## 8.2 极端用户法：为什么选两端而不是中间

### 8.2.1 方法卡明确的立场

> **原文（Clarkson p534）**：
> 「**One of our method cards is entitled 'Extreme users'. It describes how we frequently select the people we observe from the extreme ends of a distribution, rather than people who might be described as 'typical' or 'average'.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：`from the extreme ends of a distribution, rather than ... 'typical' or 'average'` —— 这是 IDEO 方法卡上的正式立场，不是权宜之计。**

### 8.2.2 四个极端用户案例（原书给的）

> **原文（Clarkson p534）**：
> 「**We were designing a web and TV service aimed at creating communities, so we talked to a woman who had been sacked twice for overuse of online chat rooms. Her impassioned perspective on these interactions helped us to understand what she got out of her online relationships.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p534）**：
> 「**In designing packaging for mineral water, we talked to a bicycle courier who explained how she navigated the city by water stations and helped us to understand needs for mobility that we all share to some extent.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p534）**：
> 「**We talked to people who drank pints of soft drinks every day; they had an understanding of the subtleties of soft drink formulation that**」

> **原文（Clarkson p535）**：
> 「**helped us to produce a new energy drink.**」

（该句跨 p534–p535；此处按页分两段引用，不改字。）

> **原文（Clarkson p535）**：
> 「**We also asked contacts of friends and family across the globe to send back photos of people drinking. The range and ingenuity of portable drinking vessels allowed us to develop novel packaging solutions.**」

| 案例 | 极端在哪 | 产出了什么 |
|---|---|---|
| **网络社群服务** | 因过度使用聊天室被开除两次 | 理解"人从线上关系里获得了什么" |
| **矿泉水包装** | 自行车快递员，靠水站导航全城 | 理解"我们都有的移动性需求" |
| **能量饮料** | 每天喝数品脱软饮 | 理解配方微妙之处 |
| **饮用容器** | 全球亲友拍的"人怎么带饮料"照片 | 新颖包装方案 |

> **★★★★ 判据：极端用户的产出不是"他们需要什么"，而是"这件事的本质是什么"。**
> 快递员的案例最能说明：她不是"移动性受限者"，而是**移动性的极端使用者**；她从水站导航这件事，揭示了所有人对补水点的依赖。
> **这就是"从极端反推主流"的机制：极端用户把某个需求的权重放大到肉眼可见。**

### 8.2.3 极端用户 ≠ 只服务极端

> **原文（Clarkson p538）**：
> 「**They can argue that the views of a 7 0 year old might inform the design of an MP3 player, or that voice interface software could be improved by listening to how the blind navigate the world, and that someone with learning difficulties could help us to design a better VCR interface.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：三个具体的"跨人群迁移"例子 —— 70 岁老人 → MP3；盲人导航 → 语音界面；学习障碍者 → 录像机界面。**
> 每一个都是**把极端端的知识与技能迁移到完全不同的人群**。
> **判据形式：判断一个极端用户是否有价值，不问"他是不是目标用户"，而问"他在哪件事上比常人更懂"。**

## 8.3 四条流程原则（Coleman ch30 的核心）

> **原文（Clarkson p531）**：
> 「**This chapter is about IDEO's response to this, which has been essentially to develop and maintain a design process that. .. embraces diversity of input emphasises inspiration over validation is not aimed at consensus prototypes the experience of use.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：这四条不是技巧，是流程的四个设计原则，且每一条都与常规做法相反。**

| # | 原则 | 常规做法 | 本原则的差别 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **embraces diversity of input**（拥抱输入的多样性） | 收敛到代表性样本 | 主动要更多、更不相关的输入 |
| **2** | **emphasises inspiration over validation**（启发优先于验证） | 用户研究用于验证方案 | 用户研究用于**产生**方向 |
| **3** | **is not aimed at consensus**（不追求共识） | 会议目标是达成一致 | 刻意**推迟**共识，同时持有多个观点 |
| **4** | **prototypes the experience of use**（原型化使用体验） | 原型化形态 | 原型化**体验**（用人和椅子就能做） |

### 8.3.1 原则二：启发 vs 验证

> **原文（Clarkson p534）**：
> 「**Our user research can emphasise the search for divergent content because later we will emphasise prototyping to identify the best ideas and directions to pursue. We can afford to be non-judgemental with our ideas at this stage because we know that we will counter this chaotic thinking with some honest evaluation with potential users.**」

> **★★★ 判据：发散阶段可以不做判断，因为后面有严格的用户评估来收口 —— 这才是"不判断"的正当理由。**
> 没有后半句，"不判断"就是偷懒。**这是一个成对的机制：前期极度发散 + 后期严格验证。**

### 8.3.2 原则三：不追求共识

> **原文（Clarkson p535）**：
> 「**During this divergent phase all efforts should be aimed at postponing consensus, holding multiple points of view about the same thing, finding orthogonal views to support these different standpoints, assuming nothing and treating all input as worthwhile.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p535）**：
> 「**Challenging preconceptions should be one of the main aims of design research, and the design process should be open to hearing these challenges.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：设计研究的主要目标之一是"挑战先入之见"，而不是"确认先入之见"。**

> **原文（Clarkson p535）**：
> 「**The value of Stanley and Margot is partly that they are ingenious, interesting and motivated people, and partly that they challenge a few preconceptions about how the elderly spend their time.**」

**两个反刻板印象的用户（原文）：**

> **原文（Clarkson p535）**：
> 「**About Stanley, the 80-year-old clock restorer who taught himself computing so he could restore clocks better. He instruments his clocks with sensors and the software he's written allows him to see how circular he's made the cogs or how consistently he's fashioned the teeth.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p535）**：
> 「**Or Margot, the 80-year-old in the 17th century cottage, who has had a PC for the last 20 years or more. Her husband worked for IBM and she was one of the first people in the UK to get a PC - when she was already in her 60's. She had an Internet service provider (ISP) well before IDEO London got one!**」

> **★★★★ 判据：选这两个人不是因为他们"有代表性"，恰恰因为他们**反代表性** —— 他们打破了"老人不会用电脑"的先入之见。**
> 这解释了极端用户法的另一半价值：**它同时提供"需求放大"和"刻板印象破除"两种收益。**

### 8.3.3 原则四：原型化体验（本章最可复制的方法）

> **原文（Clarkson p536）**：
> 「**Most designers will be familiar with prototyping the physical form as a way of communicating and exploring the weight, balance, colour, shape and material. Many of these physical forms will simply be vessels for electronics and software with which users will interact. We now have ways of simply and faithfully proto typing these behaviours and interactions, sometimes separately, sometimes within the designed form.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p536）**：
> 「**Even before we've chosen the physical form, or thought about the user's interaction, designers decide (sometimes implicitly) on the experience they will create - will it be reassuring or challenging, will it be slick or whimsical, fun or business-like. We try to prototype these experiences. It is often extremely simple to do and revealing.**」

**三个"体验原型"案例（原书原文）：**

**案例一：在飞机经济舱睡觉**

> **原文（Clarkson p536）**：
> 「**When asked by a major airline to consider what it might be like to sleep in economy class we prototyped the experience with some chairs and volunteers. The major issues soon became clear.**」

**案例二：远程机器人手术（水球与钉子游戏）**

> **原文（Clarkson p536）**：
> 「**A colleague asked the project team to play a game with him. He blindfolded two people, one was asked to sit down with a water-filled balloon on his lap and the other was given two 5 Ocm poles with pins sticking out of one end. A third person was told to give simple, verbal instructions to the person with the poles, the aim being to tell him how to take the balloon away without pricking the balloon and drenching his colleague. The people doing this were surgeons and theatre nurses, and the project was the development of remote robotic surgery equipment.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p536）**：
> 「**This simple game conveyed the communication and control difficulties and enormous anxieties of the actual situation with next to no preparation or expense.**」

**案例三：植入式除颤器（周末随机呼叫）**

> **原文（Clarkson p537）**：
> 「**One of the team distributed pagers, notebooks and disposable cameras to the rest of the team, client team included. Over the course of a weekend he paged individuals at different times and had them record what they were doing at that moment- the moment of their 'shock'.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p537）**：
> 「**On the Monday when everyone shared their stories they began to realise the personal and social impact of this remedy. One person was up a ladder, another holding his baby; the person entering a railway carriage realised the impossibility of communicating what had just happened to him and how easily that might lead to well-intentioned but potentially disastrous assistance.**」

> **★★★★★ 判据（本章最可复制的一条）：用"最低保真"的手段（椅子、气球、钉子、呼机）就能原型化最复杂的体验问题，成本"几乎为零"（`next to no preparation or expense`）。**
> 三个案例的共同结构：
> ① 找到一个**无法直接做的核心体验**（在飞机上睡、远程手术、体内放电）；
> ② 用**日常物件**重建该体验的**关键约束**（空间、盲视 + 单向指令、随机打断）；
> ③ 让**真实的当事人或团队**参与；
> ④ 在周一分享故事 → 转化为设计判断。

> **原文（Clarkson p537）**：
> 「**There are three significant points to draw out of these stories. First, that we intentionally create prototypes we know to be 'wrong', but which are provocative and from which we'll learn something. Second, the realisation of consequences that would otherwise be undiscovered until the device was developed and first used (the assumed value of proto typing). Third, the teams have shared a significant experience and understood how closely it resembled real life.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：三个收益 —— ① 明知是"错的"原型但能学到东西；② 提前发现只有上市后才会暴露的后果；③ 团队共享一次经验，从而把团队绑在一起。**
> 第三条最被低估：**体验原型的产物不只是洞察，还有团队的共同记忆。**

## 8.4 critical users：年轻的、重度残障的用户

### 8.4.1 定义与策略

> **原文（Clarkson p542）**：
> 「**For the DBA Design Challenge, the focus shifted generationally to younger users with disabilities that are more severe in nature whom we term 'critical'users. It was a deliberate strategy. for the reasons outlined in the following sections.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p543）**：
> 「**Being young, they present a design scenario based on active social engagement. A long life lies ahead of them with all this implies in terms of a desire for access to mainstream products, services and employment opportunities.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p543）**：
> 「**Design can be said to be a young person's profession as exemplified by those who have participated to date in the DBA Design Challenge, all of whom are aged below forty. Thus, the users are the designer's contemporaries not their parents or grandparents with an equivalence of lifestyle and taste existing between the two groups.**」

> **★★★★ 判据："同代人"是 critical users 策略的核心 —— 用户是设计者的同辈，不是父辈或祖辈。**
> 理由（原文）：`designers and young 'critical' users tend to share common cultural tastes and aspirations, an aspect which helps to rapidly establish empathy`。
> **这解决了 §8.1.5 的"接触产生共情"问题：同代人之间共情建立得最快。**

> **原文（Clarkson p543）**：
> 「**When I spoke with Dawn, her lifestyle was just like my lifestyle - the things she wanted - it's just that she had more problems than I.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：`her lifestyle was just like my lifestyle - the things she wanted - it's just that she had more problems than I.` —— 这一句话是全部共情方法的浓缩。**
> 差别不在"想要什么"，只在"遇到多少问题"。**因此设计任务是消除后者，而不是重新设计前者。**

### 8.4.2 为什么是"重度"而不是"轻度"

> **原文（Clarkson p544）**：
> 「**Young 'critical' users represent the extreme end of the usability needs spectrum, not its middle reaches, as would be the case with many older users. For this latter group, their disabilities may be multiple but are often minor in nature and more easily compensated for in lifestyle and design terms. Indeed they may not even identify them as disabilities, but inevitable inconveniences of growing old.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p544）**：
> 「**In contrast, the severity of disability experienced by the 'critical' user tends to give them a more acute understanding of design failure and the ability to articulate both the problem and their idiosyncratic or common response to it.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：重度用户的两种能力 —— 更敏锐地识别设计失败（`more acute understanding`）+ 更能清楚表达问题与自己的应对方式（`articulate both the problem and ... response`）。**
> 第二条是**可用性极低的资源**：多数用户说不清哪里不好用，重度用户往往说得非常准。
> 这与第 6 章"内部 user champion"是同一件事：

> **原文（Clarkson p544）**：
> 「**When buying products, people with disabilities tend to look beyond the product features for potential problems that may prove difficult or prevent them using it. They plan very thoroughly what they can and cannot do.**」

> **★★★ 判据：`They plan very thoroughly what they can and cannot do` —— 残障用户天然是"预演型用户"，这使他们成为最好的评估者。**

### 8.4.3 一个用户的例子：改造 cookie

> **原文（Clarkson p544）**：
> 「**He had devised a means by which the much-maligned 'cookie,' could be subverted and customised, thereby stripping it of its negative 'junk mail' qualities and transforming it into a feature, which aided rather than hindered his navigation of a site.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p544）**：
> 「**This ability to assess a product thoroughly and then offer alternatives to negative aspects of an existing design was cited by Alloy Total Product Design as being the key factor that enabled them to rapidly establish the hierarchy of issues needing to be addressed in the final design for Kettlesense**」

> **★★★★ 判据：`assess thoroughly and then offer alternatives` —— 这是 critical user 与普通用户的区别：不只会抱怨，还能给出替代方案。**
> 这个能力直接产出了"问题层级（hierarchy of issues）"，即**优先级**。

### 8.4.4 反直觉结论：约束越多，创新越强

> **原文（Clarkson p545）**：
> 「**A common perception among designers with no experience of the inclusive design process is that severity of disability places limitations on the design process with fatal creative compromise as the inevitable end result.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p545）**：
> 「**"Designers think of inclusive design as being a ticking the box exercise" said one design manager**」

> **原文（Clarkson p545）**：
> 「**In contrast, the participating DBA design teams found it an invaluable discipline, forcing them to go back to first principles in concept development and not merely reconfigure an existing product or service cosmetically. "The more constraints there are, the more creative designers have to be" was the response of one designer to the question as to whether they found the needs of'critical' users hampered them creatively. It was echoed by the others interviewed.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：`forcing them to go back to first principles in concept development and not merely reconfigure an existing product or service cosmetically` —— 极端用户的作用是**阻止"化妆式改款"**。**
> 这是对"约束抑制创造力"这一普遍担忧的最强反驳：**约束不是限制创造，是禁止敷衍。**

> **原文（Clarkson p545）**：
> 「**A visually impaired user who participated in the two user forums associated with Imagination's Inspiration-Park (case study 3) commented, "I don't want a spacey park'- in other words they aspired to mainstream and not stigmatising special needs design but were prepared to accept a design solution that may not meet all of their needs but was inclusive in its foundation and basic features.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：用户自己愿意接受"不完美但基础包容"的方案，拒绝"完美但特殊化"的方案。**
> 这是全部包容性设计里最重要的一次用户表态：**用户宁可要一个 80% 好用的主流产品，也不要一个 100% 贴合但一看就是"给他们做的"产品。**
> 与第 3 章的"污名化"、第 1 章的 Suitable Aesthetic 完全一致。

### 8.4.5 两类用户的差异（沟通方式与应对策略）

> **原文（Clarkson p545）**：
> 「**Older users may be wary of design solutions involving technology or the manipulation of unfamiliar interfaces whereas many young users are computer literate, product literate and technology 'savvy' to a high degree.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p547）**：
> 「**to specialist education means they may neither read Braille nor have mastered the computer or even developed the sophisticated coping strategies displayed by younger disabled users (Cassim, 1998). Hence the scenario they present to the designers is more likely to be based on inability than positive alternative ability, which younger users bring to the user forum along with their unsurpassed technological expertise.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：年轻重度用户带来的是"正向替代能力（positive alternative ability）"，年长用户带来的多是"不能（inability）"。**
> 这是一个精确的对照，直接影响选择谁：
> - 要**创新方向**→ 选年轻重度用户（他们有替代策略，能给出"另一种做法"）；
> - 要**真实困难**→ 选年长用户（他们呈现的是能力缺口）。
> **注意：这不是价值判断，是产出类型判断。**

> **原文（Clarkson p547）**：
> 「**In many of the projects undertaken for the DBA Design Challenge, exposure to the highly developed coping strategies of the disabled users has offered the designers lateral ways of dealing with standard scenarios and in many cases been the trigger for the truly innovative features of their final designs.**」

> **★★★ 判据：`coping strategies` 是创新触发的直接来源 —— 不是"用户的需求"，是"用户自己发明的办法"。**
> 这一点与第 6 章"系统化方法能发现 coping 策略"呼应：一边靠推演，一边靠观察，产物相同。

### 8.4.6 一个完整的迁移案例：Inspiration Park

> **原文（Clarkson p547）**：
> 「**one visually impaired user's fear of open spaces based on her inability to pick up the sound cues supplied by the built environment; her need for clear navigational configurations of space and in particular her terror of open stretches of water were directly translated into innovative design features which reconciled her love of the sound and sensation of flowing water with her primary need for safety.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p547）**：
> 「**Separate issues were raised by wheelchair users who cited their desire for an all-weather environment while a user with a cognitive disability required instinctive wayfinding indicators, which were not language based.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：这个案例展示了"矛盾需求的调和"—— 她既爱流水的声音与触感，又恐惧开阔水面。**
> 设计任务的本质是：**不是二选一，而是找出同时满足两者的形式**（有安全边界的水景）。
> **这是 critical user 方法能产出的最高价值：不是清单式的需求，而是一个待解的矛盾。**

## 8.5 与"焦点小组"的对照（一个极易被忽略的实操差异）

> **原文（Clarkson p543）**：
> 「**Interviews conducted with eight of the nine DBA Design Challenge teams (Cassim 2002, Hua Dong et al., 2002) revealed that while focus groups were a common feature of commercial design projects, the designer's 'fly on the wall' status and lack of control over the selection of participants militated against any substantive analysis of usability issues other than those defined by the market research aims of the project.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p543）**：
> 「**Hence, while observational data could be gleaned by designers from such sessions, the participants were likely to be drawn from narrow age bands and unrepresentative of the multi-generational diversity of needs and abilities**」

> **原文（Clarkson p544）**：
> 「**Of all the design tools and resources at their disposal, the participating designers cited the ability to directly engage with 'critical' users as being the major factor enabling them to push the design envelope since it supplied them with a set of creative triggers that stimulated key features of their final designs.**」

| 维度 | **焦点小组** | **用户论坛** |
|---|---|---|
| 设计者角色 | `'fly on the wall'`（旁观者） | **主动访谈者** |
| 参与者选择 | 设计者**无法控制** | 按项目相关性选择 |
| 目标 | 市场研究目的 | 可用性问题 |
| 年龄构成 | `narrow age bands` | 跨代际 |
| 产出 | 观察数据 | `creative triggers` |

> **★★★★ 判据：焦点小组的根本问题是"设计者在里面没有权限"—— 既不能选人，也不能定议题。**
> 因此它再规范也无法替代用户论坛。**判据形式：判断一个用户活动是否有效，先问"设计者能否决定议题与人选"。**
