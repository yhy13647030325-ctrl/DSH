## 6.0 总纲：为什么需要"一库"而不是"一法"

> **原文（Clarkson p469）**：
> 「**Suggesting one single rigid framework of methods for the assessment of product inclusivity, would be contradicting the basic ethos of inclusivity, by limiting designers to a narrow set of choices.**」

> **★★★ 判据：只推荐一种评估方法，本身就是不包容的（原文：`would be contradicting the basic ethos of inclusivity`）。**
> 这条判断极强：**方法的单一化 = 排除设计团队间的差异**。不同公司流程不同、项目预算不同、可用用户不同 —— 单一方法必然在某些场景下不可行，从而被整体放弃。

> **原文（Clarkson p469）**：
> 「**Assessment tools to support the assessment of product inclusivity should be straightforward, fast and economic to implement. Assessment techniques have to be developed with designers in mind, by being practical and efficient, as well as appealing and inspiring.**」

> **★★★ 判据：评估工具的四条门槛 —— 直接、快、经济、且"有吸引力、能激发人"。**
> 最后一条（`appealing and inspiring`）在工程文献里罕见，但它决定了采纳率：**一个正确的但让人不想用的方法，实际使用率接近零。**

**本章的六种方法（按 Clarkson ch27 顺序）：**

| # | 方法 | 类型 | 需要用户？ | 本章节 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Systematic approach**（系统化方法） | 结构化自省 | **否** | §6.2 |
| 2 | **User observation + interviews** | 观察 | 是 | §6.3 |
| 3 | **User trials + questionnaires** | 试验 | 是 | §6.4 |
| 4 | **Checklists and guidelines** | 检查表 | 否 | §6.5 |
| 5 | **Simulation** | 模拟 | 否（模拟替代） | §6.6 |
| 6 | **Expert appraisal** | 专家评审 | 否（专家替代） | §6.7 |

## 6.1 前提：先解决"选谁"的问题

### 6.1.1 为什么"代表性用户"是个陷阱

> **原文（Clarkson p467）**：
> 「**To achieve reliable and useful results, many assessment methods require the participation of'representative' users of the intended target population. It has been argued, though, that it can be difficult to identify and recruit representative users (Norris and Wilson, 1999).**」

> **原文（Clarkson p467）**：
> 「**It has further been said that it might not be necessary to spend so much effort looking for representative users, since particular interesting users can provide design inspiration if one knows how to look for it. An interesting user is someone that has some particular characteristics that can contribute to the problem being addressed and ultimately be integrated into the final included population.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：`an interesting user` 取代 `a representative user` —— 判断标准从"能否代表总体"变成"能否贡献到这个具体问题"。**
> 这不是偷懒，是因为后者在非主流人群中**统计上不可能实现**：

> **原文（Clarkson p467）**：
> 「**However, inclusive design approaches aim to expand the boundaries of mainstream design to include other users with a wider variety of functional capability profiles. While 'non-mainstream' users are numerically frequent as a whole, they are also, by definition, heterogeneous.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p467）**：
> 「**Consequently, the broad umbrella description 'non-mainstream' applies to a multitude of different user sub-groups. Each of these sub- groups exhibits its own unique characteristics and is often significantly different to the others. Consequently, rather than a single large population group to draw representative users from, there are many smaller groups, each representing fewer people.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p467）**：
> 「**Thus, the designer is faced with the two-fold difficulty of deciding which users to choose from within each sub-group and which sub- groups to address. In addition, it is difficult to know whether the selected users are representative of the intended target population (and their subgroup), or if this sample is complete in terms of including all the users that could potentially benefit from a commercially feasible design.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：主流用户同质（可抽样代表），非主流用户"总体人数多但内部高度异质"（不可抽样代表）。**
> 这是本章最有方法论价值的一句。它解释了为什么传统抽样逻辑在包容性设计上失效：
> - 主流：N 大、方差小 → 抽 30 人可得代表性结论；
> - 非主流：总人数也大，但被切成几十个小子群，每个子群 N 小、彼此差异大 → **任何抽样都只能覆盖一两个子群**。

### 6.1.2 破解：用独立能力量表代替"人群身份"

> **原文（Clarkson p468）**：
> 「**One way to overcome problems with identification, sampling and completeness of non-mainstream users is to map their functional profile onto an independent functional capability scale.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p468）**：
> 「**Such independent scale also takes into account the user's actual range and level of functional capabilities, rather than simply their medical diagnosis. What are used are descriptions of what the user can and cannot do, rather than the medical cause of their impairment.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p468）**：
> 「**For instance, it is not very helpful for the designer to know that certain users have 'arthritis of the knee joint', unless they have the appropriate medical expertise to interpret that diagnosis. Instead it is more useful to know how long the user can stand or walk or how many steps the user can climb.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p468）**：
> 「**This kind of information is unambiguous and thus easier to incorporate into design decisions. Designers are more receptive to learning about possibilities when dealing with less familiar areas, rather than vague and obscure terminologies or constraints.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：能力量表解决了三个问题 —— 识别、抽样、完整性。**
> ① **识别**：不必先判断"这算不算残疾"（那需要医学判断）；
> ② **抽样**：只要能力分数落在需要区间就纳入，不受子群边界限制；
> ③ **完整性**：同一能力分数的多病因用户被合并，不会漏掉某个子群。
> **与 `user-research-methods` 的接口**：它教怎么抽人、怎么访谈；本技能给"用什么标准筛人"（能力分数，而非人口学或诊断）。

### 6.1.3 量化反馈 vs 对比基准

> **原文（Clarkson p469）**：
> 「**Comparative benchmark assessments allow designers to know if the solution being developed is better (or worse) than a competitor's one, it does not provide further information about how much its inclusivity has improved over previous designs. It would be more useful to know how far the present design is from the 'ideal' solution (i.e. something that includes everyone in the intended target population).**」

> **原文（Clarkson p469）**：
> 「**A more effective evaluation of the existing design exclusion suggests that quantitative feedback could give designers a more accurate picture of the level of inclusivity of their designs.**」

> **★★★ 判据：竞品对标只能回答"比它好吗"，不能回答"还差多少"。后者才驱动改进。**
> 这与第 3 章第 4 号比值（Included ÷ Ideal，"最重要"）是同一判断。

### 6.1.4 评估必须迭代，且不能只在末端

> **原文（Clarkson p469）**：
> 「**Whilst some design companies adopt methodologies that advocate a major assessment activity at the end of the design process, what is needed is an iterative cycle of evaluation that the designer applies throughout the creative process. Inadequate and late evaluations often result in poor reactive adaptations to flawed interfaces, rather than genuinely inclusive designs.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p469）**：
> 「**Typically, time and cost constraints imposed on design manufacture makes it unfeasible to retool the manufacturing process to perform changes on the existing design at this stage. Thus the final design may have cumbersome and unattractive features that have been 'fixed' to meet inclusivity requirements, instead of being designed proactively from the outset.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：晚期评估的产物一定是"打补丁"—— `cumbersome and unattractive features that have been 'fixed'`。**
> 这句把 ch05 的 reactive/proactive 二分落到了评估制度上：
> **末端一次大评估（reactive）→ 补丁；全程迭代评估（proactive）→ 本来就好用。**
> 而且补丁不只是难看，往往还会**引入新需求**（回到 ch04 的水壶代际悖论）。

## 6.2 Systematic approach：不接触用户也能做

> **原文（Clarkson p471）**：
> 「**An alternative to the self-observation assessment approach that designers usually carry out using their own intuition and daily experience, is a systematic and structured approach to design assessment (Cardoso et al., 2002). The aim is to scrutinise and capture, in increasing levels of detail, the sequence of functional capabilities that may be needed during user-device interaction.**」

> **★★★ 判据：系统化方法的定位是"替代自省（self-observation）"，不是替代用户研究。**
> 它把设计者的"我试了一下觉得还行"从**直觉**升级成**结构化扫描**。这一步不需要用户、不需要预算，因此是成本最低的起点。

### 6.2.1 做法：建一张交互图

> **原文（Clarkson p471）**：
> 「**The approach can be enhanced, for example, with the use of task- analysis or link-analysis techniques (Stanton and Young, 1999). The procedure starts with the definition of the overall goal(s) that is(are) expected to be achieved by using the product, until the possible sequence(s) of functional capability constituent steps that compose the interaction are identified.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p471）**：
> 「**One way to annotate the interaction narrative is to construct a graphical map of the interaction where, after defining the overall goal and possible contexts of use, the designer builds up the sequence of motor, sensory and cognitive constituent actions taking place. In addition, for each constituent action the product features (e.g. handles, weight, switches), product surroundings (e.g. furniture, cupboards, stairs) and the environmental influences (e.g. level ofluminosity. thermal and auditory conditions) involved are identified.**」

**交互图的三列结构（原书隐含的模板）：**

| 列 | 内容 | 举例（原文） |
|---|---|---|
| **组成动作** | motor / sensory / cognitive 的逐步序列 | — |
| **产品特征** | handles, weight, switches | — |
| **产品周围** | furniture, cupboards, stairs | — |
| **环境影响** | level of luminosity, thermal and auditory conditions | — |

> **★★★★ 判据：每个动作要同时标注三类影响源 —— 产品、周围物、环境。**
> 这条极易被漏：**很多排除不是产品造成的，是"产品 + 周围 + 环境"的组合造成的**（如：微波炉面板不亮，但厨房台面暗 → 组合成排除）。
> 这解释了为什么 ch04 的 Step 1 要求先写使用语境。

### 6.2.2 关键收益：发现"应对策略"

> **原文（Clarkson p471–p472）**：
> 「**Alternatively, different sequences of interaction can be conceived by exploring possible chains of constituent actions that the product(s)** … **would allow users to perform, beyond the expected use. Such structured brainstorming could give designers insight into 'coping' strategies, which would probably be overlooked using random and informal assessments.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：系统化方法最有价值的产出是"想象出用户会怎么绕过设计"—— 即 coping strategies。**
> 这一步**不需要真实用户参与**，只需要设计者被迫按"如果我做不了这一步会怎样"逐格推演。
> 这与 `design-psychology-norman` 的"对缺损用户的思维模拟"同源，但本方法给了它一张有结构的图，避免随意。

### 6.2.3 边界：它只是子集

> **原文（Clarkson p472）**：
> 「**Despite the level of detail of a map of the interaction, it is important to be aware that it will only represent a subset of all the ways of performing certain tasks.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p472）**：
> 「**The main goals of a systematic approach are to increase the validity of evaluation by systematising the designers' typical self-observation approach and minimising the possibility of overlooking any particular aspect of the interaction.**」

> **★★★ 判据：系统化方法的两个目标都是"降低遗漏概率"，不是"保证正确"。**
> 原文用 `minimising the possibility of overlooking`。它不是发现真相的工具，是防止你没想到的工具。

### 6.2.4 优先级的判据

> **原文（Clarkson p472）**：
> 「**Other assessment criteria involve identifying the frequency and severity of certain problems encountered during the interaction. Without relating the product demands to the number of possible users affected, it can be difficult to decide which problems should be given priority.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：优先级需要三个量 —— 频率、严重度、受影响人数。**
> 缺第三个就无法排序（这是第 4 章排除率存在的理由）。**这是一个完整的排序公式的构成要素，原书未给权重，但给了必需要素。**

## 6.3 User observation + interviews：看行为，再问原因

> **原文（Clarkson p472）**：
> 「**User observation involves watching people's behaviour when interacting with products or services in real-life environments, for instance at home, in their workplace, or in a controlled environmental setting such as a usability laboratory. The observer is usually present, yet as 'invisible' as possible throughout the interaction, capturing the events taking place.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p472）**：
> 「**However, despite the degree of validity of the method, it must be noted that the users may perform differently when they know they are being observed.**」

### 6.3.1 三个必做的准备

> **原文（Clarkson p473）**：
> 「**To increase the chances of achieving reliable and valid information with this method, it is important ensure that the users are comfortable with the procedures and aware that what is being tested is eventual difficulties caused by poorly designed attributes and not the users themselves.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：必须明确告诉用户"被测的是设计，不是你"。**
> 这条对老年与残障用户尤其关键 —— 原书在别处指出他们有`propensity ... to blame themselves rather than poor design`（Coleman p178）。
> **不说这一句，观察到的数据会系统性偏向"是我笨"，从而掩盖设计问题。**

> **原文（Clarkson p473）**：
> 「**User observation should be adequately structured so as to enable the classification of the observed behaviour into specific categories, rather than relying solely on the observer's interpretation of the actions.**」

> **★★★ 判据：观察必须有分类框架，否则结论只是观察者的解释。**

### 6.3.2 出声思考（Thinking Aloud）及其偏倚

> **原文（Clarkson p473）**：
> 「**In addition, if users are encouraged to verbalise their thinking process during the interaction, it helps the observer to learn more about behaviour that is not easily observable, for example regarding the usability, the user's motivation or social acceptability issues. Speaking throughout the interaction is known as the 'Thinking Aloud' protocol (Nielsen, 1993).**」

> **原文（Clarkson p473）**：
> 「**Although very useful, it is important to be aware that it may encourage interaction practices that differ from those expected in normal use, when users will not go through the rig our of verbalising their thought processes.**」

> **★★★ 判据：出声思考会改变行为 —— 它本身是一种干预。**
> 因此不能用它来测"自然操作时长"这类指标。

### 6.3.3 应对策略（coping strategies）：观察的金矿与陷阱

> **原文（Clarkson p473）**：
> 「**Usability and accessibility mismatches occurring with everyday interfaces can be captured through user observation. Such mismatches can lead, for instance, to coping strategies that are adopted by the user to help perform a certain task. Sliding heavy objects that were intended to be lifted and using scissors to open packaging that were supposed to be opened by hand are examples of some of these strategies.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p473）**：
> 「**However, coping strategies are not always straightforward to capture through direct observation. Some users may become used to coping with certain design faults, making it difficult for the observer to identify where the problems lie.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p473–p474）**：
> 「**On the other hand, situations where the user is apparently coping with a certain feature may in truth not represent a significant difficulty. For instance, the user may handle a particular object with two hands, when the expected behaviour was to do it only with one hand, suggesting that the object might be too heavy. However, in reality the object might not be particularly heavy and the user just wants extra accuracy when performing a particular task.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：应对策略有两个相反的解读错误 —— 把习惯当正常（漏报），把偏好当困难（误报）。**
> 原文给了具体例子：
> - **漏报**：用户已习惯某个设计缺陷，不再表现出困难 → 观察者看不出问题；
> - **误报**：用户双手拿一个不重的物体，可能只是为了"更准"，不是拿不动。
> **破解手段（原文给的）：** 出声思考 + 事后追问（`the observer can ask the participant to clarify any questions that arise during the observation`，p474）。

> **原文（Clarkson p474）**：
> 「**Any difficulties observed can be related to the seven functional capability scales discussed earlier, and it is possible to start quantifying the levels of capability demanded by the product.**」

> **★★★ 判据：观察结果要立刻回填七轴分数 —— 这样观察就同时是量化输入。**
> 这一步把 ch04 的评估表与观察法打通：观察不只是"发现问题"，是"给出该问题的需求分数"。

### 6.3.4 建可复用的用户画像

> **原文（Clarkson p474）**：
> 「**At the same time, by observing different users, it is possible to build specific user profiles and map their functional capabilities on the scales. Such mapping is useful for later reference throughout the project when it is not feasible to involve users. The capability scale also enables the designer to be aware that some capability impairments might be over- represented or under-represented during user observations.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：把观察到的用户"标定"到能力量表上，可以形成项目内的可复用画像 —— 后期无法再接触用户时仍可用。**
> 这也解决了原书强调的"抽样代表性"问题：不是抽到代表性的人，而是**抽到的每个人都被精确定位在能力谱上**，从而可以推断他们覆盖了哪些区间。
> **附带价值：** 同一张量表能暴露样本偏差 —— `some capability impairments might be over-represented or under-represented`。

### 6.3.5 何时做观察

> **原文（Clarkson p474）**：
> 「**User observations can be performed at milestones in the design process when more realistic assessments are needed. Although user observation is usually carried out when a model or prototype is available, it can also be very useful at the early stages of the design process, when designers are still trying to understand the implications of the problem they want to resolve.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p474）**：
> 「**The main disadvantages of this method are the time and cost involved in preparing the user observation sessions and analysing the results.**」

## 6.4 User trials + questionnaires：让用户自己说

> **原文（Clarkson p475）**：
> 「**In user trials, users are provided with the product and asked to test it, but are not necessarily observed while doing so. Feedback from user trials originates from the users themselves, often in the format of questionnaires as well as interviews.**」

> **★★★ 判据：用户试验与用户观察的分工是"是否观察"—— 试验让用户自然使用，观察记录行为。**
> 二者各有偏倚：试验更自然但反馈依赖用户自述；观察更客观但受观察者影响。

### 6.4.1 自评的系统性偏差（关键警告）

> **原文（Clarkson p475）**：
> 「**Users in general, and particularly older users, tend to either underestimate or overestimate their own functional performance and questionnaires are therefore of limited use for gaining additional feedback. However, if emphasis is put on the problems that the design presents, it might be possible to get a more objective response from the users.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：不要问"你能做到吗"，要问"这个设计哪里难用"—— 前者不可靠，后者可靠。**
> 这是把问卷从"能力自评"改造成"问题定位"的关键转向。
> 与 ch02 的"自陈数据在灵巧度上失真"（Clarkson p376）是同一条经验证据的两次表述。

### 6.4.2 可行性：小样本也行

> **原文（Clarkson p476）**：
> 「**However, it seems unrealistic to expect that designers will be interested in carrying out such complex assessments. Studies carried out in design education report the success of involving small groups of users and running simple user trials to provide insight into user-device interaction.**」

> **★★★ 判据：简单的小规模用户试验已被证明有效 —— 不必等"能做出统计学显著的结果"才开始。**
> 这与 Coleman ch10 的严格样本量（每类 ≥12、每次 ≥50）并不矛盾：**学术研究用严格样本，设计实践用小样本启发。** 两者目的不同。

## 6.5 Checklists and guidelines：能查但不能创新的那类工具

> **原文（Clarkson p476）**：
> 「**Checklists and guidelines are based on information that represents design issues (e.g. ISO, BSI) or specific information about users (e.g. population data). Checklists can be used to stimulate ideas reminding the assessor about a variety of issues that may not have been given proper consideration.**」

### 6.5.1 三种致命局限（原书全列出来了）

> **原文（Clarkson p476）**：
> 「**Although there is no single inclusive design checklist, some general guidelines exist that afford insight into issues that should be considered when designing for a wider population.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p476）**：
> 「**Due to their pre-defined composition, checklists and guidelines may not be suitable if a designer is developing, for example, a completely new product for which there is no previous information available.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p476）**：
> 「**Depending on the knowledge and skill of the assessor, checklists, can be adapted and modified to particular evaluations. However, such adaptability may cause variability in checklist analysis (Stanton and Young, 1998).**」

> **原文（Clarkson p477）**：
> 「**Although, checklists and guidelines can be used at all stages of the design process, at present there is a lack of 'inspiring' inclusive design checklists and guidelines.**」

| 局限 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **① 无单一清单** | `there is no single inclusive design checklist` |
| **② 不适合全新产品** | 预定义构成 → 无先例时失效 |
| **③ 可改编但因此可变** | 不同人改出不同版本 → 结果不可比 |
| **④ 缺乏启发性** | `a lack of 'inspiring' inclusive design checklists` |

> **★★★★ 判据：检查表的三重身份是"防漏"而不是"启发"—— 它提醒你别忘，但不告诉你该创什么。**
> 原文用 `stimulate ideas reminding the assessor`，重点是 **reminding**（提醒）。
> **这与第 1 章"七原则是检查表不是目标函数"完全一致。**

### 6.5.2 替代方案：能力量表检查表

> **原文（Clarkson p477）**：
> 「**Instead of using checklists that are product- or feature-dependent, a capability scale checklist, e.g. the ONS capability scale (Martinet al., 1998), can provide a general basis for assessing a product's functional demands. Capability scales checklists based on population data offer the potential to quantify the number of people who may have difficulty with the product.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：用"能力量表检查表"替代"产品特征检查表"—— 前者还能顺便算出人数。**
> 这是本章最实用的一条替代方案：**把检查表从"逐条打勾"改成"逐轴打分数"**，产物直接就是 ch04 评估表的输入。

### 6.5.3 人体工学指南的具体价值

> **原文（Clarkson p476–p477）**：
> 「**Anthropometric guidelines in particular are important** … **for the evaluation of specific interaction demands on the user. The variation and combination of the product feature dimensions has implications on the required user anthropometric characteristics, which in turn will influence the functional capability profiles supported. For example, depending on the dimensions, location and mechanism of a handle on a door, it is likely that different levels of dexterity, reach and stretch and, possibly, vision capabilities, will be required to use it.**」

> **★★★ 判据：一个门的把手，其"尺寸 + 位置 + 机构"三者共同决定了对灵巧、伸手、视觉的需求分数。**
> 这是把几何参数与能力分数挂钩的最简例子。**可迁移：任何操作件都可以按这三个变量做一张"尺寸→需求分数"的映射表。**

## 6.6 Simulation：让人亲自体验

> **原文（Clarkson p477）**：
> 「**Simulation involves designers using physical simulators to 'reproduce' physical impairments. For example, the designer can use simulators that restrict movement in key parts of the body. While motor and sensory impairments are easier to simulate, it is difficult to simulate cognitive impairments.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p477）**：
> 「**Simulation is probably one of the most attractive assessment methods designers can use due to the immediacy of the experience. It allows designers to feel usability and accessibility limitations physically while interacting with the product.**」

> **★★★ 判据：模拟的最大优势是"即时性（immediacy）"—— 它绕过理解，直接给体验。**
> 这解释了它为何在工业界最受欢迎（ch07 有完整论述）。但也正是"绕过理解"带来风险：体验强烈 ≠ 判断正确。

> **原文（Clarkson p477）**：
> 「**A simulation resource kit offers the benefit of being particularly useful if a quick product test is required. If reasonably calibrated against**」

> **原文（Clarkson p478）**：
> 「**some of the common impairments real users might have, it works like a 'scale' or 'ruler' that the designer can use to find the most critical problems with the user-product interaction.**」

（该句跨 p477–p478；此处按页分两段引用，不改字。）

> **★★★★ 判据：`works like a 'scale' or 'ruler'` —— 模拟器的正确定位是"量具"，不是"体验玩具"。**
> 量具的前提是**标定（calibrated）**。未标定的模拟器给出的优先级排序不可信（ch07 §7.3 详述）。

## 6.7 Expert appraisal：最快也最不可靠

> **原文（Clarkson p478）**：
> 「**Expert appraisal is the evaluation of a product or service by someone who has professional training or experience to make an informed judgement on the design. It is a valuable method for providing analyses that identify areas for improvement. Assessment techniques such as heuristic evaluation and cognitive walkthrough, originally developed for human-computer interaction, can provide a basis for the assessment of inclusivity.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p478）**：
> 「**Experts include usability professionals, engineers, other designers, experts in particular environments, existing users (usually defined as expert users), carers and so forth. It is likely that the experts will have their own modus operandi for assessments, thus the seven capability scales approach discussed earlier in this chapter might not be of relevance.**」

> **★★★ 判据：专家有自己的方法论，可能不用七轴量表 —— 这意味着专家评审结果难以与其他方法合并计算。**
> 这是它最大的实用缺陷：**快，但不可比。**

> **原文（Clarkson p479）**：
> 「**It is preferable to have an internal user champion present throughout the design process, rather than external 'hired help' who may only be contracted in for occasional assessments.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：内部的用户倡导者（user champion）优于外部顾问。**
> 理由：可及性问题在项目里是"随时冒出来的小决定"，外部顾问只在评审点出现，接不住这些决定。
> **这与第 5 章的"responsive designer"是同一条判断的组织版：能力内化 > 服务外购。**

> **原文（Clarkson p479）**：
> 「**Since expert appraisal will depend on the background of the assessor and the evaluation procedures adopted, achieving reliable results often requires the participation of several experts, so that different perspectives and problems can be identified.**」

> **★★★ 判据：单一专家的结论不可靠，须多位不同背景专家。**
> 与 ch04 的"多专家评估需 justification"合起来：**多人 + 逐条理由 = 可复核。**

## 6.8 生命周期与流程表：什么时候用什么

### 6.8.1 全生命周期：不只是"用"的那一段

> **原文（Clarkson p479）**：
> 「**When usability and accessibility assessment methods are usually performed, they focus on the evaluation of the actual use of the product for its principal functional purpose (i.e. product operation). However, design disablement or exclusion, can also happen at less evident levels**」

> **原文（Clarkson p480）**：
> 「**Certain stages of the user-product activity cycle may cause higher levels of exclusion than the operation stages. The commissioning stage, for instance, involves buying the product (i.e. looking for, selecting, and transporting the product) and installing it (i.e. unpacking, assembling, and connecting it to power, etc.) may present serious problems to many people. Likewise, within product operation, the maintenance stage can sometimes be difficult to carry out, irrespective of whether the product is generally easy to use.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：`Certain stages ... may cause higher levels of exclusion than the operation stages` —— 排除的高发区常常不在"使用"，而在"安装"与"维护"。**
> 这是全书最反直觉、也最容易省成本的一条判据：
> - **开箱/安装**：包装难开、说明难懂、组装步骤多 → 这一步排除的人可能比"能不能用"更多；
> - **维护**：换滤芯、换电池、清洗 → 常常整机好用但维护做不了。
> **可执行动作：在排除审计里，先把生命周期的每一段单独评一次，再找最高排除段。**

**图注还原（图 27.1，Clarkson p479）「Product life cycle：A map of the product development and user-product activity cycles」：**

图中并列两条链：

- **Product development cycle**（产品开发周期）：`Design process` → `Implementation`
- **User-product activity cycle**（用户—产品活动周期）：`Design brief` → `Design` → `Implementation` → `Commissioning` → `Operation` → `Decommissioning`

（注：图中文字在 OCR 中部分重叠，以上为可读出的全部标签；**未读出的层级细节不做补猜**。）

**ADL / IADL 分层（原文 p480）：**

> **原文（Clarkson p480）**：
> 「**Occupational therapy theories describe ways of measuring user independence as the capacity to perform functional tasks necessary to meet the demands of daily life. These activities of daily living (ADLs) are categorised into basic and higher level ADLs. The basic ADLs are those that are necessary for individuals to care for themselves within a limited environment (e.g. eating and dressing). Higher-level, or Instrumental, ADLs (IADLs) are those activities necessary to function in the community (e.g. using a phone, paying bills and shopping).**」

| 层 | 定义 | 例子 | 产品含义 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ADL（基础）** | 在有限环境内自理 | 进食、穿衣 | 生存线 |
| **IADL（工具性）** | 在社区中运作 | 用电话、缴费、购物 | **产品的主战场** |

> **原文（Clarkson p480）**：
> 「**In IADLs, a large number of devices are used to support the efficient, comfortable and pleasant fulfilment of a variety of everyday tasks. However, the majority of those devices, particularly the ones used in domestic environments, have usability and accessibility shortcomings for people who are not fully able-bodied.**」

> **★★★ 判据：家用设备绝大多数在 IADL 上存在可用性与可及性缺陷 —— 包括那些"看起来很简单"的设备。**
> 原文用 `the majority of those devices`。

### 6.8.2 阶段 × 方法对照表（原书唯一的流程表）

> **原文（Clarkson p482）**：
> 「**Assessment methods can be used at all stages of the design process. Problem definition Systematic approach Observation Interviews User trials Questionnaires Simulation Expert appraisal Conceptual design Systematic approach Interviews Questionnaires Checklist and guidelines Simulation Expert appraisal Design Detail development design Systematic Systematic approach approach Observation Observation Interviews Interviews User trials User trials Questionnaires Questionnaires Checklist and Checklist and guidelines guidelines Simulation Simulation Expert appraisal Expert appraisal**」

**图注还原（Table 27.2，Clarkson p482）「Product assessment：Assessment methods can be used at all stages of the design process」：**

| 阶段 | 可用方法 |
|---|---|
| **Problem definition**（问题定义） | Systematic approach、Observation、Interviews、User trials、Questionnaires、Simulation、Expert appraisal |
| **Conceptual design**（概念设计） | Systematic approach、Interviews、Questionnaires、Checklist and guidelines、Simulation、Expert appraisal |
| **Design development**（设计发展） | Systematic approach、Observation、Interviews、User trials、Questionnaires、Checklist and guidelines、Simulation、Expert appraisal |

> **★★★★ 判据：Observation / User trials 在"问题定义"阶段也可用（用现有竞品做），但从"概念设计"阶段起不可用（还没有东西可测）。**
> 表格的这个空档非常有用：**概念期不要去找用户测"我还没做出来的东西"，改用访谈 + 问卷 + 检查表 + 模拟 + 专家评审。**

> **原文（Clarkson p482）**：
> 「**Table 2 7. 2 presents a summary of where the assessment methods can be implemented within the design process. This is not meant to be a recipe for assessment, since different companies have different design procedures.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p482）**：
> 「**It also suggests that adopting approaches that implement assessments only towards the end of the project are insufficient.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p482）**：
> 「**On the other hand, if the client is not conversant with the need for more thorough and realistic assessments, it is unlikely that the design consultancy will be allocated the financial resources necessary to do it.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：评估能否做，取决于客户是否理解 —— 不是取决于设计团队是否想做。**
> 这条把评估问题从"方法问题"转成"商务问题"：**设计方若要争取评估预算，必须先把客户教育到位。**

### 6.8.3 快慢分层

> **原文（Clarkson p481）**：
> 「**Methods that are likely to be more time-consuming and expensive (e.g. user observation, user trials and expert appraisal) can be applied at key milestones of the design for additional feedback, while others (e.g. systematic approach, simulation, questionnaires, interviews, hecklists and guidelines), can be used for frequent, quick assessments.**」

| 层 | 方法 | 频率 |
|---|---|---|
| **慢/贵（里程碑用）** | User observation、User trials、Expert appraisal | 关键节点 |
| **快/便宜（日常用）** | Systematic approach、Simulation、Questionnaires、Interviews、Checklists and guidelines | **频繁** |

> **★★★ 判据：日常高频用便宜方法，里程碑用昂贵方法 —— 这是评估资源分配的唯一正确结构。**
> 反例（常见错误）：只在最后做一次昂贵的用户试验 —— 那就是 ch06 §6.1.4 说的"补丁式评估"。

## 6.9 专家评审 + 排除审计的组合（Coleman ch12）

> **原文（Coleman p209）**：
> 「**Expert Appraisal is the broad review of a product's features coupled with a detailed task-based interaction analysis to highlight potential usability and accessibility issues. It is usually undertaken by someone who has the professional training or experience to make an informed judgement on the design, for example, usability professionals, engineers, designers, experts in the product or its particular environment, or existing users.**」

> **原文（Coleman p210）**：
> 「**Although expert appraisal can be carried out informally, it often involves checking a design against predeﬁ ned guidelines or recognised principles, and is likely to be accompanied by a life cycle and task analysis.**」

> **原文（Coleman p210）**：
> 「**Expert appraisal is usually used to detect critical problems before a product is released for more thorough, and potentially more expensive, evaluation. However, it can be applied at any stage of the design process. The quality of the appraisal depends on the availability of appropriate experts and their knowledge of the users and of the circumstances of use of the product. The participation of several specialists is recommended, in order that a variety of perspectives and problems can be identiﬁ ed.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：专家评审的标准组合是「广扫产品特征 + 细查任务交互」两段，且通常配生命周期分析。**
> 单做前一段（看外观特征）会漏掉流程性问题；单做后一段会漏掉静态特征问题。

> **原文（Coleman p211）**：
> 「**An exclusion audit is used to estimate quantitative measures of design exclusion, indicating how many people can use a product and how many are excluded from its use.**」

> **原文（Coleman p198）**：
> 「**The concept of an exclusion audit (see Chapter 12) has also been developed to combine the exclusion analysis described above with an expert analysis of the product interaction process and trials with actual users. This approach has proven to be particularly successful if the users involved in the trials are 'boundary' users, that is those who are right on the limit of being able to use the product. However, there remains a need to develop the means to train the expert assessors and provide guidance on the selection of suitable users for the trials.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：排除审计 = 排除分析（量化）+ 专家交互分析 + 真实用户试验，三者合一，且用户必须是 boundary users。**
> 这是本章的"集成方法"，也是唯一同时给出"多少人""为什么""确实吗"三件事的组合。
> **原书自陈的缺口：** `there remains a need to develop the means to train the expert assessors` —— **专家如何训练，书中没有。**

### 6.9.1 一个完整的双方法案例：手机

> **原文（Coleman p214）**：
> 「**The ﬁ rst stage in addressing this challenge was to undertake a broad-based accessibility audit of ﬁ ve mobile phones, comprising: detailed life cycle and task analyses of key phone functions from 1. purchasing through set up to use; expert appraisal, by two independent teams, of each product and 2. its use throughout its life cycle; an exclusion audit of each product and its use throughout its life 3. cycle; user trials of each product with older users; 4. consolidation of the assessment results and development of a 5. business case and requirements for an improved product.**」

（原文在每条之前夹排了序号 `1.`–`5.`，此处照录。）

| 步 | 动作 | 方法类型 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 生命周期 + 任务分析（从购买到设置到使用） | 系统化 |
| 2 | **两个独立团队**做专家评审（全生命周期） | 专家评审 |
| 3 | 每个产品全生命周期的排除审计 | 量化 |
| 4 | 老年用户试验 | 用户试验 |
| 5 | 汇总 + 商业论证 + 改进需求 | 收口 |

> **★★★★ 判据：`expert appraisal, by two independent teams` —— 两个独立团队，不是两个专家。**
> 这是防止专家评审系统性偏倚的实操办法（单团队会形成内部共识）。
> **这五步就是"集成方法"的可照抄模板。**

> **原文（Coleman p214）**：
> 「**However, it also reminded the team that users with impairments still want phones that are small, stylish and desirable. Compromise will be necessary, but inclusive design can inform the compromise to improve the end result.**」

> **★★★ 判据：可及性与"小、时尚、想要"是必须妥协的两端；包容性设计的作用是"让妥协更优"，不是"取消妥协"。**
> 这条对产品团队的预期管理极重要：**不存在"既要又要"的干净答案。**

### 6.9.2 家政用品的完整流程（另一模板）

> **原文（Coleman p215）**：
> 「**As a result they: attended an inclusive design • conference; sent key management, design, • marketing and production personnel on a 1-day general inclusive design training course; read key texts on inclusive design; • undertook extensive user trials to • observe customers using existing products; encouraged staff to identify good and • bad examples of inclusive design; commissioned the Royal College of Art and The Helen Hamlyn • Centre to organise a workshop with extreme users and designers; developed a number of novel product packaging concepts. •**」

（原文在每条之间夹排了项目符号 `•`，此处照录。）

> **原文（Coleman p215）**：
> 「**In addition, they identiﬁ ed a number of products for further evaluation and redesign. For each product the team undertook a market survey, limited user trials and used product with 'gloves and glasses' simulators. They also undertook an expert appraisal and exclusion audit for each product and its use throughout its life cycle, identifying limiting tasks and reasons for exclusion. Finally, exclusion targets for a more inclusive product were set and a number of improved design concepts proposed, which were subsequently subjected to a further exclusion audit.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：请注意最后一句 —— 改进方案出来后，**再做一次排除审计**。**
> 这是"设定排除目标 → 改 → 复测"的闭环，而不是"改完就算"。
> **这是本章唯一给出的"目标设定"做法：先设一个排除率目标，改完复测验证是否达标。**

> **原文（Coleman p216）**：
> 「**Typical ﬁ ndings from such evaluations included: all products had a strong brand • identity that assisted their location in the supermarket, but product variants were not always as easy to distinguish; instructions for use were typically • compromised by attempts to make packs multi-lingual, resulting in the use of small fonts and ﬁ gures; much of the packaging was • impenetrable without the aid of a sharp object and set up procedures tended to be overly complex; product features designed to assist use were often not visible due to • lack of colour contrast; product use was compromised by the need for two-handed • operation or understanding of overly complex use sequences; simple changes to the product or packaging design could potentially • yield much lower levels of exclusion or difﬁ culty in using the products.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：这六条实际发现，是"可及性缺陷的高发清单"，可直接当检查项用。**
> ① 品牌识别强但**变体难区分**（同类产品长得太像）；
> ② **多语言化 → 字号变小**（一个可及性改进（多语言）如何制造了另一个可及性问题）；
> ③ 包装**非利器不可开**，设置流程过复杂；
> ④ 辅助使用的特征**因对比不足而看不见**（有这个功能，但看不见等于没有）；
> ⑤ **需双手操作**或使用序列过复杂；
> ⑥ **简单改动即可大幅降低排除**。
> 其中②最值得记住：**它证明了"改进"可以互相抵消**（回到 ch04 水壶代际悖论）。
