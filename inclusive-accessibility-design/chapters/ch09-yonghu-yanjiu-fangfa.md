## 9.0 总纲：为什么不是"方法越多越好"

> **原文（Coleman p167）**：
> 「**Many user research methods are derived from basic methods, so designers should not feel overwhelmed by the large numbers of methods with various names.**」

> **★★★★ 判据（本章的核心主张）：几十种方法都只是三种基本动作的变体 —— 问（ask）、看（observe）、参与（participate）。**
> 这条判断解决了一个真实困境：方法名录越长，团队越不敢开始。原书用一次分类把名录压缩成三格，并明确授权：

> **原文（Coleman p156）**：
> 「**The advantage of classifying these numerous user research methods into three basic types is that it emphasises an essential activity (that is 'ask', 'observe' or 'participate') and gives freedom for designers to create their own means of understanding the user.**」

> **★★★ 判据：分类的目的是"授权自造方法"，不是"规范方法"。**
> `gives freedom for designers to create their own means` —— 这是全章最实用的一句：**方法可以自己发明，只要它属于问/看/参与之一。**

## 9.1 三分类：Forum / Representation / Co-design

> **原文（Coleman p154）**：
> 「**Designer-orientated user research methods can be categorised into three basic forms: Forum • : the basic activity is 'to ask'; methods include interviews, focus groups, and those derived from focus groups, and so on; Representation • : the basic activity is 'to observe'; methods include ethnography, simulation, personas, and so on, as well as user data such as anthropometric data and user capability data; Co-design • : the basic activity is 'to participate'; methods include empowerment games, development panel, co-decision, and so on.**」

| 类 | 基本动作 | 代表方法（原文） | 关键特征 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Forum**（论坛型） | **问** | 访谈、焦点小组及其衍生 | 用户说给你听 |
| **Representation**（表征型） | **看** | 人种志、模拟、persona、**人体测量数据、能力数据** | 你观察或读取 |
| **Co-design**（共创型） | **参与** | 赋权游戏、开发小组、共同决策 | 用户与你共同决定 |

> **★★★★ 判据：`personas` 和 `anthropometric data` 与"观察"归为一类 —— 因为它们都是"表征"，不是"收集"。**
> 这条归类很有洞察：**数据表和人物画像都是"已有观察的固化物"**。它们不需要重新接触用户，因此**成本最低**，但也最容易过时。
> **产品含义：项目中期无法接触用户时，正确做法是回到 Representation 类（读表征），而不是凭记忆。**

### 9.1.1 方法的谱系（同一基本动作的不同包装）

> **原文（Coleman p155）**：
> 「**'Focus group' has evolved into an extensive family of related methods (Ireland, 2003), such as: 'one-on-one interview' • 'dyads' (or 'friendship pairs') • 'triads' (three people) • 'mini focus groups' (six to eight consumers) • 'party group' and 'super groups' (50−100 or more people) • 'online discussion group'. •**」

> **原文（Coleman p155）**：
> 「**'Ethnography' can take forms of: 'ﬁ eld ethnography' • 'digital ethnography' (or so-called Digital Ethno) • 'photo ethnography' • 'ethnofuturism' • 'real world ethnographic enactments'. •**」

> **原文（Coleman p155）**：
> 「**'User participation' can be categorised in seven major forms (Sanoff, 1992): representation (designer represents the user) • questionnaires • regionalism (with consideration of speciﬁ c cultural heritage within • a geographically limited area) dialogue (user consultation) • alternative (users are given the choice of several alternatives within • a ﬁ xed set of boundaries) co-decision (direct and active user participation) • self-decision (the decision is made by the users themselves). •**」

**参与程度的七级阶梯（原书引用 Sanoff 1992）—— 这是本章最有操作价值的一张表：**

| 级 | 形式 | 谁做决定 | 用户投入度 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **7** | **self-decision**（自决） | **用户自己** | 最高 |
| **6** | **co-decision**（共同决策） | 用户 + 设计者 | 高 |
| **5** | **alternative**（多选一） | 设计者定边界，用户选 | 中 |
| **4** | **dialogue**（对话/咨询） | 设计者 | 中低 |
| **3** | **regionalism**（地域文化考量） | 设计者 | 低 |
| **2** | **questionnaires**（问卷） | 设计者 | 低 |
| **1** | **representation**（设计者代表用户） | **设计者** | **无** |

> **★★★★ 判据：这是一条"参与阶梯"，从"设计者代表用户"到"用户自己决定"共七级。**
> **用途：判断一个项目声称"有用户参与"时，它在第几级。**
> 大多数被称为"用户参与"的做法实际停在第 1–2 级（设计者代表 + 问卷）。
> **第 3 级的 `regionalism` 特别值得注意**：它提醒**文化差异也是一种参与维度**（回到第 3 章：包容性不只是能力，还有文化）。

> **原文（Coleman p155）**：
> 「**It should be noted here that many user research methods are derived from basic ones; depending on the numbers of users recruited or speciﬁ c tools/ techniques used, they may have various names.**」

> **★★★ 判据：名字不同往往只是"人数不同"或"工具不同"，不构成新方法。**
> 所以不必为"超焦点小组"（50–100 人）与"迷你焦点小组"（6–8 人）分别学一套流程。

## 9.2 好工具的四条性格（可直接用于自造工具）

> **原文（Coleman p156）**：
> 「**The IDEO Method Cards have the following characteristics: Visual • (use of visual aids such as pictures) Versatile • (different cards can be used for different projects) Inspiring • (not too descriptive, open for interpretation) Playful/fun • (card-sorting can be a fun experience).**」

> **原文（Coleman p156）**：
> 「**These can be regarded as successful characteristics of designer-orientated methods and tools.**」

| # | 性格 | 原文 | 含义 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Visual** | 用图片等视觉辅助 | 设计师的母语是图，不是表格 |
| 2 | **Versatile** | 不同卡片可用于不同项目 | 一个工具能跨项目复用 |
| 3 | **Inspiring** | `not too descriptive, open for interpretation` | **不能太描述性**，要留解释空间 |
| 4 | **Playful/fun** | 卡片分类可以是件有趣的事 | 有趣提高使用率 |

> **★★★★ 判据：第 3 条最反直觉 —— `not too descriptive`（不能太具体）。**
> 这与工程工具的评价标准相反（工程工具越精确越好）。理由：**工具若把答案说尽，设计者就只是在执行；留白才有解释与创造的空间。**
> 再加上第五条（下一节）：

> **原文（Coleman p156）**：
> 「**Designer-orientated methods should also take into account time constraints for design projects. As Plowman (2003) argued, when companies like IBM, IDEO, Apple, Design Continuum, Cheskin, Intel, Xerox, Herman Miller and Microsoft say they conduct ethnographic research, they are not conducting ethnographic research in the same way as academic ethnographers. It is simply not practical for business entities to invest the same amount of time or to engage in the same form or extent of ethnography as is practised by academics.**」

> **原文（Coleman p157）**：
> 「**another characteristic of a designer-orientated user research method is to be 'practical'.**」

> **★★★★ 判据（第五条）：`practical` —— 连 IBM、IDEO、Apple 都不做学术式人种志。**
> 原书用这份名单直接消解了"我们做不起真正的研究"的心理负担：**这些顶级公司做的也不是学术版本。**
> **判据形式：设计用的方法必须能在项目周期内完成；做不到就换方法，而不是假装做了学术研究。**

## 9.3 三例之一：Task Analysis（任务分析）

### 9.3.1 定义与双重身份

> **原文（Coleman p157）**：
> 「**Task analysis is a process by which detailed information is gathered from users about what they are required to do, in terms of actions and/or cognitive processes, to achieve a task objective. More simply, it is used to gain an understanding of what people do in the tasks and jobs they carry out (Shepherd and Stammers, 2005).**」

> **原文（Coleman p157）**：
> 「**It can be used as both an interview method ('to ask') and an observational method ('to observe'), depending on what type of information needs to be collected and what is to be done with it. Using both interview and observation, however, will lead to even richer data, especially when combined with other user research methods.**」

> **★★★ 判据：任务分析是唯一横跨"问"与"看"两类的工具 —— 因此它是包容性设计里性价比最高的单个方法。**
> 这与第 5 章 Level 2（功能规格）推荐"task analysis"、第 6 章 Systematic approach 的核心都是它，三处呼应。

### 9.3.2 HTA 与停止规则

> **原文（Coleman p158）**：
> 「**There are a number of different techniques of task analysis, for example Hierarchical Task Analysis (HTA), ﬁ rst proposed by Annett and Duncan (1967). This form of task analysis examines what people need to do to attain a particular goal, breaking it down into sub-tasks, in a hierarchical form in order of complexity until a suitable stopping place is reached ('stopping rule'), and following a plan on when to do each sub-task.**」

> **原文（Coleman p158）**：
> 「**When to stop the analysis is down to the analyst and the purpose to which the data are to be used, so the stopping rule is ﬂ exible.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：HTA 的关键不是"拆多细"，而是**停止规则**（stopping rule）—— 由目的决定，不是由技术极限决定。**
> **可执行问答：** 拆到"能判断该步的能力需求分数"为止（因为下一步要交给七轴评估）。

### 9.3.3 提问清单（本章最可直接抄用的一段）

> **原文（Coleman p158）**：
> 「**In addition to interviewing and observing the users, further details about the task can be elicited through verbal protocol analysis, or 'thinking aloud' (Robson, 2002), whereby users can be asked to think aloud their thought processes as they actually perform a task. What are they doing? Why? How are they going about it? Can they easily see, hear, feel, and understand what needs to be done? When do they go to the next operation? Is it difﬁ cult? Is it boring? Is it enjoyable? Are any special skills or abilities needed? Are any aids or support required to do the task, for example, reading glasses or human help?**」

> **★★★★ 判据：这组问题就是"能力需求访谈"的标准脚本，且最后一个问题直接问出了辅助需求。**
> 逐个对应能力轴：
> - `Can they easily see, hear, feel` → **感官轴**
> - `understand what needs to be done` → **认知轴**
> - `Are any special skills or abilities needed` → **机动/技能**
> - `reading glasses or human help` → **辅助手段（= 已被排除的证据）**

### 9.3.4 记录方法：PTR 与配色编码

> **原文（Coleman p158）**：
> 「**While interviewing users, it is important to take notes to record the tasks and behaviours. One such note-taking method is called Personalised Task Representation (PTR) (Gregory, 1979), where rough notes, probably on large sheets of paper, full of scribbles and corrections, can later be represented more clearly to check their accuracy with the users. This is where some creativity can be shown, for example, loops can be included in the notes, indicating yes/no options, and colours can be used to indicate critical issues of time, visual/audio inputs, information availability, and so on.**」

> **原文（Coleman p158）**：
> 「**These may highlight areas which could be particularly problematic for users, as illustrated by a 'quick and dirty' PTR (Figure 9.5) to identify an older person's difﬁ culties when going on a bus journey (Dekker, Nicolle and Molenbroek, 2004). Colour coding was used to indicate where timing was important, and where additional information was required by the traveller. The numbers above the diagram referred to a list of possible solutions to each of the issues or difﬁ culties.**」

**图注还原（图 9.5，Coleman p158）「Using task analysis to plan a bus trip」**：一张手绘的乘公交流程 PTR 图；用颜色标出"时间关键处"与"乘客需要额外信息处"；图上方的编号对应问题解决方案清单。（**具体颜色与编号内容在 OCR 中不可读，未录**。）

> **★★★ 判据：PTR 的价值在于"用颜色编码四类关键问题"—— 时间、视觉/听觉输入、信息可得性。**
> 这三个编码类目可以直接搬用；它们恰好覆盖了"通路任务"（第 3 章）最易失效的三处。

### 9.3.5 实例：修剪树篱 → 背带式与加长杆

> **原文（Coleman p159）**：
> 「**Brown's work at the Helen Hamlyn Centre designing garden power tools for B&Q illustrates how task analysis can help designers think through usability issues when designing a product. Integrating task analysis with observational research, he broke down the steps required to climb a ladder and trim a hedge with an electric hedge trimmer.**」

> **原文（Coleman p159）**：
> 「**He found these issues fell into the following broad categories: assembly, handling, lifting, applying force (for example to operate the trigger or remove the battery), storage and safety.**」

> **原文（Coleman p159）**：
> 「**For example, the weight of the hedge trimmer, largely due to the motor (and battery in the case of the cordless version), made it difﬁ cult to handle, especially at arm's length. This discovery led directly to the 'backpack hedge trimmer' concept, where the battery and motor are mounted on the user's back on a backpack. A ﬂ exible drive shaft powers the handheld cutting head which becomes a small fraction of the weight of the original product.**」

> **原文（Coleman p159–p160）**：
> 「**People can also ﬁ nd it hard to do the reaching required in cutting hedges – reaching down to the ground, reaching up to the top of hedges and reaching over** … **ﬂ owerbeds. Robert's research highlighted this issue and inspired a concept for an extended hedge trimmer, where the cutter is mounted on the end of an extension bar at an angle. This makes all reaching operations much easier, reducing the reach, stretch and strength demands made by the product.**」

> **原文（Coleman p160）**：
> 「**In many cases, this idea means the user can reach the top of a hedge without needing to climb a ladder, and so this (often dangerous) task step is avoided completely.**」

**两条设计产出与它们的推导路径：**

| 发现 | 设计对策 | 消除了什么需求 |
|---|---|---|
| 重量大，伸展手臂時难操作 | **背带式**：电机与电池移到背上 | **lifting + handling 的力需求** |
| 伸手够不到（地面/顶部/花坛对面） | **加长杆 + 斜置刀头** | **reach / stretch / strength 需求；并彻底消除了"爬梯子"这一步** |

> **★★★★ 判据：`this (often dangerous) task step is avoided completely` —— 最好的可及性改进是**删掉那一步**，而不是让它更容易。**
> 这是本章最有分量的设计原则：**当某一步既危险又困难时，正确动作是消灭该步骤，而非优化它。**
> 这与第 6 章"减少动作次数"（ch07 §7.6.3 的"动作变成需要预谋的事"）配成一对。

### 9.3.6 软件化：任务分析直接算出排除人数

> **原文（Coleman p160）**：
> 「**Sprout Design and the Cambridge Engineering Design Centre are developing a software-based tool (Figure 9.7) that uses task analysis to estimate the number of people in the UK who would be excluded from using any particular product. A simple building-block graphical interface guides operators in constructing their own task analysis, using input from real users if available.**」

> **原文（Coleman p160）**：
> 「**Tasks are broken into steps and the difﬁ culty of each task step is then assessed in terms of the cognitive, sensory and motion capability a person would need to carry out that step. People who do not have that level of capability are excluded. Results are shown graphically and the task analysis serves to highlight the steps where the most people will have difﬁ culty.**」

> **原文（Coleman p160）**：
> 「**Improvements to the design can be assessed in real time, the software instantly showing the impact on exclusion. Knowing what proportion of a company's market their products are excluding can also be a powerful business case for adopting a more inclusive approach to design.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：这就是第 4 章四步法的工具化形态 —— 任务分析 → 每步三轴打分 → 排除人数 → 实时显示改进效果。**
> **产品含义：这套流程今天是可自动化的**（只要有人口能力分布数据 + 一个打分界面）。原书 2007 年已在做，是"设计辅助工具"而非纯方法。

### 9.3.7 Task analysis 的五条性格评价

> **原文（Coleman p161）**：
> 「**As a designer-orientated user research method, task analysis has the following characteristics: Visual • : It can be presented in either graphical form, or in page or sheet form. Versatile • : It can be used during the analysis phase or in planning evaluation studies; for both existing systems or a hypothetical product; information elicited from users through, for example, 'interviews' or 'observation'; can use ﬂ exible forms of note-taking and representation. Inspiring • : Decomposition of tasks is useful to explore how activities ﬁ t into a wider context; in later stages of the development the current solution can be checked against the original task or activity analysis to see how the design deviates from the intended solution, and what consequences this leads to.**」

> **★★★ 判据：`Inspiring` 这一条给了一个具体用法 —— 用原始任务分析做后期校验，看设计偏离了多少、后果是什么。**
> 这是一个"回头对表"的动作：**任务分析不只是前期工具，也是后期验收的基准。**

## 9.4 三例之二：Persona（人物画像）

> **原文（Coleman p162）**：
> 「**Personas are hypothetical archetypes, representing real people throughout the design process. They are deﬁ ned with signiﬁ cant rigour and precision, backed up by actual user research data (Cooper, 1999). Personas can be used as both a design tool and a communication tool. It is a typical 'Representation'-type user research method.**」

> **原文（Coleman p162）**：
> 「**A persona description includes typical work ﬂ ow, attitudes, skills, frustrations, goals, and environment. A persona set (in general between two and seven**」

> **原文（Coleman p163）**：
> 「**The primary value of personas is in focusing the design on the most typical users to optimise the product for them. Its purpose is 'to get everyone thinking in a uniﬁ ed way from a user's perspective'.**」

| 要素 | 原文要求 |
|---|---|
| 内容 | `typical work ﬂ ow, attitudes, skills, frustrations, goals, and environment` |
| 数量 | **2–7 个** |
| 两个用途 | 设计工具 + 沟通工具 |
| 核心价值 | 聚焦、统一视角 |

> **★★★★ 判据：`between two and seven personas` —— 数量上限是"可管理"，不是"覆盖度"。**
> 这与第 2 章的抽样困境互补：**persona 不追求代表性覆盖，它追求的是"让全队盯住同一个人"。**

### 9.4.1 一个实证案例（CITD 七口之家）

> **原文（Coleman p163）**：
> 「**A programme sponsored by the DTI was undertaken by the Centre for Inclusive Technology and Design (CITD) in early 2005 to understand the level of awareness of Inclusive Design in industry and deliver a high impact training package to eight major companies in the UK. Persona cards were produced by the project team, representing seven members of a ﬁ ctional family. The characters reﬂ ected different ages, lifestyles and levels of capability.**」

> **原文（Coleman p163）**：
> 「**Each persona raised speciﬁ c usability issues – for example, Jenny is a single mother with an active eighteen-month-old son and a 4-year-old daughter. Consequently her time is very precious and she has little patience with anything that unnecessarily wastes her time. She is also concerned with the safety of her children.**」

> **原文（Coleman p163）**：
> 「**Other persona cards covered issues such as a broken wrist, arthritis, deafness, visual impairments and reduced intellectual function. The results from these workshops showed that the cards stimulated lively discussion and directly inspired new ideas for how to make the products easier to use.**」

**七张卡覆盖的条件（原文点名的）：**

| # | 画像 | 覆盖什么 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Jenny**（单亲母亲，1 岁半 + 4 岁孩子） | **时间压力 + 安全性**（注意：不是能力缺陷） |
| 2 | 腕骨骨折 | **临时性损伤** |
| 3 | 关节炎 | 灵巧/力量 |
| 4 | 耳聋 | 听觉 |
| 5 | 视觉受损 | 视觉 |
| 6 | 智力功能下降 | 认知 |

> **★★★★ 判据：第一张卡不是残障者，而是"单亲母亲" —— 这个选择本身就是方法论。**
> 它说明：**能力受限的主要来源不只是疾病，还包括处境（时间、注意力、同时照护）。**
> 这与第 3 章 DTV 案例的 (c) 组（技术恐惧，非能力问题）是同一判断。
> **可执行判据：persona 组里至少要有一个"没有身体损伤但处境受限"的人。**

> **★★★ 判据：`a broken wrist`（腕骨骨折）说明临时性损伤必须被覆盖 —— 这是"每个人都会经历"的现实（回到第 2 章能力连续统）。**

## 9.5 三例之三：Informance（信息化表演）

> **原文（Coleman p165）**：
> 「**Informance is a short word for 'informative performance'. It is a set of techniques in which actors and/or researchers study what is known about consumers and role-play potential consumers. Informance subsumes both ethnography and empathy.**」

> **原文（Coleman p165）**：
> 「**The goal of 'informance' goes beyond understanding consumers' culture or even having an 'inside' understanding of consumers. Its goal is to create, through performance, characters that can speak about their world, express informed opinions about product features, answer questions about design possibilities, and even design products.**」

> **★★★ 判据：informance 的目标不是"理解用户"，而是**让用户角色能参与设计**—— 回答设计问题、甚至提出方案。**
> 这是三例中唯一进入 Co-design 类（同时含 Representation）的方法。

### 9.5.1 步骤

> **原文（Coleman p165）**：
> 「**Informance begins with ethnographic study – questioning and observing people in particular consumer segments. Researchers move on to interpreting their data through empathy: seeing situations, uses, and elements as the studied consumers would see them. The next step is informance itself: acts of pretending which transform empathy into action.**」

三步：**人种志研究 → 共情解释 → 表演转化（把共情变成行动）**。

> **原文（Coleman p165）**：
> 「**The idea of informance is to allow typical consumers to say what they cannot say themselves by transforming them into characters in a performance.**」

> **原文（Coleman p165）**：
> 「**These consumers become 'we' rather than 'they', and as such, join the design team.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：`become 'we' rather than 'they'` —— 这是共情方法在语言层面的验收标准。**
> 一个可用的判据：**项目会议里谈到用户时用的是"他们"还是"我们"？如果是"他们"，共情还没发生。**

### 9.5.2 何时特别需要 informance

> **原文（Coleman p166）**：
> 「**Two situations especially call for informance. Design for entirely new categories of products calls for informance because new kinds of products are particularly difﬁ cult for most people to imagine. Informance is also particularly useful with potential users who have little in common with the designers. A product may be for a segment that is demographically remote from the designer; for example, 30-year-old designers creating products for people who are 70; or a product may be intended as 'worldwide' and thus culturally remote from any individual designer (Dishman, 2003).**」

> **★★★★ 判据：informance 的两个必需场景 —— ① 全新品类（用户无法想象）；② 用户与设计者差异极大（人口学或文化上遥远）。**
> 第二条正好命中包容性设计的经典情境（30 岁设计者为 70 岁用户设计）。**因此 informance 对本书主题有特殊价值。**

### 9.5.3 一个实证：ElderSpace

> **原文（Coleman p165–p166）**：
> 「**A good example of the successful use of informance is the ElderSpace project (Dishman, 2003). The research team, comprising three social scientists, one artist, and an industrial designer, set out to produce informance after spending weeks analysing videos and photos from the nursing home. They each adopted** … **a character based upon representative combinations of actual people they had studied (that is personas). Using theatrical techniques and through scripting, memorising, embodying and acting out the lives of those personas in a fully staged set, they learned more by 'doing' and 'being' those characters than they could have by any other means.**」

> **原文（Coleman p166）**：
> 「**As a design process, most saw informance as having enormous power in two areas: 1) helping the designer to consider how each individual user is embedded in larger social systems; and 2) forcing technologists to evaluate the whole electronic ecosystem and interaction infrastructure when trying to bring in new technologies.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：informance 的两个产出 —— ① 让设计者看到"个体嵌在更大的社会系统中"；② 迫使技术方评估整个生态而非单个设备。**
> 第二条对产品团队极重要：**它把设计单位从"产品"推到"系统"**（回到第 6 章生命周期与第 3 章 IDC 的系统观）。

## 9.6 实操与伦理：Coleman ch10

### 9.6.1 五条工作原则

> **原文（Coleman p171）**：
> 「**Inclusive design requires a holistic approach. For example, travel is made up of a chain of events. Before the design of the train, coach or bus is even considered, passengers must be able to book tickets easily, to get to the station, ﬁ nd and use an accessible toilet and so on. Every aspect of the journey needs to be accessible if the journey is to be problem-free.**」

> **★★★★ 判据（TAKE A HOLISTIC APPROACH）：`Every aspect of the journey needs to be accessible if the journey is to be problem-free` —— 链条强度等于最弱环。**
> 这是"整体法"的正式表述，与第 3 章"通路任务"呼应：**一个环节不可及，整段旅程不可及。**

> **原文（Coleman p171）**：
> 「**However the focus of inclusive design is not disability. Rather it aims to eliminate disability caused by design by making sure that as many people as possible can use a product, irrespective of the nature of their impairment. The social model of disability demonstrates that it is poor design that turns an impairment into a disability. Good design can make the impairment irrelevant to use of the**」

> **原文（Coleman p172）**：
> 「**product.**」

（该句跨 p171–p172；此处按页分两段引用，不改字。）

> **原文（Coleman p172）**：
> 「**Designers of mainstream products sometimes simply ignored or were ignorant of the needs of those who did not ﬁ t into what was then considered the norm while the market for 'special products' was thought to be too narrow to support innovative design. The result has been stigmatising, poorly-designed and undesirable products that no one would choose freely.**」

> **★★★ 判据（THINK MAINSTREAM）：`Good design can make the impairment irrelevant` —— 好的设计能让损伤"与使用无关"。**
> 这是本技能反复出现的命题的最强措辞：不是"补偿损伤"，是**让损伤变得不相关**。

> **原文（Coleman p172）**：
> 「**Most R&D projects involve some consumers, but older and disabled people tend to be involved at the periphery of any consultation, if they are involved at all. In some cases, small numbers of older or disabled people are added at the end of the consultative process with the result that their input is attenuated and too late to be useful.**」

> **★★★ 判据（START EARLY）：`attenuated and too late to be useful` —— 末期加入的用户，其输入已被削弱且太晚。**
> 与第 6 章"晚期评估必产补丁"互补：一个讲人加得太晚，一个讲评估做太晚。

> **原文（Coleman p172）**：
> 「**There is compelling evidence to show that the conventional method of carrying out consumer research needs to be inverted. Development work should concentrate on those who have the most difﬁ culty rather than on the able- bodied majority. Designing for disabled people makes products easier to use for everybody else.**」

> **★★★★ 判据（LOOK AT HARD CASES）：`the conventional method ... needs to be inverted` —— 研究顺序应当反转：先研究最困难的人。**
> 这是全书最锋利的方法论主张之一，且给了因果：`Designing for disabled people makes products easier to use for everybody else.`
> **与第 2 章"设计基准人应是较矮的老年女性"是同一动作的不同表述。**

> **原文（Coleman p172）**：
> 「**When planning consultation, managing the sessions and interpreting the information, common sense is likely to be your best guide. Many research techniques have evolved a jargon all of their own where esoteric-sounding techniques are merely familiar processes newly wrapped. Do not use any technique unless you can explain it in plain English.**」

> **★★★ 判据（USE COMMON SENSE）：`Do not use any technique unless you can explain it in plain English.` —— 说不清就别用。**

### 9.6.2 十项常见设计缺陷（可与第 6 章六条合并使用）

> **原文（Coleman p173）**：
> 「**Other common design failures revealed by our research include: Small print on labels – often pale grey on white • Instructions in small print with poor colour contrast • Poorly lit, small read out panels • Complicated menu systems • Rotary knobs with smooth sides • Small controls which require high dexterity to grip • Controls which require too much force to operate • Lack of tactile feedback, which mean controls cannot be used by feel • Multi-function controls on electronic devices that confuse the user • Tasks which require the use of two hands**」

| # | 缺陷 | 对应能力轴 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 标签小字（常为浅灰配白） | 视觉 |
| 2 | 说明书小字 + 对比差 | 视觉 |
| 3 | 读数面板照明差、尺寸小 | 视觉 |
| 4 | 菜单系统复杂 | 认知 |
| 5 | 光滑侧面的旋钮 | 灵巧 |
| 6 | 需要高灵巧度才能抓的小控件 | 灵巧 |
| 7 | 需要过大力气的控件 | 力量 |
| 8 | 缺乏触觉反馈（无法凭手感操作） | 触觉/视觉替代 |
| 9 | 电子设备的多功能控件令人困惑 | 认知 |
| 10 | **需要双手完成的任务** | 机动 |

> **★★★★ 判据：这十项可以直接当"评审清单"用，且第 10 项（双手）在多个章节反复出现 —— 是原书最稳定的红旗。**

### 9.6.3 招募：无标准、需冗余

> **原文（Coleman p175）**：
> 「**There are particular problems associated with carrying out assessments which will be useful for people with disabilities. The ﬁ rst and most obvious one is that there is no standard for disability which can be used as a benchmark. The severity of any particular impairment varies considerably, affects people in different ways, and may change from day to day. What individuals are able to do also depends on motivation and to some extent on their determination and ingenuity.**」

> **原文（Coleman p175）**：
> 「**If conclusions are to be drawn about people with particular impairments, it will be necessary to consult a sufﬁ cient number of them to understand their diversity. As an example, for comparative tests Ricability aim to recruit a minimum of twelve people with a single characteristic we wish to treat separately.**」

> **原文（Coleman p175）**：
> 「**It follows that at least 50 people need to be recruited for each test, a number that increases substantially if the number of products to be tested results in each user being only able to assess a proportion of them.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：两个样本量门槛 —— 每类特征 ≥12 人；每次测试 ≥50 人。**
> 组成：12 人 × 若干类（强度、灵巧、认知、视觉、听觉、行动）+ 混合与虚弱者 → 合计 ≥50。
> **注意这是**研究级**样本量；第 6 章明确指出设计实践可以从小样本开始（Coleman p476 / Clarkson p476）。两者不冲突：目的不同。**

> **原文（Coleman p176）**：
> 「**Since techniques of random selection used by statisticians are likely to be unavailable, it will be necessary to use more informal methods. Here one should be aware of possible sources of bias. These include: avoid people you know, including colleagues. Your relationship to • them, and their knowledge of your work may serious distort your ﬁ ndings try to get people from a multiplicity of sources and contexts. If • you recruit from a single day centre, for example, you may only be talking to a very narrow group avoid recruiting people who know each other • recruit from as many contrasting geographical areas as is practical. •**」

| # | 偏倚源 | 对策 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 熟人（含同事） | 避开 |
| 2 | 来源单一（如一个日间中心） | 多来源多情境 |
| 3 | 彼此相识 | 避开 |
| 4 | 地域集中 | 跨地理区域 |

> **★★★ 判据：这四条偏倚源对"极端用户法"（第 8 章）是一个重要补充警告 —— 极端用户往往来自少数几个社群，极易互相认识。**

> **原文（Coleman p176）**：
> 「**As a rough guide, recruiting a reasonably diverse group of 25 people may take 5 days work spread over 4 weeks.**」

> **★★★ 判据：招募 25 人的参考工时 = 5 个工作日，摊在 4 周内。**
> 这是全书唯一给出的**招募时间成本**，对项目排期直接可用。

### 9.6.4 六种信息获取技术

> **原文（Coleman p177）**：
> 「**The main techniques, which can be carried out on a small scale, include: focus groups, depth interviews, quantitative surveys, user panels, user tests, observation and expert panels.**」

> **原文（Coleman p177）**：
> 「**These are informal groups of usually 6−8 people. The main advantages of focus groups are speed, the opportunity to bounce opinions between participants, the fact that groups can develop a dynamic that encourages frank and free discussion and general ﬂ exibility (unexpected leads can be followed at will).**」

> **原文（Coleman p178）**：
> 「**Many focus groups routinely include older consumers but their opinions can sometimes be eclipsed by younger more vociferous participants. Given the propensity of older people to blame themselves rather than poor design, their opinions have not always been widely heeded.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：`the propensity of older people to blame themselves rather than poor design` —— 老年人在焦点小组里会被更健谈的年轻人盖过，且倾向于自责。**
> 这是第 6 章"必须告诉用户被测的是设计"的组织版解释。**可执行对策：分组时按年龄/能力分开，不要混组。**

> **原文（Coleman p178）**：
> 「**Premises need to be fully accessible – not only for wheelchair users but also for those with sensory and other impairments. Ask participants about their requirements. For example, you may need to avoid glare (bright light through windows) for participants with some visual impairments while people with hearing impairments may need induction loops.**」

> **★★★ 判据：场地可及性要问参与者本人，而不是按清单猜（`Ask participants about their requirements`）。**

### 9.6.5 伦理与成本

> **原文（Coleman p181）**：
> 「**You also have a duty of care for those you are working with and you cannot ask users to carry out any task that puts them at risk. The element of risk can be hard to evaluate since many people exaggerate the extent to which they can**」

> **原文（Coleman p182）**：
> 「**carry out common tasks with ease. Talk to them, and stress that they should not carry out any task they have doubts about; make sure such warnings are included on questionnaires, instructions and informed consent documents.**」

> **原文（Coleman p182）**：
> 「**Users, particularly those who are older or have disabilities, get tired; so keep the testing sessions to a reasonable length (1½−2 hours) and build in tea and rest breaks.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：单次测试时长 1.5–2 小时，必须含休息 —— 因为老年与残障用户更容易疲劳。**
> 这是一个可直接写进测试方案的数字。

> **原文（Coleman p182）**：
> 「**If you are taking photographs, get written permission to use these photographs in all likely situations – to illustrate speeches, in publications, annual reports, and so on.**」

> **原文（Coleman p182）**：
> 「**Assuming focus group members are recruited from scratch, the moderator is paid and direct costs include fees to respondents, the hire of a venue, refreshments and transport, audio and/or visual recording, the cost may be between £3,000 and £4,500 depending on whether you use a specialist research agency / researcher to do everything or you are using your own facilities. Professional interviewers cost some £150−175 per day, plus travel and other necessary expenses.**」

| 项 | 成本（2007 年英镑） |
|---|---|
| **一场焦点小组（全含）** | **£3,000–4,500** |
| **专业访员** | **£150–175/天** + 差旅 |
| 参与者酬金 | 约 £25（含交通） |
| 专业主持 | 约 £500/天（直聘） |

> **★★★★ 判据：这是全书唯一的成本数据，可用于给项目做预算论据。**
> 按当时汇率粗算，一场焦点小组约 4–6 万元人民币。**这个数量级足以支撑"要不要做"的管理决策。**

## 9.7 观察法：Clarkson ch24 的补充

> **原文（Clarkson p413–p431 章节主题）**：`Observation for innovation`（观察为了创新）。

> **原文（Coleman p179）**：
> 「**Observation is a useful tool used in conjunction with user tests. Some users are accustomed to experiencing difﬁ culty using products that they will nevertheless describe as being easy to use because they present no more difﬁ culty than other products in their experience. Observation may reveal that in fact they have had to adopt quite awkward strategies to use the product.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：`accustomed to experiencing difﬁculty ... that they will nevertheless describe as being easy to use` —— 用户会把困难描述成"容易"，因为没有比较基准。**
> 这是观察法不可替代的根本理由，也是第 6 章"coping 策略双向误读"的又一证据。
> **可执行判据：只要用户说"挺好用的"，就必须补一次观察 —— 否则无法知道是否只是习惯了。**

> **原文（Coleman p179）**：
> 「**It is better not to combine observations with other duties during a user test.**」

> **★★ 判据：观察不能兼职做 —— 一个人不能既是主持又是观察者。**

### 9.7.1 用户小组（panel）的取舍

> **原文（Coleman p179–p180）**：
> 「**If user tests are to be carried out regularly, recruiting a panel may be cheaper overall as its costs can be spread over several projects. The other advantages are that the panel can be designed to be large enough to provide a choice of** … **participants for later work, which makes future consultation much easier. With panels it is also possible to collect and accrete detailed background data over time which allows for the more sophisticated interpretation of results.**」

> **原文（Coleman p180）**：
> 「**The disadvantages are the time involved in managing the panel and the theoretical possibility that panel members lose their value as typical consumers by adopting a professional critical stance.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：常设用户小组的最大风险是"专业化"—— 成员会变成职业批评者，失去典型性。**
> 这与第 8 章 critical users 形成有趣张力：**我们既要 critical（能说清问题），又要 typical（不代表专业立场）。**
> **对策（本技能据原文推得）：critical 用于概念期（要尖锐），panel 用于验证期（要典型）；不要让同一批人承担两种角色。**

## 9.11 本章一句话可用版

> **方法只有三种动作：问、看、参与。**
> **先定参与等级（七级阶梯），再选方法名。**
> **样本量：每类特征 ≥12 人，每次 ≥50 人（研究级）；设计实践可小样本。**
> **单场测试 1.5–2 小时，含休息。**
> **用户说"挺好用"时，必须补一次观察。**
> **能被删掉的步骤，不要优化它。**
