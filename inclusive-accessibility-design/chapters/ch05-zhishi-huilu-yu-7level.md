## 5.0 总纲：第 3 章说"要量"，本章说"在哪一步量"

第 3、4 章建立了度量体系，但没有回答一个流程问题：**这些度量应该嵌在设计的哪一步？**

本章给两个答案：

| 答案 | 是什么 | 解决什么 |
|---|---|---|
| **① Knowledge Loop（知识回路）** | 信息流模型：从用户 → 数据表征 → 设计者 → 产品 → 回到用户验证 | **组织层面**："信息该以什么形式、在什么时候到达设计者手里" |
| **② 7-Level 设计法** | 把"解决方案开发"这一步拆成 7 个层级，每层带验证 | **项目层面**："在项目内部，按什么顺序做、每层验什么" |

> **★★★★ 判据：本章是全书唯一提供"项目内可执行顺序"的地方。**
> 第 1 章给原则、第 2 章给人、第 3 章给模型、第 4 章给数字 —— 都还停在"知道"；只有本章回答"下周一开工按什么顺序做"。

**本章的三个主干判断：**

> **原文（Clarkson p449）**：
> 「**Designers instinctively design for able-bodied users and are either unaware of the needs of users with different capabilities, or do not know how to accommodate their needs into the design cycle.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p449）**：
> 「**However, the principal concern for a designer should be physical capabilities, irrespective of cause.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p449）**：
> 「**This has led to the common concept of the disabled and elderly as being groups requiring separate attention. Consequently, many design approaches for allowing accessibility by members of either group focus on their disabilities.**」

> **★★★ 判据：「不知道需求」与「不知道怎么把需求装进设计周期」是两个不同的失败，后者才是流程问题。**
> 本书的自我定位不是"提供更多用户知识"（那是第一类失败），而是"提供把知识装进周期的工具"（第二类）。
> 这解释了为什么全书有大量篇幅在讲**信息的形式**（profiles / models / multimedia 'stories' / tables）而不只是内容。

## 5.1 Knowledge Loop：信息怎么流动

> **原文（Clarkson p450）**：
> 「**Recognising that many inclusive (and universal) design approaches are fundamentally derived from user-centred design theory, it is clear that enabling inclusive design requires the successful capture of information about the end-user and representing that information in a form that is accessible for the designer. The designer then needs to have the necessary tools and techniques available to translate the end- user information into a concept, which can then be tested against the end-user needs and wants.**」

**图注还原（图 26.1，Clarkson p451）**「The knowledge loop：Translating user-information into inclusive products and services」：

图中为闭合回路，逆时针依次为：

| 环节 | 图上的标注 |
|---|---|
| ① 认识用户 | `Know the user` |
| ② 捕获并表征终端用户信息 | `capture and represent end-user information` |
| ③ 验证数据 / 评估信息表征的可接受性 | `Verify the data/ assess information representation acceptability` |
| ④ 验证产品 / 评估产品的可接受性 | `Verify the product/ assess product acceptability` |
| ⑤ 使用信息以提供产品/服务 | `use the mformation to provide products/services` |
| ⑥ 获取终端用户信息与可用方法 | `acquire the necessary information about the end-users and the available inclusive design methods` |

- **用户侧（信息源）标注**：`Older users`、`'Disabled'`、`Carers`、`Support staff`
- **信息使用方（information-users）标注**：`OTs`（作业治疗师）、`Designers`、`Managers`、`Prescribers`
- **数据形态示例**：`Profiles`、`Models`、`Multimedia 'stories'`、`Tables`
- **能力轴示例**：`Comms`、`Mobility`
- **编号 1–8**：原书正文用编号 1…8 描述回路的进入点与顺序（见下）

> **原文（Clarkson p451）**：
> 「**The data generated during the user trials needs to be captured '2 and summary representations '3' generated. Only once those summary representations are available can the product be truly validated' 4'. If the product is not successfully verified or validated, then the designer revises the concept product and begins the process again.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：`Only once those summary representations are available can the product be truly validated` —— 没有"表征"就没有验证。**
> 这一条把"数据"和"可用信息"明确分开：
> - 数据 = 原始用户试验记录；
> - 表征 = profile / model / story / table（图 26.1 列的四种形态）。
> **产品含义：做了一堆用户测试但没做成可复用的表征，等于没做 —— 下一轮还得重做。**

> **原文（Clarkson p452）**：
> 「**As a final note, it is important to recognise that the knowledge loop is intended for iterative application, and in practice develops through many cycles of concept development and testing.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p450）**：
> 「**The loop can be entered at any point, but we shall consider it mainly with respect to the information- and end-users.**」

> **★★★ 判据：回路可以从任意点进入 —— 不必从"认识用户"开始。**
> 这解决了一个现实问题：**项目中途接手、或只有样机没有研究预算时，仍可进入回路**。
> 原文给了两条典型入口：有原型时入口是"用户与原型交互得如何"（p452）；有现成数据时入口是"数据验证"（p452）。

> **原文（Clarkson p452）**：
> 「**Depending on the entry point into the knowledge loop, it may be that a prototype product exists, in which case the end-user data that needs to be captured is how well the user interacts with the prototype.**」

### 5.1.1 信息量的黄金区间：太少要重做，太多没人看

> **原文（Clarkson p450）**：
> 「**At present, the data needs of the information-users are being met either by the designers having to obtain the data from first principles for each project, or else by comprehensive approaches where large volumes of data are provided, but most of it is irrelevant for the case in hand.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p450）**：
> 「**The ideal solution for the information-users is to have sufficient data available on hand when it is needed - a kind of data 'just in time'. The need for sufficiency is of prime importance - the data needs to contain all the necessary information, but no more, to avoid information overload.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p461）**：
> 「**However, the provision of too much information is equally ineffective. Industrial designers have no wish to plough through many pages of data to find snippets of information. Presenting them with large volumes of primarily irrelevant data will not encourage the adoption of inclusive**」

> **原文（Clarkson p462）**：
> 「**design practices. Instead, a happy medium is required, where just the right amount of information is provided at just the right time to the right person.**」

（该句跨 p461–p462；此处按页分两段引用，不改字。）

> **★★★★ 判据：信息供给有两个失败端 —— "每个项目从零推导"与"给一大本数据但用不上"。**
> 原书用 `just in time`（准时制）与 `a happy medium` 明确定位了这个区间。
> **这条判据直接适用于今天的设计规范文档/设计系统：** 一份没人查的 200 页规范，与没有规范，效果等同。
> **可执行判据：** 判断一份包容性资料是否合格，看它是否能在"需要的那一刻"被查到（而不是看它是否完整）。

### 5.1.2 回路的目标：从"被动响应"到"响应式设计者"

> **原文（Clarkson p450）**：
> 「**The ultimate purpose of finding successful data representations is to support the concept of a 'responsive designer' who has the necessary understanding of the end-users to accommodate their wants and needs proactively during the design process and obviate the need for retrospective adaptations.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p462）**：
> 「**This would be a big step forward towards the concept of'responsive' designers, no longer designing for their own capabilities and skills, but automatically considering the implications for the wider population of each of their design decisions.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：`responsive designer` 的定义是"在每一个设计决定时自动考虑更广人群的影响"—— 不是"知道有包容性设计这回事"。**
> 这是本章的终点判据。区别在于：知道的人会在评审时补一次检查；响应式的人在第 100 个尺寸决定时就已自动带入。
> **产品含义：** 判断团队是否真正具备了包容性能力，看它**需要不需要额外交付一次"可及性检查"** —— 如果需要，说明还没内化。

### 5.1.3 回路的真实含义：并行而非串行

> **原文（Clarkson p452）**：
> 「**The fundamentally circular nature of the information transfer between end-users' I' and information-users' 5' illustrates how future research activities can be performed in parallel.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p453）**：
> 「**Consequently, it becomes clear that for inclusive design practices to become optimised with respect to the cost of applying them requires a holistic view to be taken of the design process. Defining as much of the knowledge loop as possible in the shortest possible time will keep the overall design process swift and focused.**」

> **★★★ 判据：成本优化的方式是"尽早把回路定义清楚"，而不是"少做几步"。**
> 原文的逻辑：回路越晚闭合，返工越多；把回路尽快定义完，整体流程才快。这与"先做出来再补可及性"的常规做法正好相反。

## 5.2 三个设计阶段与 7-Level 的定位

> **原文（Clarkson p453）**：
> 「**There are 3 principal stages in design (Blessing, 199 5): define the problem develop a solution evaluate the solution**」

> **原文（Clarkson p453）**：
> 「**These stages apply to all design processes, whether designing product interfaces, telephone handsets or design approaches.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p456）**：
> 「**Building on this theory of interaction, a design approach has been developed that expands the second stage of the design process, solution development, into three specified steps (Keates, Clarkson and Robinson, 1999).**」

> **★★★★ 判据：7-Level 不是替代三阶段，而是**只展开第二阶段**（解决方案开发）。**
> 三阶段映射：
> - Stage 1 问题定义 = **Level 1–2**
> - Stage 2 系统定义 = **Level 3–5**
> - Stage 3 系统验证 = **Level 6–7**
> 这个映射是理解 7-Level 的关键：**它不是"七个步骤"，是"两个 + 三个 + 两个"。**

### 5.2.1 两条设计路线：功能驱动 vs 能力驱动

> **原文（Clarkson p454）**：
> 「**The first approach is that the properties of the product, such as its content or functionality, are already defined to meet set functionality objectives. Having these properties defmed directly affects the properties of the interface selected.** … **All of these properties then determine the level of functional capability required for the user to be able to interact successfully with the target product. In other words, the properties of the product are determining which users can access it. This is nearly always the result of the design process unless specific steps are taken at the outset to incorporate usability and accessibility measures into the design process.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p454）**：
> 「**The second design approach is that if the user's functional capabilities are known, then the range of hardware that the user can interact with will be defined as well. This in turn defines the interface elements that are accessible to the user and hence the range of tasks that can be achieved with the product identified. The product then has to be structured so that the required functionality it has to provide is available through a succession of the identified accessible tasks for the users. In this case, the functionality is being driven by the user's capabilities.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p454）**：
> 「**The predominance of these two design approaches has led to the two principal strategies for making interfaces accessible for different user capabilities. The first strategy is to take the existing interface for able-bodied users and then tailor it retrospectively to different users, by adding further functionality to provide assistance or compensation. The second strategy is to change the definition of the user at the very outset of the design process to include a wider range of capabilities. These**」

> **原文（Clarkson p455）**：
> 「**approaches can be described as 'reactive' and 'proactive' respectively (Stephanidis, 2001).**」

（该段跨 p454–p455；此处按页分两段引用，不改字。）

| 路线 | 决定顺序 | 策略名 | 后果 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **① 功能驱动** | 先定功能 → 界面 → 所需能力 → 谁能用 | **reactive（被动响应）** | 事后补适配；常导致"打补丁式"的可及性 |
| **② 能力驱动** | 先定能力 → 可用硬件 → 可用界面元素 → 可达任务 → 功能 | **proactive（主动）** | 一开始就按能力边界组织功能 |

> **★★★★ 判据：`This is nearly always the result of the design process unless specific steps are taken at the outset` —— 功能驱动是默认结果，不是错误选择。**
> 原书用了 `nearly always`。这意味着**如果不刻意设步骤，团队一定会滑向 reactive**。
> **因此 7-Level 的存在意义就是"刻意设的那些步骤"。**

> **原文（Clarkson p454）**：
> 「**The traditional view of designers has been to follow the first approach, to specify the needs of the product and then through the interface place functional demands that the user must be able to meet. User-centred design practices, however, put the emphasis on the user capabilities driving the process in the other direction, as for the second approach.**」

## 5.3 7-Level 逐层展开（本章主体）

> **原文（Clarkson p456）**：
> 「**Each level of the resultant 7 -level design approach, shown in Figure 26.3, is accompanied by user trials throughout and a final evaluation period before progression to the next level, thus providing a framework with clearly defined goals for system usability.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p456）**：
> 「**The 7 -Level approach (Figure 26.3) has been structured as a high-level model, so each level represents an aim, but the method of achieving that aim can vary according to the expertise and knowledge of the designer.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：7-Level 的每一层是"目标（aim）"而不是"动作（step）"—— 达成手段由设计者自选。**
> 这条极重要，因为它决定了 7-Level 不是一套僵化流程，而是一个**验收清单**：每层有一个可判定的目标 + 一个验证动作。

**图注还原（图 26.3，Clarkson p457）「The 7-Level design approach」：**

| Level | 层名（图标注） | 目标（图上第二列） | 验证（图上第三列） | 所属 Stage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | `USER WANTS/ ASPIRATIONS` | `identify the complete problem to be solved` | `validate problem definition` | STAGE 1 PROBLEM DEFINITION |
| **2** | `USER NEEDS` | `specify the functionality to be provided` | `verify functional definition` | STAGE 1 / 2 交界 |
| **3** | `USER PERCEPTION` | `develop a minimal, but sufficient representation of the system status` | `verify user perception` | STAGE 2 SYSTEM DEFINITION |
| **4** | `USER COGNITION` | `structure the interaction to match the user's expectations` | `verify user understanding` | STAGE 2 |
| **5** | `USER MOTOR FUNCTION` | `develop quality of control and user input` | `verify user comfort` | STAGE 2 |
| **6** | `USER PRACTICAL ACCEPTABILITY` | `evaluate system functionality, usability and accessibility` | `validate practical acceptability` | STAGE 3 SYSTEM VALIDATION |
| **7** | `USER SOCIAL ACCEPTABILITY` | `evaluate social acceptability and match to user wants` | `validate social acceptability` | STAGE 3 |

（图上另有 `Problem requirements`、`Functional specification`、`Output to user`、`User mental model`、`Input from user`、`Functional verification`、`Solution validation` 作为各层的产出名；**图不清处以读出的标注为准，不补猜**。）

### 5.3.1 Level 1：用户期望（不只是需求）

> **原文（Clarkson p456）**：
> 「**Level 1 defines the user needs, that is the social motivation for designing the product. This can be identified through softer,**」

> **原文（Clarkson p458）**：
> 「**sociological assessment methods. Questionnaires and interviews are good methods for identifying the user needs.**」

> **★★★ 判据：Level 1 问的是"社会动机"，不是"功能清单"—— 方法必须是软的（问卷、访谈）。**
> 注意图上这层叫 `USER WANTS/ ASPIRATIONS`（期望/抱负），正文叫 `user needs`。**命名本身透露了它的困难：期望比需求更难抓，且更容易被跳过去。**
> **跳过 Level 1 的典型症状：** 需求文档里全是功能条目，没有一句"为什么值得做"。

### 5.3.2 Level 2：功能规格

> **原文（Clarkson p458）**：
> 「**Level 2 focuses on specifying the required utility of the product. Traditional engineering requirements capture techniques (Beitz and Kuttner, 1994) can be used, as can task analysis (Nielsen, 1993). Alternatively, assessments of rival products or user observation can provide insight into the necessary functionality.**」

> **★★ 判据：Level 2 是唯一可以"用传统工程方法"的一层 —— 竞争品分析、任务分析、需求捕获都可直接用。**
> 这也意味着：**如果项目只做了 Level 2，它看起来会完全正常** —— 这是最容易被误判为"已经做了包容性设计"的地方。

### 5.3.3 Level 3–5：交互三段（与 IDC 三轴一一对应）

> **原文（Clarkson p458）**：
> 「**Levels 3 to 5 focus on the stages of interaction. Usability and accessibility techniques can be applied directly to these levels, as can anthropometric and ergonomic data. Prototypes of varying fidelity play a key role in these levels.**」

#### Level 3：感知（输出）

> **原文（Clarkson p458）**：
> 「**Level 3 addresses how the user perceives information from the system. This involves assessing the nature and adjustability of the media used, their appropriateness for the utility, and the physical layout. Anthropometric data are important to ensure that the output is in a position that the user can perceive it. Ergonomic and empirical data from trials are also necessary to ensure that the stimuli are intense enough to be perceived. Ideally, environmental conditions, such as lighting and noise, also need to be identified and modelled.**」

**Level 3 的三个评估维度（原文提取）：**

| 维度 | 问什么 |
|---|---|
| **媒介的性质与可调性** | 视觉/听觉/触觉？可调吗？ |
| **媒介对功能的适配性** | 用声音表达这个信息合适吗？ |
| **物理布局** | 输出在用户能感知到的位置吗？（需人体测量数据） |
| **刺激强度** | 够不够强到能被感知？（需试验数据） |
| **环境条件** | 照明、噪声是否被建模？ |

> **★★★ 判据：Level 3 的目标是图上那句 `develop a minimal, but sufficient representation of the system status` —— 最小但足够。**
> "最小"与"足够"是一对约束：信息太少用户不知道状态，太多则认知过载。这与 Level 4 的"匹配期望"是两个不同的失败点。

#### Level 4：认知（心智模型）

> **原文（Clarkson p458）**：
> 「**Level 4 assesses the matching of the system contents and behaviour to the user mental model. Once the output channels are defined, the functionality (utility) can be added to the system and evaluated because the functionality for monitoring the system is in place. Literally the user can see or hear. the data. Common techniques to map the user system behaviour to user expectations include cognitive walkthroughs.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：Level 4 的前提是 Level 3 已完成 —— `Once the output channels are defined, the functionality (utility) can be added to the system and evaluated because the functionality for monitoring the system is in place`。**
> 顺序不可颠倒：**先能感知状态，才有资格谈"理解得对不对"**。
> **这条对评审员极有用：** 如果用户说"我不知道现在是什么状态"，那是 Level 3 的问题，不是 Level 4 —— 别急着改文案。

> **★★★ 判据：Level 4 的验收动作是 `verify user understanding`，标准技法是 cognitive walkthrough（认知走查）。**
> 这与 `design-psychology-norman` 的"行动七阶段 + 执行/评估鸿沟"是同一套工具的不同切面：诺曼解释机制，Level 4 给出流程位置。

#### Level 5：机动（输入）

> **原文（Clarkson p458）**：
> 「**Level 5 focuses on the user input to the system. As with level 3, this involves assessing the nature and adjustability of the media, their appropriateness for the utility, and the physical layout. Again anthropometric measures are important to ensure that the input media are within the operating range of the user. Ideally, empirical data from user trials needs to be gathered to evaluate the effectiveness of the input solutions. These can be supported by adopting user modelling techniques. Where user trials are impossible, suitably calibrated user models can be used to provide design data.**」

> **★★★ 判据：Level 5 与 Level 3 用同一套三维度（媒介/适配/布局），但方向相反 —— 一个是输出，一个是输入。**
> 原文用 `As with level 3` 明确了这个对称性。**这是 7-Level 结构上最优雅的一处：对称的两层用同一个检查表。**
> 图上 Level 5 的验收是 `verify user comfort`（舒适），不是"能操作"—— 措辞已把门槛定在舒适而非可用。

> **★★★ 判据：无用户试验时可以用"标定过的用户模型"代替 —— 原文允许 `Where user trials are impossible`。**
> 条件是 `suitably calibrated`（经过恰当标定）。这是第 7 章模拟工具的合法性依据。

### 5.3.4 Level 6–7：两类可接受性

> **原文（Clarkson p458）**：
> 「**Level 6 involves the evaluation of the complete system to ensure satisfactory practical acceptability, i.e. utility, usability and accessibility. Formal user trials are essential at this point, before the design can progress to the final level.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p459）**：
> 「**Level 7 assesses the resultant system against the user needs. This mirrors Nielsen's social acceptability requirement. Softer, more qualitative approaches are generally needed, such as surveys, interviews and questionnaires.**」

| Level | 检验什么 | 方法 | 硬性要求 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **6** | **实用可接受性**：效用 + 可用性 + 可及性 | **正式用户试验** | `essential`（必需，不可省） |
| **7** | **社会可接受性**：是否匹配用户期望 | 软性定性：问卷、访谈、调查 | 无硬性要求，但需回扣 Level 1 |

> **★★★★ 判据：Level 6 是全书唯一使用 `essential` 这个词的地方 —— 正式用户试验在这一层不可替代。**
> 对比：Level 3、5 允许用标定模型代替；Level 7 用软方法；**只有 Level 6 无替代方案。**

> **★★★★ 判据：Level 7 必须回到 Level 1 的用户期望 —— 图上写的是 `match to user wants`。**
> 这意味着 Level 7 的检验对象不是"产品好不好"，而是"**产品是否是当初那个动机的答案**"。
> **这条闭合了整个 7-Level：1 层问"为什么做"（期望），7 层问"做到没有"（是否匹配期望）。中间 2–6 是"怎么做"。**

### 5.3.5 7-Level 的循环性与顺序纪律

> **原文（Clarkson p459）**：
> 「**Although the 7-Level approach is presented as a flow diagram, in practice each of the stages can be applied in an iterative manner. Indeed, in many circumstances, it may prove essential to iterate within and between levels. However, the general order of levels should be applied in the order shown in Figure 26.3.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：可以迭代，但**大顺序不可颠倒**。**
> 原文用了两个不同的词：`iterative manner`（可迭代）与 `the general order ... should be applied`（顺序应遵守）。
> **实操含义：** 可以在 Level 4 发现问题后回到 Level 3 补输出；但**不能跳过 Level 3 直接做 Level 4**，也不能先做 Level 6 的测试再补 Level 1 的动机。

> **原文（Clarkson p459）**：
> 「**The underlying strength of the 7 -Level approach is that because it was derived from a model of interaction that was not product-specific. the approach itself is not product-specific either. Consequently,it is applicable to a wide range of products.**」

> **★★★ 判据：7-Level 的普适性来自它的来源 —— 人类信息处理模型，而不是某类产品。**
> 这是它区别于"某产品可及性指南"的根本：指南随产品失效，模型随产品扩展。

### 5.3.6 7-Level 与 IDC 的接口

> **原文（Clarkson p459）**：
> 「**Both the 7 -level approach and the inclusive design cube share the same inherent emphasis on the interaction consisting of perceptual, cognitive and motor actions. Consequently, if the 7 -level approach has been adopted as the framework for the development, then the Inclusive Design Cube (IDC, introduced in Chapter 5), can be adapted to monitor the population coverage achieved by different design choices, as shown in Figure 26.4. Effectively. the 7 -Level approach can be thought of as designing for each axis on the IDC. The modification necessary to use the IDC for this is a straightforward re-labelling of the axes to reflect Levels 3 to 5 of the 7 -Level approach.**」

**图注还原（图 26.4，Clarkson p459）「The inclusive design cube for use with the seven-level approach」** —— 轴标签改为 `User perception` / `cognition`（第三轴 OCR 未完整读出）。

| 7-Level | IDC 轴 |
|---|---|
| Level 3 感知 | **Sensory** |
| Level 4 认知 | **Cognitive** |
| Level 5 机动 | **Motor** |

> **★★★★ 判据：`the 7 -Level approach can be thought of as designing for each axis on the IDC` —— 这是全书两套主方法（IDC 与 7-Level）的正式接口。**
> 用法：
> ① 用 7-Level 组织流程（按顺序做）；
> ② 每个 Level 结束时，把该轴的需求分数填进 IDC；
> ③ IDC 体积变化 = 这一步带来的覆盖增量。
> **这两套东西不是并列选项，是"流程"与"仪表盘"的关系。**

> **原文（Clarkson p461）**：
> 「**So, for example, exclusion arising from a viewing screen being set too high can be included with other sensory exclusions, to give a complete exclusion for the user output level of the analysis.**」

> **★★★ 判据：人体工学类排除（如屏幕装太高）可以直接并入 IDC 的对应轴，不必单列。**
> 这解决了第 4 章"两条桥"合并时的归类问题：**人体工学失配按"它阻碍了哪一段交互"归轴**（屏幕太高 → 感知轴）。

## 5.4 本章自陈的局限（原书自己列的，最诚实的一节）

> **原文（Clarkson p460）**：
> 「**With this in mind, the 7 -Level approach was envisaged as being a framework, rather than a string of rigorously defined steps, and offering designers the freedom to select which design tools they deem most appropriate for each level and for the product being considered. Further work is being carried out on populating the approach with common design tools and usability methods.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：`Further work is being carried out on populating the approach` —— 2003 年时 7-Level 是空框架，还没有配套工具。**
> 这是本章最大的空白，必须明写：**7-Level 给的是"每层验什么"，不给"每层用什么工具"**。

> **原文（Clarkson p460）**：
> 「**However, there are potential difficulties in populating the cube. Populating the IDC for a specific product requires the designer to be able to assess the functional demands of the product and then to map those back to the target population. The designer has to identify the key actions involved in using the product, then establish the levels of sensory, cognitive and motor capabilities required to perform those actions. Those capability requirements then have to be mapped to proportions of the target population. However, unlike anthropometric data, there are comparatively few complete population data sets that describe the prevalence of different level of capability in the general population.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p461）**：
> 「**There is a further potential source of difficulty. It has been assumed that the presence of one impairment in a user is independent of the presence of other impairments. It has been further assumed that there is no correlation between anthropometric and capability data. However, this has yet to be verified.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：IDC 的两个建模假设**未经证实**：① 各项损伤相互独立；② 人体测量数据与能力数据不相关。**
> 原书原文：`However, this has yet to be verified`。
> **但第 2 章的实测数据（机动×感官耦合 50%）已经直接否定了假设 ①。** 这是全书内部一处未消解的张力，必须明写。

> **原文（Clarkson p462）**：
> 「**While the underlying principles of the above are available within the research community, they are not making the transfer into industrial practice. Similarly, the research community has proposed and developed a number of tools, techniques, methods and philosophies for the design of more inclusive products and services. However, industry uptake is slow and has often only happened because of the threat of litigation under punitive legislation.**」

> **原文（Clarkson p462）**：
> 「**Given the sound economic arguments in favour of more inclusive products, that companies are still dragging their collective feet to adopt such practices implies that industry perceives that there are fundamental problems with the proposed inclusive design processes. The most common such perceived problem is that of increased cost and time to develop products and services through the addition of more requirements and constraints into the design process.**」

> **★★★★ 判据：业界不采纳的头号原因不是"不认同"，是"认为会增加成本与时间"。**
> 原书对此的回应不是否认成本，而是：**把回路尽快定义完，整体反而更快**（§5.1.3）。
> **这一条对说服商业方最有用：不要论证"应该做"，要论证"早做比晚做便宜"。**

## 5.8 本章一句话可用版

> **按 7 层顺序做，每层只验一件事：**
> **L1 为什么做（期望）→ L2 做什么（功能）→ L3 看得见吗（感知）→ L4 想得通吗（认知）→ L5 操作得了吗（机动）→ L6 整体能用吗（正式试验，不可省）→ L7 是不是当初要的（回到 L1）。**
> **可以回退补做，不可跳级。**
